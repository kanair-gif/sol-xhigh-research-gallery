Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Define perturbational wavefront identity, arrival fields, moving causal sections, metastable lifetimes, and front lineages without assuming Euclidean space, a unique neural grain, or phase estimates extracted from passive activity alone.
  • Prove the Moving-Section Separation Conjecture using a finite recurrent front model and a history-matched phase-scrambled replay, and establish invariance under node relabeling, common time translation, and causally faithful state-coordinate changes.
  • Derive when front-aligned rank reduces to registered-cut mediated-control rank, when fixed cuts underestimate distributed coavailability, and why RLQO content reserve or mode transport can remain unchanged after temporal binding is destroyed.
  • Apply the resulting inference ladder analytically to feedforward transformer passes, clocked recurrent agents, global-workspace-inspired architectures, spatial recurrent networks, neural fields, and neuromorphic systems, while withholding claims where internal intervention semantics are unavailable.
  • Separate the mechanistic claim that a wavefront binds temporal inputs for control from additional claims about report generation, robust agency, observer attribution, phenomenal experience, welfare, and moral status.
  • State explicit positivity, intervention-feasibility, causal-sufficiency, temporal-resolution, and route-isolation assumptions; when a physical phase shear cannot preserve local marginals or isolate propagation routes, report only partial identification rather than a binding score.
  • Require the arrival field to be recovered from localized perturbation responses and propagation constraints, not selected post hoc to maximize coherence or behavioral prediction, and test sensitivity to alternative graph grains and temporal parameterizations.
  • Use adversarial nulls that preserve local autocorrelation, power spectra, activation distributions, metastable dwell times, passive transcripts, behavioral accuracy, and relevant Hankel ranks so that only cross-site causal phase organization differs.
  • Audit mode and wave estimates for sampling-range, resolution, spectral-gap, nonstationarity, and over-sampling failures highlighted by dynamic-mode-decomposition methodology, with convergence checks performed mode by mode.
  • Label empirical evidence, mathematical consequence, mechanistic inference, phenomenal bridge premise, and ethical precaution separately; no wavefront statistic will be presented as a direct measure of experience.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • A standing oscillation or global synchrony episode may create high coherence and broad availability without a propagating arrival field or phase-dependent temporal registration.
  • A clocked delay line, cached replay, or externally scheduled pipeline may reproduce passive propagation, reports, and decisions while remaining insensitive to interventions on the putative front.
  • Temporal binding may be implemented without metastable wavefronts through attractor completion, dendritic coincidence, synaptic eligibility traces, cross-frequency coupling, reservoir state, or a global workspace with explicit timestamps, making wavefront binding non-necessary.
  • Metastable traveling waves may organize unconscious sensory, motor, sleep, or autonomic processing, making the operator non-sufficient for phenomenal consciousness even if it is causally important for temporal access.
  • A programmable delay network that passes every route-isolated shear and compensation test is causally equivalent to a wavefront operator at the selected grain; in that boundary case the calculus identifies a temporal-registration equivalence class, not a uniquely biological physical mechanism.
  • The formal conjecture is false if, under its stated finite-state and intervention assumptions, fixed-cut mediated-control channels together with finite-horizon joint and behavioral predictive invariants mathematically determine the front-aligned channel and its shear response, making the proposed front-versus-replay construction impossible.
  • The operational criterion loses specificity if a route-excluded replay, standing-wave, or observer-imposed phase model can satisfy front-aligned rank, positive shear efficacy, and compensated-shear covariance under the same intervention graph while no causal path traverses the candidate wavefront.
  • For any claimed biological or artificial instance, the wavefront-binding interpretation is rejected if validated differential shears of propagation leave joint downstream control unchanged, or if matched shifts of local event timing fail to restore control, after front existence, intervention selectivity, local-marginal preservation, and statistical power have been established.
