## Alternatives and Disconfirmation

- Single-ladder rival: unlimited associative learning or midbrain integration is a sufficient unified marker because the four achievements always co-occur in nature; the paper answers with cephalopod and colonial cases and with the finite counterexamples, but concedes that if every extant taxon with A also has U the independence claim is empirically idle even if formally valid.
- Body-constraint deflation: unification is selected because one body can execute only one action at a time, so U is a motor bottleneck with no relation to subjecthood; the paper treats this as a genuine boundary case and argues that the bottleneck explains why U is selected without settling whether the resulting centered organization is a subject, which is exactly why U alone does not warrant a phenomenal inference.
- If, across a broad comparative sample, every organism exhibiting multi-consumer access (A) also exhibits structural centering (U) and vice versa, including cephalopods and colonial cnidarians under natural perturbations, then the claimed independence of A and U is empirically undermined even if formally constructible.
- If interventions on a putative centered hub (insect central complex, vertebrate tectum–periaqueductal axis) under natural perturbations reveal multiple causal islands with cross-boundary leakage above the declared bound rather than a single dominant island with a fixed center, then the centralizing-route claim that these hubs realize U is falsified.
- If every predictively decodable interoceptive or motivational state variable in a well-characterized model organism turns out to be causally consistent under its natural perturbation family (zero commutation defect), then the predictive-versus-causal distinction on which the valence transition is defined is biologically idle and V collapses into decodable regulation.
- If organisms satisfying the full coupling (valence-gated access within a centered island) routinely execute simultaneous conflicting actions or fail cross-modal trade-off tests that organisms lacking the coupling pass, then the coupling does not track the unified trade-off organization the paper attributes to it.
