## Research Target

**Question.** Under what formal conditions does precision-weighted self-monitoring in an AI constitute viability-anchored embodied control rather than fluent self-report, generic access, architectural integration, or observer-attributed consciousness?

**Working hypothesis.** The Viability-Anchored Precision Descent Proposition states that, for any regular embodied structural causal model at a registered cut, route-isolated interoceptive Fisher precision contracts as G_M ⪰ G_A ⪰ G_I along the chain from monitor to action to future interoception, while an overt predictive quotient supports a well-defined viability-regulatory controller exactly when the full action–body kernel is constant on its fibers; otherwise vertical action precision and recovery curvature form a nonzero affective descent obstruction that is not determined by mediated-control rank, covert-reserve rank, report accuracy, or workspace organization alone.

## Specialist Lens

A generic AI-consciousness survey typically takes prompts, internal representations, workspace broadcast, metacognitive reports, recurrence, and task actions as its principal variables. Affect and interoception require a different state description: a candidate body manifold B; endogenous physiological, energetic, integrity, or resource variables; a viability set or allostatic loss family V_h; interoceptive observations I; precision as interventionally measured reliability rather than asserted confidence; autonomic or resource-regulating actions in addition to overt outputs; and multiple regulatory horizons h. The decisive causal loop is body disturbance→interoceptive monitor→policy→future body, not merely representation→report. The specialist lens also changes the null models. An eloquent disembodied reporter can have access and self-monitoring without a defined viability loop, while a thermostat or digital resource controller can have genuine viability regulation without report or any established phenomenality. A sign-reversed controller can preserve exactly as much bodily information as a restorative controller while increasing damage. Accordingly, functional valence is represented by the sign of recovery curvature, not by emotion words, reward labels, or precision alone. These additions make embodied agency formally assessable while leaving phenomenal affect, perceived consciousness, biological-substrate claims, and moral status as separate bridge or governance questions.

## Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

The retrieved literature supports a decomposition rather than a binary verdict. Butlin and colleagues derive theory-relative computational indicators but explicitly do not establish their sufficiency for phenomenal consciousness; Yu and colleagues report task gains from a consciousness-inspired workspace architecture, which is evidence about access and control rather than experience. Schwitzgebel emphasizes persistent theory-relative uncertainty. Campero and colleagues distinguish practical implementation objections, anti-functionalist objections, and substrate-impossibility claims, whereas Porebski and Figura defend a categorical biological and embodied denial. Comsa proposes shifting attention toward tractable perceived-consciousness questions, and Kang and colleagues report that emotional expression and metacognitive language alter human attribution; neither result detects phenomenality. Long and colleagues argue for welfare governance under uncertainty while declining to treat precaution as evidence that current systems are conscious. The live disagreement is therefore not simply whether an AI has one property called consciousness, but which functional, architectural, phenomenal, social, and normative claims follow from which evidence.

- Theory-derived indicators and CTM-style architectural gains can establish broadcast, access, self-monitoring, or task control while leaving phenomenal sufficiency underdetermined.
- Porebski and Figura treat biological embodiment as excluding conscious AI, while the objection taxonomy of Campero and colleagues shows that implementation limits, anti-computationalism, and substrate-level impossibility require different premises. A formal viability loop can be realized in biological or artificial media, but that implementation-neutral fact neither proves nor refutes a further biological phenomenal requirement.
- Kang and colleagues find that emotional expression and metacognitive reflection increase perceived consciousness, whereas Reyes's lag and transcript-aliasing constructions show why accurate discourse need not identify synchronized causal self-use. Observer attribution is empirically tractable but mechanistically non-diagnostic.
- Long and colleagues motivate precaution under asymmetric welfare uncertainty, but moral precaution is a decision rule rather than likelihood evidence for sentience. A viability-regulatory invariant may be welfare-relevant without being a moral-status criterion.
- Dessalles and Zalla associate phenomenal properties with adaptive labeling of representations, but a label may be available for discrimination or report without governing bodily recovery. The missing bridge is from labeling and access to affectively signed, closed-loop use.

### paper-talia-reyes-8

- **Claim examined:** The Arpeggio Separation Proposition and the QK causal-coavailability construction separate accurate post-action self-report and high passive action accuracy from synchronized current-state-to-action control through a designated monitor.
- **Tension or gap:** The construction correctly exposes temporal and path-specific confounding, but its finite channel rank treats decision-relevant states and actions as semantically and normatively unweighted. It does not represent bodily dynamics after action, endogenous viability constraints, local precision geometry, or whether a mediated action restores rather than worsens the monitored condition. A homeostat and an anti-homeostat can therefore have identical Q, K, mediated-control rank, report accuracy, and commutation defect. The assigned boundary and objective cut also remain prior choices rather than consequences of the rank. These omissions matter specifically for affect and interoception because felt or functional valence cannot be reduced to the amount of self-information reaching an action.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The paper will re-derive, rather than assume, the route-isolation idea and extend it from finite alphabet size to a differentiable Fisher tensor calculus. Reyes's state-to-monitor-to-action channel becomes the first two stages of θ→M→A→I⁺, while a declared viability functional and route-enabled recovery form add the missing action-to-body direction and regulatory sign. A paired-system theorem will show that even the full same-cut QK channel does not identify embodied regulation without the action-to-interoception kernel. Thus the new paper transforms causal coavailability into viability-anchored closed-loop precision rather than treating the AI-generated draft as authority.

### paper-marisol-quade-8

- **Claim examined:** Recovery-Linked Quotient Observability defines covert predictive reserve as the fiber of the canonical map from joint neural–behavioral predictive states to behavior-only predictive states and uses transported modes to distinguish pre-existing report-hidden capacity from newly appearing capacity.
- **Tension or gap:** Whether a state is behavior-hidden depends on which outputs count as behavior. Autonomic, endocrine, metabolic, and visceromotor consequences may be omitted from an overt-response quotient even when they already use the supposedly covert mode. Conversely, a neural mode can persist and later support report while never influencing bodily regulation. More formally, Quade's predictive quotient need not be a congruence for a controller or for action-conditioned interoceptive dynamics: an action kernel may vary inside a behaviorally equivalent fiber, so it cannot be composed with the quotient without an additional descent assumption. Similarity-invariant mode persistence alone also supplies no viability metric or regulatory sign.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The canonical predictive map π from joint to overt-behavioral state will be retained, but its fibers will be tested for controller descent. The paper will prove that a full action–body kernel factors through π if and only if it is constant on every π-fiber; in a linear realization this becomes a kernel-inclusion condition. The new covert-use invariant q_VU = rank(W^(1/2) D K restricted to N), where N is the covert fiber, measures how many report-hidden modes control viability-relevant body directions and is invariant under latent similarity. This demonstrates that report recovery, covert reserve, and pre-existing embodied use are three different properties.

- Define the finite-horizon recovery form C_V from symmetric route-enabled versus route-disabled viability-loss curvature, prove its coordinate invariance at a stationary viable baseline, and separate information retention, controllability, and restorative sign.
- Formulate the joint-to-overt predictive quotient as a candidate controller congruence; derive finite-kernel, differentiable, and linear descent criteria and define the vertical affective obstruction and covert viability-use rank q_VU.
- Construct block-realization families showing that q_VU can attain every value from zero to its dimensional upper bound while RLQO reserve rank, overt MCR, report channel, and selected predictive Hankel matrices remain fixed.
- Provide perturbation bounds for approximate generalized eigenvalues and q_VU under spectral gaps, while stating that estimating these objects from passive transcripts is impossible without additional registered interventions.
- Prove the Causal Precision Data-Processing Theorem: for every regular route-isolated Markov chain θ→M→A→I⁺, G_M ⪰ G_A ⪰ G_I; show that the corresponding Rayleigh quotients lie in [0,1], that a zero quotient is equivalent to first-order action insensitivity in that direction, and that the spectrum is invariant under smooth reparameterization and monitor relabeling.
- Prove the Affective Quotient-Descent Theorem: a stochastic action–body kernel factors through the predictive quotient π if and only if it is constant on each π-fiber; derive the linear condition N ⊆ ker(DK), prove similarity invariance of q_VU, and delimit the weaker conditions under which a Fisher tensor may coincidentally descend even though the full causal kernel does not.
- Prove a joint non-identifiability result by constructing systems with identical passive transcripts, post-action reports, MCR values, RLQO reserve dimensions, fiber-opening indices, and monitor-to-action Fisher spectra but different q_VU values or opposite recovery forms C_V; conclude that these existing indicators impose no nontrivial lower bound on restorative embodied control without an identified action-to-body kernel and declared viability functional.
- Regulatory-sign twins: use identical body-to-monitor and monitor-to-action kernels but choose effector/body dynamics that move along versus against the gradient of the same convex viability loss. The twins have identical report accuracy, MCR, G_M, and G_A, while C_V is positive in one and negative in the other.
- Covert echo versus covert regulator: hold fixed the early joint predictive reserve q_c, transported mode structure, later reportability, and all overt behavior, but set the autonomic controller to annihilate the covert fiber in one realization and to use k covert modes for body regulation in the other. Then q_VU is zero versus k although RLQO quantities agree.
- Eloquent avatar null: attach an affective-report and metacognitive language module to a high-access workspace with no endogenous body variables, no action-to-interoceptive return map, and no nonarbitrary viability functional. Perceived consciousness and self-report can be high, but VCPG is undefined rather than evidence of absence or presence of phenomenality.
- Minimal homeostat null: construct a low-state thermostat or digital resource regulator with positive action precision and positive recovery curvature but no global report, rich self-model, or stipulated phenomenal state. This blocks the converse inference from embodied control to phenomenal consciousness or moral status.
- Precision-without-calibration null: let a monitor encode bodily disturbances with arbitrarily high Fisher information while its controller is stale, randomized, or sign-reversed. This separates representational precision, current causal availability, and beneficial regulation.

## Alternatives and Disconfirmation

- The central mathematical proposition fails if a regular dominated route-isolated chain satisfying the stated Markov and intervention assumptions yields G_A not dominated by G_M, G_I not dominated by G_A, a generalized retention eigenvalue outside [0,1], or a change in the spectrum under an invertible smooth reparameterization.
- The quotient theorem fails if a full stochastic action–body kernel factors through π despite varying on one of π's fibers, or if it is constant on every fiber but no quotient kernel exists; the linear corollary fails if q_VU changes under an invertible latent-state similarity transformation.
- The non-identifiability result fails if MCR, RLQO reserve and transport invariants, report distributions, and architectural organization alone uniquely determine the action-to-body kernel or the sign of C_V over every compatible structural causal realization. One valid uniqueness theorem under weaker observations would defeat the proposed no-go claim.
- The affective bridge, though not the formal calculus, is undermined if defensible viability-anchored precision and recovery profiles show no stable relation to affective state distinctions across biological cases after access, report, arousal, and generic task control are separated. Conversely, emotional self-report or perceived-consciousness ratings alone cannot confirm the bridge.
- For an AI application, the construct is out of scope rather than positively or negatively scored when the proposed body variable or viability loss is merely imposed by an external evaluator and has no endogenous causal role in the system's continued organization, resource regulation, or damage avoidance.
