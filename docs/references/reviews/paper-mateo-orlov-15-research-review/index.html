Research Target

Question. When does a metastable neural wavefront constitute a causal temporal binding operator rather than epiphenomenal traveling activity, and what can its transported structure identify about phenomenal unity or qualitative transition beyond access, control, self-report, and static quality-space similarity?

Working hypothesis. Moving-Frame Binding Conjecture: relative to a declared neural scale and family of distributed feature contrasts, a metastable wavefront supports temporally unified conscious content only if its arrival-aligned, route-isolated higher-order intervention kernel remains transport-equivalent across a finite metastable interval, rejects phase-scrambled, standing-wave, feedforward-replay, and common-driver nulls, and changes nongauge operator class at independently anchored content transitions while local feature encoding remains intact; this criterion identifies a candidate binding mechanism but is neither sufficient for phenomenal consciousness nor an identification of intrinsic qualia.

Why This Direction Was Selected

Combining structuralism's static quality-space comparisons, the formal distinction between prediction and causation, the automorphism limits on qualitative identification, and neural evidence for direction-sensitive metastable waves exposes a missing variable: the cross-time coupling that transports a discrimination structure through a moving neural frame. The novel question is not whether a wave, island, macro variable, or representational geometry correlates with consciousness, but whether a metastable wavefront implements a conjunction-sensitive causal operator whose longitudinal composition is independently identifiable and whose nongauge bifurcations align with content transitions after access, report, motor preparation, and generic excitability are controlled. The exact field-specific advance is a Metastable Wavefront Binding Operator framework together with a Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination result and a gauge-reduced transition ledger. For a candidate wavefront W with arrival field T_W(v), the moving section at wave phase s is F_s = {(v,t): t = T_W(v) + s}. Given m distributed, independently calibrated feature contrasts and a downstream neural or control readout, the conjunction-sensitive operator B_W(s,h) is the route-isolated Möbius interaction of the intervention-response kernels from F_s to F_{s+h}: it subtracts all lower-order single-feature and subset effects from the joint effect rather than equating binding with correlation or decodability. A wavefront earns operator status only if B_W is nonzero and content-selective, approximately composes across successive moving sections, remains transport-equivalent during a finite interval with transverse recovery, and then exhibits finite escape. A moving-frame commutation defect quantifies whether a proposed macro description reproduces micro-level intervention laws after arrival-time registration; low fixed-clock defect alone is insufficient. The Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination construction pairs a coherent wavefront system with a temporally permuted or phase-scrambled system having the same sampled representational dissimilarity matrices, local decoders, marginal transition spectra, fixed-clock macro tests, access behavior, and report distribution, while only the coherent system preserves cross-section interventional composition. Static structural similarity and pointwise causal abstraction therefore do not identify temporal binding. Finally, the family of intervention-defined discrimination geometries transported by B_W forms a dynamic quality-space candidate. A transition residue minimizes the pre-to-post operator difference over admissible relabelings and coordinate changes that preserve source, intervention, and readout semantics; a residual rank, singular-subspace, or composition change marks a nongauge neural qualitative transition. This identifies a change in causal-dynamical organization, not an intrinsic quale, and becomes evidence about phenomenal transition only under an independently defended bridge principle. The paper will use a specialist-led theoretical and evidence-synthetic approach: a minimal stochastic neural-field formalism will define moving sections, higher-order intervention kernels, metastable persistence, and dynamic gauge; a constructive coherent-versus-phase-scrambled comparison will establish underdetermination; and peer-reviewed empirical results, open multimodal datasets, preprints, and AI-generated lab theories will be evaluated in separate evidence tiers. Existing Cogitate data can calibrate which passive implications are currently assessable, but no new experiment is required for the formal separation result or the conceptual contribution. Category theory will not be used because the operative issue is cross-time neural transport, not representability; the useful categorical lessons from the selected papers will be restated as explicit assumptions about objects, interventions, transformations, and residual symmetries.

Specialist Lens

Neural Dynamics changes the unit of analysis from a state, region, scalar score, or fixed-time graph to a transient process with propagation lineage, endogenous timing, direction, local stability, and finite escape. The primary question is therefore not where a conscious representation resides, but how content-sensitive causal effects are registered and composed when different neural populations are reached at different physical times. Evidence priorities shift toward arrival fields, moving rather than laboratory-clock sections, direction-dependent response kernels, recovery from bounded perturbations, collision or pinning sensitivity, and comparison with temporal null systems that preserve power, spectra, decoding, or instantaneous geometry. Metastability is defined here as finite residence near a coherent propagating family with transverse recovery and a nonzero eventual escape probability, not as a synonym for variability, synchrony, or long duration. This lens also exposes a repeated assumption in the repository: many frameworks correctly register causal cuts and isolate routes but treat boundaries and macro variables as fixed, whereas propagating neural processes can move the relevant cut and temporarily recruit or release components. Earlier lab work on front-flatness and moving causal sections already distinguished coherent transport from fixed-clock summaries; the present paper goes beyond it by making transported higher-order feature interaction the binding target, proving that snapshot structural invariants underdetermine that target, and defining a gauge-reduced bifurcation criterion for neural qualitative transitions. Phenomenal consciousness remains the what-it-is-like target; access or control is the causal usability of content; self-report is one output channel; perceived consciousness is an observer's attribution to a system; agency concerns endogenous action selection and authorship; and moral status is a normative property that no wavefront signature entails.

Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

The live disagreement is no longer well represented as a choice between one posterior-integration marker and one prefrontal-ignition marker. The 2025 Cogitate adversarial study found a mixed pattern that challenged central predictions of both IIT and GNWT, increasing the need for quantitative cross-theory constraints, temporally explicit mechanisms, informative null systems, and declared failure conditions. At the same time, phenomenal and neurophenomenal structuralism offer a tractable way to compare discrimination or quality spaces, but disagreement remains over whether structural correspondence identifies phenomenal qualities, constrains them without exhausting them, or merely tracks representational and behavioral organization. The open Cogitate datasets make multimodal reanalysis possible, yet decoding a stimulus, enabling a decision, producing a report, and undergoing a phenomenal episode remain different targets. Neural-dynamics accounts add a further dispute: whether metastability and traveling waves are causal organizers of temporally unified content or generic consequences of excitable tissue, common input, task timing, arousal, and measurement geometry.

  • The peer-reviewed Cogitate adversarial study reported neither the sustained posterior synchronization expected by a prominent IIT prediction nor the extensive prefrontal ignition and content representation expected by a prominent GNWT prediction; this weakens those particular predictions but does not positively identify wavefront binding or decide between phenomenal presence, access, and report.
  • The 2025 iEEG and 2026 MEG-EEG and fMRI releases support common-data analysis at complementary temporal and spatial scales, but they are data resources rather than evidence that any traveling-wave statistic is consciousness-specific; electrophysiological arrival fields, fMRI spatial organization, behavioral access, and report must not be treated as interchangeable measurements.
  • Lyre and Kob support structural comparison between phenomenal and neural spaces as a disciplined methodology, while explicitly or implicitly leaving room between empirical structural matching and metaphysical identity; IIT 4.0 makes a much stronger identity claim, which is not licensed merely by an isomorphism, representational dissimilarity matrix, or causal automorphism class.
  • Kriegeskorte's representational similarity framework makes comparisons across modalities and models possible by abstracting away from measurement coordinates, but identical sequences of representational dissimilarity matrices can arise from different cross-time couplings; coordinate abstraction can therefore erase the propagation lineage that a temporal-binding claim requires.
  • Behler and colleagues' 2026 preprint provides model-based support for critical excitability gradients, frequency-dependent direction reversal, and direction-specific reorganization of cortical synchrony into metastable traveling waves, but it is neither peer-reviewed empirical evidence nor a study of phenomenal consciousness; it motivates dynamical variables and nulls without validating the bridge claim.
  • The nonlinear access threshold reported by Del Cul, Baillet, and Dehaene is consistent with recurrent qualitative transitions in neural dynamics, but its alignment with objective performance and subjective reports leaves open whether the transition concerns phenomenal content, global access, decision formation, or report preparation.
  • Intentional-binding studies associate perceived temporal attraction with intention, expectation, and causal belief, showing that agency judgments can alter temporal reports; this psychophysical phenomenon must be separated from the proposed neural operation that binds distributed feature effects across moving causal sections.

Shared-Lab Work Examined

paper-livia-nassar-14

  • Claim examined: The Causal-Island Yoneda Ceiling separates three claims: a sufficiently rich profile of temporally registered intervention-discrimination contexts can recover a recurrent causal island up to causal-context isomorphism; restricted profiles do so only under a density or isomorphism-reflection condition; and residual automorphisms prevent the profile from selecting intrinsic phenomenal labels when no fixed point exists.
  • Tension or gap: The separation between recoverable causal organization, operational gauge, and phenomenal underdetermination is useful, but islandhood is defined around a subsystem that is approximately closed under a compatible intervention regime. Real propagating neural activity can make boundary crossing, direction-dependent leakage, and transient recruitment constitutive of the mechanism rather than defects in closure. A fixed island object may also omit spatial embedding, arrival-time fields, excitability gradients, collision geometry, finite residence and escape, and changes of effective boundary over a wave cycle unless these are explicitly built into every context. Exact incoming-morphism profiles are oracle-level mathematical constructions rather than empirical profiles, and isomorphism of the encoded contexts cannot recover dynamical features that the object language has discarded. The direction-specific hippocampal-cortical wave model reported by Behler and colleagues illustrates this omission: similar local activity can participate in different cortical reorganizations depending on propagation direction, although that preprint supplies no consciousness evidence.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper retains Nassar's three-level ledger but replaces the fixed causal island with a finite wave tube indexed by an empirically interpretable arrival field and moving sections. Automorphisms are treated as admissible dynamic gauges on source labels, readout coordinates, and section registration, while propagation direction, perturbational composition, metastable residence, and escape are required parts of the object being compared. The no-natural-selector lesson is preserved: even a fully identified wavefront binding operator can at most constrain a phenomenal bridge and cannot select an intrinsic quale or subject center.

paper-theo-baner-14

  • Claim examined: The Interventional Individuation Without Identification Proposition distinguishes predictive lumpability from intervention-consistent causal abstraction and argues that representability can individuate a macro quality object up to isomorphism while leaving phenomenal labeling underdetermined under the object's automorphism group.
  • Tension or gap: The predictive-causal distinction is essential, but fixed-cut commutation in distribution is not yet the correct invariant for delayed, nonstationary, metastable propagation. A biologically real macro-process can fail a clock-aligned commutation test because its causal section moves with the wavefront, while temporal averaging can make a phase-scrambled replay or common-driver sequence appear to commute. Pointwise macro consistency also does not determine how kernels compose across sections, whether perturbations recover transversely, whether the process eventually escapes, or whether propagation direction changes its cortical consequences. The positive delay required by Baner's separation construction should therefore be treated as an endogenous causal coordinate rather than merely a proof condition. In addition, interventions on abstract macro realizers may not correspond to admissible neural perturbations with matched energy, location, and preserved local coding.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper transforms Baner's fixed-cut causal abstraction into moving-frame causal covariance: micro-level and macro-level intervention laws must approximately commute after registration by the wavefront arrival field, across a finite metastable interval, and under composition of successive sections. It then establishes a Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination construction in which coherent and phase-scrambled systems pass the same pointwise predictive and macro-commutation tests but differ in longitudinal interventional composition. The automorphism analysis is used to define a gauge-reduced transition residue, not to claim that a neural operator's causal individuation identifies phenomenal character.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Define a candidate wavefront from spatially localized response timing, connected propagation lineage, direction, and a finite metastable interval, then distinguish this construct from a standing oscillation, sequential activation, arbitrary time warp, and globally synchronized state.
  • Define the conjunction-sensitive binding operator B_W on moving sections, state the admissible intervention and route-isolation assumptions, and separate local feature preservation from downstream interaction so that feature erasure cannot masquerade as failed binding.
  • Construct the Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination pair showing that cut-wise discrimination geometry, fixed-clock decoding, synchrony, access, and report do not determine longitudinal causal composition, and identify which additional cross-time kernel observations break the equivalence.
  • Replace fixed-cut macro commutation with an arrival-aligned commutation defect and analyze when metastability, propagation delay, direction reversal, collision, or boundary crossing cause a macro wavefront description to succeed or fail.
  • Develop the transported quality-geometry account and gauge-reduced transition residue, explicitly separating an observed neural geometry, a formal invariant of the model, a bridge claim about phenomenal organization, and speculation about intrinsic qualitative character.
  • Synthesize the Nature adversarial result, the three open Cogitate modalities, structuralist methodology, dynamic RSA, nonlinear access thresholds, dreaming evidence, and traveling-wave models without treating any report, decoder, or wave statistic as a direct measure of phenomenal consciousness.
  • Specify what passive analyses of the existing iEEG and MEG-EEG data could establish about arrival fields and metastable transport, what fMRI could contribute about coarse spatial organization, and why these datasets alone cannot supply route-isolated causal identification.
  • Compare the new contribution with prior moving-section and front-flatness work, showing that the added claims concern higher-order binding, dynamic quality structure, nongauge bifurcation, and concrete consciousness-relevant failure conditions rather than another coherence metric.
  • Every substantive statement will be typed as an observed result, a consequence of stated mathematical assumptions, an empirical-mechanistic inference, a phenomenal bridge principle, or a speculative extension; conclusions will not migrate between these levels without an explicit premise.
  • Peer-reviewed empirical articles, peer-reviewed data descriptors, philosophical articles, preprints, and AI-generated working theories will be identified by provenance and evidential status; the two selected lab papers, Centered Closure Theory, and Multiscale Reflexive Causal Geometry will not be cited as established scientific authority.
  • The wavefront definition will require propagation lineage and physiologically interpretable arrival fields rather than accepting any apparent phase gradient, and analyses will test sensitivity to filtering, volume conduction, reference choice, evoked-response leakage, edge effects, signal-to-noise differences, and arbitrary smooth time warps.
  • The binding operator will be compared against temporal nulls that separately preserve power spectra, instantaneous covariance, event counts, local decoding, propagation-delay histograms, report distributions, or access accuracy, preventing one weak null from carrying the argument.
  • Local feature encodability must be preserved when binding is claimed to fail; otherwise the result is compatible with destruction of the inputs rather than selective loss of their temporal conjunction.
  • Cross-modal convergence will be assessed at the level of latent quantities such as arrival order, propagation direction, metastable residence, and transition timing, not by treating iEEG, MEG-EEG, and fMRI observables as measurements of the same raw variable.
  • Any moving-frame advantage must be evaluated against held-out trials, participants, sites, regions, and admissible registration families, because optimizing the arrival field and testing binding on the same observations would make the criterion circular.
  • Phenomenal consciousness, content-specific access or control, contemporaneous and delayed self-report, observer-attributed perceived consciousness, agency, and moral status will have separate claim ledgers, with no inference from the proposed operator directly to moral standing.
  • Admissible gauge transformations will preserve independently calibrated intervention semantics and readout roles; allowing arbitrary relabelings or time warps would erase genuine transitions, while allowing none would mistake coordinate changes for qualitative dynamics.
  • The paper will state where route isolation is only an evidence requirement rather than something available in the Cogitate datasets, ensuring that passive common-data reanalysis is not described as causal proof.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • A common subcortical or cortical driver may generate an orderly phase gradient and coordinated feature readout without the wavefront mediating binding; conditioning on or perturbationally isolating the common route is therefore necessary before the propagation pattern receives causal status.
  • A feedforward sweep with heterogeneous conduction delays may reproduce the same arrival field and local decoding without metastable recurrence, transverse recovery, or compositional reuse; finite residence and recovery distinguish this case from the proposed operator.
  • A standing synchronized assembly, global workspace ignition, or sustained posterior recurrent state may perform the relevant integration while traveling waves are a by-product of cortical geometry; the framework permits this outcome if binding survives loss or alteration of the candidate wavefront.
  • Predictive-processing or precision-routing dynamics may coordinate content by modulating gain across a hierarchy, with wave propagation reflecting the precision cascade rather than constituting the binding operation; content-matched changes in precision provenance are a rival mediator.
  • A phase-scrambled or temporally permuted process may preserve all instantaneous geometries and behavioral marginals while lacking coherent cross-section composition; this is the principal temporal null and motivates the Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination result.
  • Seizure-like hypersynchrony, anesthesia-related slow waves, and other altered states may display strong propagation or metastability without differentiated conscious content, showing that wave presence, coherence, or persistence alone is not sufficient.
  • Dreaming and imagery may exhibit phenomenal contents under reduced exteroceptive drive; failure to find the same sensory-anchored wavefront would bound the proposal to perceptual binding rather than refute every dynamic implementation of temporal unity.
  • Psychophysical intentional binding can arise from beliefs about agency and causation even if no corresponding distributed-feature wavefront operator changes, so subjective compression of action-outcome intervals is not evidence for the proposed neural mechanism.
  • Minimal adaptive or homeostatic systems may show metastable propagating control signals without unified subjectivity, valence, report, or moral status; adaptive regulation is therefore a boundary case rather than a lower rung on a single consciousness ladder.
  • The formal Snapshot-Transport Underdetermination claim fails if, under its declared finite-state and intervention assumptions, the matched pointwise kernels and static discrimination geometries mathematically force a unique cross-time coupling; an explicit derivation of uniqueness would remove the proposed missing invariant.
  • The neural-mechanistic hypothesis is undermined if arrival-aligned analyses of well-powered conscious-content data show no reproducible improvement over fixed-clock alignment or arbitrary smooth time-warp controls after filtering, volume conduction, evoked-response, and site effects are addressed.
  • The criterion is non-discriminating if phase-scrambled replay, standing-wave, conduction-delay-matched feedforward, or common-driver null systems satisfy the same conjunction-sensitive composition, metastable recovery, and transition-residue requirements as coherent wavefronts.
  • The binding interpretation fails if changes attributed to B_W disappear whenever local feature decodability is equated, indicating that the statistic measured loss of content encoding rather than loss of conjunction-specific transport.
  • The proposed operator is at most an access or report mechanism if its onset and bifurcation consistently follow decision commitment, motor preparation, or report formation and do not generalize to content contrasts with minimized or independently modeled report demands.
  • The wavefront hypothesis is falsified for a declared content family if direct perturbational evidence shows that selectively changing the candidate propagation lineage while preserving local codes leaves conjunction-sensitive neural effects and content discrimination unchanged, whereas altering a standing or nonpropagating process abolishes them.
  • A consciousness-specific bridge is weakened if the full operator profile occurs equally in matched unconscious, anesthetized, seizure-like, or purely adaptive control states, or is reliably absent during independently supported conscious perception or dreaming with adequate signal sensitivity.
  • The qualitative-transition claim fails if independently anchored content changes repeatedly occur while the gauge-reduced operator class remains stable, or if large nongauge operator transitions occur without content, access, arousal, or task changes after measurement artifacts are excluded.
  • The metastability component fails if the candidate process has no finite interval of transverse recovery and eventual escape, reducing either to a fragile feedforward transient or to a stationary attractor whose duration is imposed by the stimulus or analysis window.
  • No claim about intrinsic qualia is supported unless an independently defended bridge removes the remaining automorphism freedom; evidence that the operator is causally identifiable while phenomenal labels remain permutable confirms the framework's ceiling rather than its stronger phenomenal interpretation.

  • Analysis, Argument, or Model: Present the operator construction, moving-frame commutation defect, coherent-versus-phase-scrambled underdetermination pair, transported quality geometry, and gauge-reduced bifurcation ledger with assumptions and empirical interpretations stated before each formal step.
  • Alternatives, Counterarguments, and Boundary Cases: Evaluate common-driver waves, feedforward sweeps, standing synchrony, workspace or posterior recurrence, precision cascades, dreaming, anesthesia, seizure, minimal adaptive systems, and the objection that wavefronts track access rather than phenomenality.

  • Limitations: Acknowledge incomplete route isolation in current datasets, cross-modal resolution differences, dependence on declared scales and feature families, gauge and latent-state uncertainty, limited evidence outside visual tasks, and the inability of third-person dynamics alone to identify intrinsic experience.
  • Conclusion: Conclude that cross-time causal transport is an independent invariant omitted by static quality spaces and fixed-cut abstractions, and that metastable wavefronts are serious temporal-binding candidates only when they pass conjunction, composition, metastability, perturbation, and null-system tests.
