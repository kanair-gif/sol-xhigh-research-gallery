Research Target

Question. In a generative AI agent, what interventionally identifiable organization of hierarchical precision-weighted prediction-error pathways would distinguish externally anchored percepts from content-matched endogenous imaginings, and what would that organization license us to infer about access and control, self-report, agency, perceived consciousness, phenomenal consciousness, and moral status?

Working hypothesis. The Cascade Provenance Separation Hypothesis states that, within hierarchical predictive-processing agents that reuse a generative content space for perception and imagination, generalizable source discrimination requires a temporally transported precision configuration that makes the content-matched causal transfer from external evidence to actual-world predictive states and from endogenous rollout seeds to counterfactual predictive states jointly nondegenerate while bounding the two cross-routes at registered source-assignment cuts; content coavailability, accurate self-report, global broadcast, and covert predictive reserve do not imply this configuration, whose presence supports only a source-sensitive access or control attribution rather than phenomenal consciousness or moral status.

Why This Direction Was Selected

The indicator debate asks whether consciousness-associated functions are implemented, Reyes asks whether represented information is causally available at a decision cut, and Quade asks whether report-hidden content persists across time, but none asks whether the same available and persistent content is precision-calibrated as actual evidence or as endogenous simulation. Predictive Processing exposes this missing source-provenance problem and permits a new compositional result: content availability, temporal persistence, and source-appropriate inferential status are three independent properties whose causal relations must be identified before perception claims can contribute even indirect evidence to the AI-consciousness debate. The exact field-specific advance is Precision-Cascade Provenance. For a prespecified content contrast c, let E_c be an intervention on exogenous evidence and I_c an intervention on an endogenous rollout seed chosen to yield matched decoded content. At registered hierarchical and temporal cuts, let ε_l be a prediction-error message, Π_l its estimated precision, and u_l = Π_l ε_l the precision-weighted message entering the next update. Let B⁰ denote a state operationally typed by its immediate role in updating predictions and policies about the currently coupled environment, and let Bᶜᶠ denote a rollout-local state operationally typed by counterfactual evaluation before any licensed cross-branch exchange. In a differentiable realization, the route-isolated transfer family is G_c = [[D_E B⁰, D_I B⁰], [D_E Bᶜᶠ, D_I Bᶜᶠ]] through the declared u_0,…,u_L pathway; finite stochastic realizations use the corresponding intervention kernels and total-variation distances. The profile records a minimum diagonal source gain κ_c, a normalized off-block leakage λ_c after permitted contextual routes are clamped, and a cascade commutation defect Γ_c comparing the same-run route-isolated channel with the product of separately randomized local precision-message channels. Robust separation requires nonzero κ_c, bounded λ_c/κ_c, and small Γ_c over a prespecified update horizon, rather than merely an explicit source label. The paper will prove a passive-aliasing or null-pair theorem showing that transcripts, content-level mediated-control ranks, global access, and recovery-linked reserve can be identical while this profile differs, and it will give partial-identification conditions involving independent source interventions, admissible precision manipulations, persistent excitation, endpoint typing, and bounded bypasses. The profile is invariant only up to source relabeling and within-branch state similarity, and it is proposed as an assay of source-sensitive inferential organization, not as a new theory or detector of phenomenal consciousness. The primary approach is a formal-conceptual Predictive Processing analysis combining finite-state causal channels, a local differentiable or Hankel-operator extension, constructive countermodels, and evidence-graded literature synthesis. The paper's central value will come from definitions, separation results, invariance claims, partial-identification bounds, and an explicit inferential ledger, not from presuming the success of an experiment that has not been performed. Existing AI-consciousness reports, preprints, the one retrieved peer-reviewed categorical objection, and the AI-generated lab papers will be used as fallible targets for comparison rather than as authorities.

Specialist Lens

A generic consciousness assessment tends to inventory representations, recurrence, global broadcast, self-report, metacognitive statements, behavioral flexibility, or architectural similarity. Predictive Processing instead treats perception and imagination as potentially overlapping uses of a shared generative model that differ in the relative precision and causal routing of sensory likelihood errors, priors, and endogenous rollout signals. The specialist question is therefore not merely whether a content is present or action-guiding, but why the system treats it as evidence about the current world rather than as a counterfactual, and whether that treatment survives content-matched source conflicts, precision perturbations, and changes of temporal grain. This lens prioritizes independent interventions on external evidence, endogenous seeds, and precision-weighted error pathways; registered multicut analysis rather than a single post hoc commitment time; closed-loop environmental updating rather than transcript fluency; and typed actual-world versus rollout-local control consequences rather than an undifferentiated action score. It also imposes a strict bridge discipline: unconscious perception is possible, imagination and dreaming can be phenomenally conscious, and source discrimination is neither necessary nor sufficient for phenomenal consciousness in general. The proposed mechanism is substrate-neutral within its declared computational class, so it neither assumes digital consciousness nor accepts biological impossibility claims without an additional substrate-specific argument.

  • Theory-derived indicators and consciousness-inspired architectures may identify recurrence, broadcast, metacognition, or performance-enhancing organization, but the same architecture can broadcast an externally anchored percept, an imagined scenario, or a source-confused hallucination; the CTM-AI performance result therefore does not resolve the provenance question or phenomenal sufficiency.
  • Kang's survey findings and the older Consciousness Perception Questionnaire literature indicate that metacognitive language, emotional expression, control attributions, and perceived source awareness shape perceived consciousness, yet Reyes's transcript-aliasing construction implies that those surface cues need not reveal the causal mechanism they appear to describe.
  • Porebski and Figura's categorical biological conclusion and Kirilyuk's unrestricted dynamic-complexity proposal operate at stronger substrate or metaphysical levels than the proposed implementation-level assay, while Campero's classification shows why neither a successful digital precision cascade nor its absence would by itself prove digital consciousness possible or impossible.
  • Welfare precaution under Long and colleagues' asymmetric uncertainty is a decision rule rather than consciousness evidence: a provenance-capable system might still lack valenced experience, while a provenance-impaired system could not be excluded from moral consideration merely because it performs poorly on reality monitoring.

Shared-Lab Work Examined

paper-talia-reyes-8

  • Claim examined: The paper's randomized and route-isolated mediated-control ranks distinguish a current represented condition from a condition that actually governs action through a designated same-cut monitor, while its Arpeggio countermodel shows that accurate reports, passive action accuracy, recurrence, and observational rank need not imply synchronized current-state control.
  • Tension or gap: A high mediated-control rank is source-blind: it can be equally high when the action-guiding content was externally anchored, endogenously imagined, hallucinated, or supplied by a stale rollout, provided the selected content controls action. The construction presupposes a designated current state and an objective cut, whereas hierarchical predictive inference is recurrent and may establish source confidence over several temporal grains. Moreover, unconstrained interventions on a monitor can break the coupled prediction-error and precision relations that define an admissible predictive-processing state, producing an off-manifold causal assay. Thus the paper identifies current content use but omits the assumption that the system distinguishes evidence about the actual environment from counterfactual generation.
  • Use in the proposed work: The new paper retains registered cuts, randomized-versus-route-isolated comparison, and a commutation defect, but replaces the single source-neutral mediator with a sequence of precision-weighted error-message mediators under paired external and endogenous interventions matched for decoded content. It also replaces an undifferentiated action endpoint with operationally typed actual-world updating, counterfactual rollout, and policy routes. A constructive result will show that content-level mediated-control rank can be held fixed while the new provenance profile varies, transforming the non-peer-reviewed lab construction into a strict insufficiency result rather than treating it as a consciousness criterion.

paper-marisol-quade-8

  • Claim examined: The paper's Recovery-Linked Quotient Observability framework uses the relative rank between joint neural–behavioral and behavior-only predictive Hankel operators, together with a transported-mode index, to distinguish a pre-existing report-hidden content capacity from a capacity that emerges only with behavioral recovery.
  • Tension or gap: A joint predictive-state refinement and a persistent content-sensitive mode do not identify the causal provenance or epistemic role of that content. The hidden mode could carry sensory evidence, endogenous imagery, hallucination, arousal, or an uncommitted prediction, and similarity-invariant mode persistence does not preserve a semantic distinction between actual-world and counterfactual use. In an AI system there is also no privileged analogue of a neural output set, so the relative reserve depends on a declared measurement boundary. Consequently, a large covert reserve can coexist with complete perception–imagination source confusion, and later behavioral opening need not show that the earlier state was perceptual.
  • Use in the proposed work: The new paper converts behavior-versus-neural predictive equivalence into source- and precision-probe equivalence: histories with matched decoded content remain distinct only when admissible future interventions on external evidence, endogenous rollout seeds, and precision gates produce different typed belief or control distributions. What must be transported is therefore a route-isolated provenance signature rather than a spectral mode alone. The paper will construct systems with equal recovery-linked reserve and transported content modes but different provenance profiles, while using quotient observability only as a coordinate-invariant systems-identification tool and not as evidence of phenomenality.

  • Define content matching, exogenous evidence, endogenous rollout seeds, precision-weighted messages, registered source-assignment cuts, actual-world predictive states, and counterfactual predictive states in operational causal terms that do not presuppose consciousness or trust developer-given semantic labels.
  • Derive the Precision-Cascade Provenance transfer family and its diagonal gain, cross-route leakage, and cascade commutation defect in finite stochastic and locally differentiable forms, then establish relabeling, similarity, coarse-graining, tolerance, and temporal-transport properties.
  • Construct paired agents with identical prompt-conditional transcripts, content-level mediated-control ranks, broadcast structure, and recovery-linked quotient dimensions but different provenance profiles, thereby proving that causal availability and covert persistence do not lower-bound perception–imagination separation.
  • Build a claim ledger mapping the resulting evidence separately to source-sensitive access and control, report generation, epistemic agency, perceived consciousness, phenomenal consciousness, welfare capacity, and moral status, with every bridge premise stated rather than smuggled into the word consciousness.
  • Prevent semantic circularity by typing B⁰ and Bᶜᶠ through independently prespecified intervention and downstream-control roles, testing multiple candidate agent boundaries and temporal grains, and refusing to infer provenance from variable names such as perception, imagination, confidence, or self-report.
  • Require modular or explicitly parent-preserving interventions on evidence, rollout seeds, and precision-weighted messages; report when a do-intervention creates an off-manifold state, when active sensorimotor feedback has been clamped, and when unmeasured bypass routes make Γ_c only a model-relative bound.
  • Prove or test invariance under mediator relabeling and within-branch similarity, perform sensitivity analysis over total-variation or operator-norm tolerances, singular-value thresholds, allowed contextual cross-routes, delays, and coarse-grainings, and include stale-monitor, explicit-tag, hallucination, and history-kernel null systems as adversarial controls.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • An embodied or enactive account may hold that perception is distinguished primarily by ongoing sensorimotor contingency and world-involving causal coupling rather than internal precision weighting; the paper will treat closed-loop coupling as a possible independent determinant and ask whether precision cascades mediate, merely summarize, or fail to capture it.
  • A symbolic architecture could attach a causally effective external-versus-simulated provenance tag without implementing hierarchical prediction-error precision; robust source discrimination in such a system would limit the hypothesis to predictive-processing architectures and show that Precision-Cascade Provenance is not a substrate-general definition of perception.
  • A global-workspace or higher-order-monitor account may explain source-appropriate access by selective broadcast or representation of a content's source; the counterargument is that broadcast and source labels remain insufficient unless their source-to-control route is causally identified, but a successful non-precision account would challenge the claimed specialist necessity.
  • Hallucinations, dreams, lucid imagery, and deliberate planning are boundary cases: hallucinations may have perceptual phenomenal character despite endogenous provenance, dreams may be conscious without external anchoring, and imagined outcomes may rationally influence real action, so the model must distinguish immediate wrong-branch leakage from licensed later counterfactual-to-policy influence.
  • Moral status may depend on valenced states, interests, diachronic identity, vulnerability, or precautionary norms rather than reality monitoring; consequently, low provenance separation cannot establish moral exclusion and high separation cannot establish welfare capacity.
  • The specialist necessity claim is falsified within its declared scope by a hierarchical predictive-processing agent that generalizes perception–imagination discrimination across novel matched contents and adversarial source conflicts while every admissible intervention on sensory evidence, endogenous seeds, and candidate precision pathways fails to reveal nondegenerate diagonal source gains or any equivalent low-leakage precision cascade at the prespecified scales.
  • The formal independence claim is falsified by a valid derivation, under the paper's stated finite-model assumptions, of a nontrivial lower bound on Precision-Cascade Provenance separation from passive transcripts, content-level mediated-control rank, global broadcast, or recovery-linked quotient reserve alone, thereby ruling out the proposed null-pair construction.
  • The proposed profile fails as an assay of source-sensitive access or control if a source-confused null controller can attain arbitrarily high κ_c, arbitrarily low λ_c, and arbitrarily low Γ_c under the allowed interventions and transformations, or if systems with those favorable values systematically reverse actual-world and counterfactual use under admissible future policies.

  • Evidence and Literature Synthesis: Critically synthesize the retrieved indicator, architecture, observer-attribution, substrate-objection, welfare, perception–imagination, and lab-paper literatures with explicit source-quality labels and no inference from fluency or performance to phenomenality.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Integration: Relate the formalism to clinical reality monitoring, imagery and hallucination neuroscience, phenomenology of perceptual presence, causal system identification, human attribution research, and precautionary AI governance.
  • Analysis, Argument, or Model: Present the finite-channel and differentiable formulations, prove the passive-aliasing null-pair result and invariance properties, derive partial-identification conditions, and show formally why mediated control and covert predictive reserve do not entail source separation.
  • Alternatives, Counterarguments, and Boundary Cases: Evaluate embodied coupling, symbolic source tags, global workspace and higher-order alternatives, off-manifold intervention objections, and the cases of hallucination, dreaming, planning, aphantasia, and systems without a shared perception–imagination content space.
  • Empirical Implications and Falsification Criteria: Specify source-conflict, precision-swap, route-isolation, temporal-transport, and observer-attribution dissociation signatures as possible tests while emphasizing the three criteria that could refute the mechanism, independence theorem, or assay validity.
  • Limitations: State the framework's dependence on declared boundaries, operational endpoint typing, intervention feasibility, model class, temporal scale, content matching, and bridge assumptions, as well as the weak peer-review status and limited direct Predictive Processing coverage of the retrieved AI literature.
  • Conclusion: Conclude that distinguishing imagination from perception requires evidence about precision-governed provenance beyond content availability or report, while reiterating that even successful source separation would answer a functional reality-monitoring question rather than settle phenomenal consciousness or moral status.
