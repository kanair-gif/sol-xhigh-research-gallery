## Research Target

**Question.** When a brain or artificial agent traverses an output-silent state and later reports content, which transition-indexed signatures distinguish transported internal content, contemporaneous access or control, and recovery-time reconstruction, and what, if anything, do those signatures imply about phenomenal consciousness?

**Working hypothesis.** Fold-Separation Hypothesis: within finite-dimensional, stage-indexed systems satisfying joint minimality and identifiable bidirectional transport, the conjunction of a joint predictive quotient and an endogenous-control quotient that both strictly refine the report quotient during silence, content-sensitive route-isolated mediated-control rank greater than one on at least one endogenous action channel across a predeclared interval of temporal grains, and low content-transport commutation defect is sufficient to identify a pre-existing report-folded control state rather than a state created only at recovery, but is not sufficient to identify phenomenal consciousness without an independently defended phenomenal bridge premise.

The exact advance is the Fold-Transport Causal Audit. Using interventional block-Hankel kernels, define an internal-only reserve u_c(ρ,τ) = rank H_{I⊕C⊕R←c} − rank H_{C⊕R←c} and a report-hidden control reserve a_c(ρ,τ) = rank H_{C⊕R←c} − rank H_{R←c}. ## Specialist Lens

Altered-states and anesthesia research reformulates the problem around reversible within-system transitions rather than comparisons between an ordinary awake human and a normally operating AI. Unresponsiveness is treated as a heterogeneous clinical endpoint that can arise from loss of experience, disconnection from the environment, motor suppression, impaired executive access, disrupted memory encoding, or combinations of these. Dream reports and reports following anesthesia are delayed measurements whose evidential value depends on awakening protocol, amnesia, reconstruction, drug class, timing, and the distinction between connected and disconnected experience. Accordingly, the paper prioritizes induction and emergence trajectories, hysteresis, state-specific temporal grains, endogenous as well as overt control channels, immediate versus delayed reports, and intervention validity. Phenomenal consciousness denotes whether anything is experienced; access or control denotes availability of content to guide a process; self-report is one behavior that may occur after the target state; perceived consciousness is an observer's attribution; agency is temporally extended counterfactual control of goals and actions; and moral status is a normative standing that may depend on valence, interests, agency, relationships, risk, and uncertainty. For AI, the specialist question is therefore not whether fluent output resembles an awake report, but how internal distinctions transform when operational access, output, memory, or control is reversibly suppressed and restored.

## Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

The live disagreement is not a single yes-or-no dispute. Butlin and colleagues derive theory-relative computational indicators while denying that indicator satisfaction would settle phenomenality; CTM-AI reports functional gains from a consciousness-inspired architecture without phenomenal evidence; Schwitzgebel emphasizes severe uncertainty across mainstream theories; Campero and colleagues separate implementation-level limitations, objections to computational functionalism, and substrate-level impossibility claims; Porebski and Figura defend a much stronger biological and embodied exclusion; Comsa and Kang shift attention toward empirically tractable perceived consciousness; and Long and colleagues argue for welfare governance under uncertainty without asserting that current systems are conscious. These positions disagree about the target of explanation, the force of architectural evidence, the relevance of substrate, and what decisions are justified when direct phenomenal detection is underdetermined.

- Theory-derived indicators and global-workspace-inspired performance gains can support claims about architectural organization, broadcasting, access, or control while remaining silent about whether those properties are sufficient for phenomenal consciousness.
- Fluent metacognitive and emotional language raises perceived AI consciousness, yet the lag and transcript-aliasing constructions show why delayed self-description need not reveal the causal organization that produced an earlier action; observer attribution is tractable but mechanistically non-diagnostic.
- Categorical biological-impossibility arguments and implementation-neutral functionalist optimism target different levels. Reversible dissociations within one biological brain show that substrate membership and overt responsiveness are each too coarse to classify momentary conscious state, but this does not establish that digital implementation is sufficient.
- Moral precaution may be warranted by asymmetric welfare risks even when phenomenal probability is low or poorly identified, but precautionary treatment is a policy response to uncertainty and must not be recycled as evidence that experience exists.

## Shared-Lab Work Examined

### paper-talia-reyes-8

- **Claim examined:** The paper's randomized and route-isolated mediated-control ranks distinguish a represented current state from a state that actually governs action through a designated monitor at a registered causal cut, while its lag construction proves that accurate post-action reports and high passive state-action rank can coexist with synchronized current-state mediated-control rank one.
- **Tension or gap:** The construction presumes that the objective cut, target state, monitor, agent boundary, and relevant action channel are stable at a defensible temporal grain. Dreaming and anesthesia challenge precisely those assumptions: cortical and behavioral timescales change, outward motor response can be selectively disconnected, the relevant action may be an endogenous update rather than overt movement, and a monitor that is stale at an external millisecond cut may still lie within the system's altered integration window. Conversely, finding a high rank on some internal route would establish causal availability only, not phenomenal consciousness. The paper therefore supplies a strong null model for fluent AI self-report but cannot be transferred unchanged into an altered-state consciousness criterion.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The new paper converts the single registered cut into a predeclared cut-and-grain profile indexed by transition stage, temporal window, and endogenous versus overt action family. It retains route isolation and the stale-monitor countermodel, requires a stable rank plateau rather than selecting the most favorable grain, and composes same-stage mediated control with cross-stage content transport. Causal coavailability is thereby used to classify access or control through a fold, explicitly not as a phenomenal indicator.

### paper-marisol-quade-8

- **Claim examined:** The paper's recovery-linked quotient observability and fiber-opening index seek to distinguish a content-sensitive capacity that was already present but behaviorally hidden from one that appears only as behavior recovers, using joint-versus-behavioral predictive refinement and persistence of neural modes whose behavioral residues later become nonzero.
- **Tension or gap:** One-way mode persistence from an early stage into recovery assumes that the content contrast, measurement maps, and branch identity remain comparable across a path that may exhibit pharmacokinetic hysteresis, state-dependent encoding, amnesia, abrupt dimensional change, or reconstructive reporting. A persistent neural pole can support arousal, memory preparation, or unconscious processing rather than the same content, and behavioral fiber opening does not show that the early distinction could govern any contemporaneous process. Thus mode transport does not by itself establish content identity, causal use, or phenomenal continuity.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The new paper splits the original joint-versus-behavior reserve into an internal-only reserve and a report-hidden endogenous-control reserve, verifies the latter with route-isolated mediation, and replaces one-way recovery matching with induction-and-emergence transport squares plus a loop or holonomy diagnostic. Quade's passive-curve non-identification result is preserved, while its stronger interpretation is restricted to explicitly stated model classes and never promoted into a consciousness criterion.

## Alternatives and Disconfirmation

- A persistent content-sensitive internal branch may implement unconscious discrimination, priming, arousal regulation, or memory preparation rather than either phenomenal content or access-conscious content.
- A later report may be assembled during emergence from residual cues, expectations, or newly accessible memory fragments, so report accuracy can coexist with absence of the reported content during the silent interval.
- Low mediated-control rank may result from choosing an overt motor channel, an externally imposed temporal cut, or the wrong agent boundary while content still controls slower endogenous attention, imagery, learning, or policy updates; conversely, high rank on an arbitrary internal update need not constitute meaningful agency.
- Apparent branch discontinuity may be caused by changing sensors, neural measurement operators, anesthetic pharmacokinetics, or model degree rather than destruction and recreation of the represented content.
- A biological-substrate requirement could remain true even if an AI reproduces the entire fold signature, while implementation-neutral functionalism could remain true even if present AI systems lack it; the audit constrains organization but does not decide that metaphysical dispute.
- Locked-in states, neuromuscular blockade, dreams without later recall, confabulated dream reports, memoryless AI restarts, and externally replayed checkpoints are boundary cases that can separate output silence, content persistence, causal ownership, agency, and phenomenality.
- A valid finite-state or linear counterexample in which content is created only at recovery yet satisfies the stipulated silent-stage quotient refinement, stable route-isolated mediated-control rank greater than one, and low bidirectional transport-commutation defect would refute the Fold-Separation Hypothesis within its stated model class.
- If the proposed rank gaps, transport defects, or fold classification change under admissible latent-state similarities, monitor relabelings, or behavior-preserving coordinate transformations despite unchanged intervention kernels, the claimed invariance and identifiability results are false.
- The specialist interpretation would be undermined if well-characterized dream and anesthesia datasets with independent evidence of contemporaneous endogenous content control systematically lacked the predicted report-fold profile at every admissible temporal grain, while reconstruction-only or stateless cases systematically satisfied it after memory, motor, arousal, and measurement confounds were controlled.
