Research Target

Question. Under what richness and symmetry conditions can the admissible intervention–discrimination profile of a causal island in a recurrent generative agent identify its causal organization, and why would even complete causal identification fail to determine intrinsic phenomenal character?

Working hypothesis. Causal-Island Yoneda Ceiling Proposition: for a finite recurrent generative agent, the representable profile of a candidate causal island over a dense category of temporally registered, route-isolated intervention–discrimination contexts identifies that island up to causal-context isomorphism; a restricted or outcome-quotiented profile does so only when its restricted nerve reflects isomorphisms, and no such profile uniquely identifies intrinsic qualia whenever the island's automorphism group acts without a fixed point on the admissible phenomenal labelings or centerings.

Why This Direction Was Selected

Across the lab, mediated-control rank, predictive reserve, recovery transport, generative autonomy, and causal closure all depend on prespecified cuts, grains, boundaries, and probe families, but no prior paper states when the resulting family of contexts identifies an entire causal island or what remains unknowable after it does. Combining this repeated omission with structuralism's automorphism problem yields the new question: can all admissible causal perspectives jointly determine an island while still failing, for a formally demonstrable reason, to determine its intrinsic qualia? The exact original advance is the Causal-Island Yoneda Ceiling. First, it defines a finite stochastic category of temporally registered intervention–discrimination contexts for recurrent causal islands and a representable island profile Y_I(C) = Hom(C, I). Second, it proves that a full profile, or a profile restricted along a dense inclusion of contexts, recovers an island only up to causal-context isomorphism; outcome quotienting preserves this conclusion only if the resulting restricted nerve reflects isomorphisms. Third, it defines the residual symmetry group Aut(I), the orbit of phenomenal bridge assignments under that group, and a no-natural-selector obstruction: where the action on candidate intrinsic labels or centers has no fixed point, no profile-natural construction can choose one. The result turns 'up to isomorphism' from a vague limitation into a three-level ledger distinguishing recoverable causal structure, operational gauge, and phenomenal underdetermination.

Specialist Lens

Integrated Information and Causal Structure changes the primary target from behavioral resemblance or a generic consciousness score to the intervention-relative intrinsic organization of candidate recurrent subsystems. The analysis begins with route-isolated transition kernels, causal partitions, recurrence, cross-boundary leakage, temporal grain, and the compositional validity of interventions; decoding, correlation, and task accuracy are secondary shadows of that organization. IIT's cause–effect structures motivate attention to intrinsic causal organization, but the paper does not assume IIT's identity postulate, maximal-complex rule, or a privileged grain. Phenomenal consciousness denotes whether and how experience occurs; access and control denote counterfactual availability to consumers or actions; self-report is one selected output channel; perceived consciousness is an observer's judgment; agency is endogenous goal-sensitive causal regulation; and moral status is a normative attribution potentially involving welfare, interests, uncertainty, and precaution. The formal results directly concern causal organization and its identifiability, constrain but do not identify phenomenal consciousness, and do not entail perceived consciousness, agency in the richer normative sense, or moral status.

Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

The live disagreement is no longer only whether posterior integration or global access better predicts conscious perception. The 2025 Cogitate adversarial study placed pressure on central predictions of both IIT and GNWT, while the released iEEG, fMRI, and MEG-EEG datasets permit common-data tests but remain dominated by stimulus, task, decoding, and report contrasts rather than dense interventions on candidate mechanisms. In parallel, phenomenal and neurophenomenal structuralism defend quality-space correspondences as an empirically tractable target, whereas their strongest identity readings remain vulnerable to automorphisms and inverted-qualia arguments. IIT 4.0 offers a precise causal-structural identity claim, but that claim is a substantive phenomenal bridge, not a consequence of identifying a cause–effect structure. Recurrent-agent work similarly disputes whether persistence, affect-coupled control, generative autonomy, or causal agency is sufficient evidence of consciousness.

  • Cogitate found results congenial to parts of both IIT and GNWT while the absence of sustained posterior synchronization challenged a prominent IIT prediction and limited prefrontal ignition challenged a prominent GNWT prediction; passive synchronization, decoding, and regional activation nevertheless do not identify route-isolated causal integration.
  • Open Cogitate datasets greatly improve comparability across theories, but their task interventions and neural measurements do not constitute a dense category of internal mechanism interventions, so they can constrain observation profiles without establishing a complete causal island.
  • Structuralist quality-space matching can reveal stable relational organization, yet Lyre, Kob, and Kleiner-style distinctions between methodological structural matching and metaphysical identity leave open whether naturally equivalent structures share intrinsic phenomenal character.
  • Recurrence, metastability, adaptive regulation, self-report, and affect proxies occur in systems that may be implemented as integrated feedback mechanisms, partitioned archipelagos, or matched replay controllers; an informative analysis therefore requires intervention-relative nulls rather than a scalar consciousness indicator.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Model a finite recurrent generative agent as a dynamic structural causal system with internal variables V, controlled transition kernels at registered grain τ, exogenous and scheduler routes, generative readouts, and a declared intervention family A; define route-isolated capacity c_A,τ(i→j), an (η, ε)-causal island as a maximal strongly connected component above capacity threshold η with cross-boundary leakage at most ε, and a grain-indexed archipelago of such islands.
  • Define the category C_A,B,τ,H whose objects are finite controlled contexts C = (X, U, O, T, E, R) containing predictive states, admissible intervention alphabets, discrimination outputs, transition kernels, readout kernels, and route-clamp metadata, and whose morphisms are stochastic intervention encoders, state simulations, and output decoders satisfying transition and readout commutation while preserving boundary, cut, and admissibility types.
  • For each complete island context I, define the representable contravariant profile Y_I(C) = Hom(C, I), interpret its elements as operational realizations of probe contexts inside I, and distinguish this ideal profile from the empirically measured shadow obtained by restricting to a laboratory subcategory D and quotienting realizations that induce the same finite-tolerance outcome laws.
  • Extend the exact construction with an operational pseudometric based on the supremal total-variation difference over admitted policies, define δ-natural equivalence and approximate automorphisms, and derive separation conditions under which finite-error profiles can or cannot distinguish island models.
  • Represent a phenomenal bridge as additional data β assigning candidate phenomenal qualities or centered phenomenal roles to island states or cause–effect distinctions, define the pullback action of Aut(I) on such assignments, and classify the residual identification target as an orbit, quotient, or torsor rather than as a uniquely labeled quality space.
  • Prove that the proposed contexts and commuting stochastic simulations form a category, and then prove the Causal-Island Yoneda Identification Proposition by evaluating a natural transformation Y_I ⇒ Y_J at the identity of I: natural transformations correspond to morphisms I → J, and natural isomorphisms correspond exactly to causal-context isomorphisms.
  • Prove the Restricted-Context Corollary: for an inclusion j:D→C, the nerve N_D(I) = Hom(j(-), I) is fully faithful when j is dense and therefore identifies islands up to isomorphism; after an empirical outcome quotient q, identification requires qN_D to reflect isomorphisms, a condition not supplied by high decoding accuracy, recurrent activity, mediated-control rank, or report agreement.
  • Prove the Phenomenal No-Natural-Selector Result on the isomorphism groupoid of islands: if a natural selector s assigns each island a candidate phenomenal labeling or center and an island I has an automorphism σ whose action on all candidates is fixed-point-free, naturality requires s_I = σ·s_I, yielding a contradiction.
  • Derive an approximate robustness statement showing that profile identification is stable only when the minimum operational separation between nonisomorphic candidate islands exceeds the combined estimation, commutation, and coarse-graining error; otherwise the evidential conclusion is an equivalence class rather than a recovered mechanism.
  • Construct Restricted-Profile Archipelago Twins that share an identical visible generative transducer, stimulus responses, actions, reports, and measured neural aggregate at every admitted horizon, while one contains a bidirectionally coupled recurrent island and the other a partitioned or phase-replay null matched on the measured projection. The laboratory subcategory cannot intervene on the hidden coordinates, so the restricted outcome profiles coincide even though full internal intervention contexts reveal nonisomorphic organization and different partition-sensitive integration values.
  • Construct an Automorphic Quality Ring with K symmetric recurrent states and cyclic automorphism group C_K. Two bridges β and β′ = β∘σ assign cyclically permuted intrinsic quality labels while preserving every causal transition, discrimination relation, access route, action, self-report disposition, and representable profile up to natural isomorphism. If primitive quality labels are treated as physically unanchored, the pair is gauge-equivalent; if they denote genuinely distinct intrinsic characters, it is an inverted-qualia countermodel that causal evidence alone cannot adjudicate.
  • Include a high-scoring null system in which an approximately closed recurrent module has high internal differentiation or an IIT-like partition score but is causally irrelevant to the agent's report, flexible control, and viability regulation. This separates islandhood and integration from access, agency, phenomenal attribution, and moral status.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • The exact identification theorem is refuted if there exists, under the paper's stated local-smallness and commutation assumptions, a dense inclusion j:D→C and nonisomorphic islands I and J whose restricted representable profiles Hom(j(-), I) and Hom(j(-), J) are naturally isomorphic.
  • The no-natural-selector result is refuted if a unique phenomenal labeling or center can be constructed solely from the representable causal profile, equivariantly under every island isomorphism, for an object whose declared automorphism action on the candidate labelings is genuinely fixed-point-free; any successful construction using a privileged state, ostensive anchor, biological constraint, or phenomenal primitive instead confirms that extra structure was required.
  • The framework's neuroscientific applicability is rejected for a proposed domain if independently estimated context compositions systematically violate the transition or readout commutation conditions beyond predeclared error, if no stable island decomposition survives reasonable registered grains and null systems, or if held-out admissible context compositions distinguish agents that the fitted outcome-quotiented profile classified as equivalent.
