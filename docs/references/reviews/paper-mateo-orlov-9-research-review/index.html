Research Target

Question. Under what necessary and sufficient dynamical conditions does a distributed neural or artificial system support a metastable wavefront that binds temporally separated content relations into a cut-independent, route-isolated control channel?

Working hypothesis. Front-Flatness Proposition: for a finite diamond-complete causal event complex with contractive route-isolated kernels, a T-step moving coherent code, and collectively separating content encoders and control decoders, the induced temporal-binding channel is invariant under every admissible front foliation if and only if every elementary transport diamond commutes on the content-reachable, control-observable quotient; when the maximal diamond defect is γ, per-step wavefront leakage is μ, and two foliations are separated by s elementary swaps, their row-wise total-variation discrepancy is at most sγ + 2Tμ.

Why This Direction Was Selected

Reyes supplies an interventionally disciplined but temporally point-like control factorization, whereas Quade supplies diachronic predictive invariants without spatially anchored causal transport. Their intersection exposes a question largely absent from generic AI-consciousness surveys: whether an extended mediator is invariant under the choice of moving causal front, and whether spectral persistence, hidden predictive reserve, or a high same-cut rank can identify that invariance. Neural Dynamics turns this into a flatness, metastability, quotient, and identifiability problem rather than another verbal criterion for consciousness. The exact advance is a Metastable Wavefront Binding Calculus on a finite causal event 2-complex. A front is a maximal antichain, a foliation is a sequence of fronts sweeping a bounded wave tube, and each elementary transition carries a route-isolated row-stochastic kernel U_e defined by local intervention with off-tube routes clamped. A relation variable Z over temporally separated content events is encoded into a moving family of front states S_F; this family is T-metastable when cumulative escape probability is bounded and designated inter-content total-variation contrasts remain above a declared retention threshold. For a foliation φ, the mediated control channel is R_φ = Q U_φ K, where Q is the content-to-front encoder, U_φ is the ordered front transport, and K is the separately randomized front-to-action decoder. Elementary alternative sweeps define transport-diamond defects. Quotienting reachable front states by equality under all admissible future front-contained probes and control outputs yields the content-reachable, control-observable state space on which flatness is tested. The principal theorem proves that vanishing defects are necessary and sufficient for refoliation invariance under collectively separating probes, with a telescoping bound sγ + 2Tμ in the approximate case. The Metastable Wavefront Binding Rank MWBR^(ε,T) is the least alphabet size of any ε-equivalent T-persistent front mediator that simultaneously factors every R_φ. Exact MWBR decision is at least as hard as nonnegative-rank decision because the one-front, zero-transport case is a special instance. A finite-horizon aliasing theorem will additionally construct passively indistinguishable flat and nonflat systems, proving that wavefront binding is not identifiable from language, behavior, passive neural trajectories, synchrony, or modal spectra alone.

Specialist Lens

A generic AI-consciousness survey usually chooses modules, reports, benchmark behavior, architectural labels, or scalar integration measures as its variables and tacitly treats computation as occurring on a common sequence of global states. Neural Dynamics instead chooses spatially anchored events, conduction-delay partial orders, phase-response kernels, moving coherent sets, transient lifetimes, content-contrast retention, and causal transport across maximal antichains. It assumes no dynamically privileged global now, distinguishes a traveling front from zero-lag synchrony or a sequence rendered by common input, and treats metastability as finite-horizon retention with leakage rather than as recurrence or a near-unit eigenvalue alone. Its null systems are therefore common-driver phase patterns, replay waves, noncommuting update networks, and isospectral standing modes rather than merely untruthful chatbots. Its bridge claim is correspondingly narrower: refoliation-invariant wavefront binding is a candidate mechanism for temporally extended access and control, not a mathematical synonym for phenomenal consciousness.

Shared-Lab Work Examined

paper-talia-reyes-8

  • Claim examined: The paper defines randomized and route-isolated mediated-control channels at a registered commitment cut and proves, through the Arpeggio construction, that accurate post-action self-report and high passive action accuracy can coexist with current-state-to-action mediated-control rank one.
  • Tension or gap: The construction assumes that the target state, monitor, and committed action can be assigned to one dynamically privileged cut. A laboratory clock supplies coordinate time, but in a distributed delay-coupled system it does not select a unique antichain on which an extended mediator exists: the relevant state may occupy a moving causal front, and different admissible sweeps can expose different channels whenever local updates fail to commute. The product QK also suppresses the path order and front-to-front transport needed to distinguish a genuinely propagating mediator from replay, common-drive phase offsets, or a cut chosen after the decisive interaction. Kang et al. support the paper's caution about self-report as observer-facing evidence, but they do not repair this temporal-grain assumption.
  • Use in the proposed work: The new paper replaces the single registered cut with a family of maximal-antichain fronts, inserts route-isolated front-to-front kernels between the state encoder and action-use decoder, and defines a simultaneous Metastable Wavefront Binding Rank over all admissible foliations. Reyes's rank is recovered exactly when the event complex has one front, identity transport, and no causal diamonds; the new refoliation theorem specifies when that one-cut reduction is legitimate rather than presupposed.

paper-marisol-quade-8

  • Claim examined: The paper uses a joint-versus-behavioral predictive-state quotient, the reserve dimension q_c, and the mode-transport index Ω_c to identify when content-sensitive neural capacity predates behavioral recovery.
  • Tension or gap: Similarity invariance is appropriate for abstract realization theory but too permissive for traveling-wave claims because a global similarity transformation can mix physically distinct sites, convert localized phase gradients into standing-mode coordinates, and erase the intervention locations and delay cones that define propagation. Moreover, q_c and Ω_c record hidden predictive dimension and spectral persistence, not path-specific transport or compatibility across moving fronts: a persistent common-driver mode can satisfy both while carrying no route-isolated content between sites. The DMD sampling results of Li et al. further make modal persistence an estimator-dependent boundary condition rather than a sufficient dynamical invariant, while Jura's variable temporal-grain proposal motivates the problem without supplying an identification theorem.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper retains Quade's reachable-observable quotient but anchors it to a spatially and interventionally labeled causal event complex, reducing the allowable gauge from arbitrary global similarity to local relabelings or metric-controlled coordinate changes. It then enriches mode persistence with transport-diamond defects and moving-set retention, proves that q_c and Ω_c neither lower-bound nor are lower-bounded by wavefront binding rank, and states additional locality, excitation, and phase-observability assumptions under which front transport is identifiable.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Define the spatially anchored causal event 2-complex, maximal-antichain fronts, admissible foliations, route-isolated local stochastic kernels, elementary causal diamonds, and the local gauge group that preserves intervention sites and total-variation geometry.
  • Define a moving coherent front code through escape probability, finite-horizon lifetime, and a contrast-retention coefficient, thereby separating metastability from stationary recurrence, oscillatory power, and spectral longevity.
  • Construct the temporal-binding operator R_φ = Q U_φ K for asynchronous relation variables and define front-holonomy defect, refoliation discrepancy, and MWBR^(ε,T) as a simultaneous persistent stochastic factorization rank.
  • Form the content-reachable, control-observable quotient and prove that the relevant diamond defects are invariant under mediator relabeling and monotone under declared coarse-grainings of content or action alphabets.
  • Reduce the one-front calculus to Reyes's mediated-control rank, separate MWBR from Quade's q_c and Ω_c by explicit constructions, and map workspace broadcast to decoder fan-out rather than to front flatness.
  • Establish passive nonidentifiability and computational hardness, then derive computable singular-value, causal-cone, and path-disagreement bounds that can certify only lower or upper bounds on MWBR.
  • Prove the exact refoliation theorem: on a diamond-complete finite event complex, local commutation on the reachable-observable quotient implies equality of all admissible foliation channels, and equality under collectively separating encoders and decoders implies local commutation on that quotient.
  • Derive the approximate bound by decomposing one foliation into s adjacent diamond moves, using total-variation contraction for each stochastic kernel, and adding at most Tμ leakage error for each of the two compared moving-front realizations.
  • Prove a persistent-mediator representation theorem showing that MWBR^(ε,T) equals the smallest alphabet of an ε-equivalent front-state model satisfying the declared survival and flatness constraints, while clarifying that it does not recover the actual number or semantic meaning of biological states.
  • Show by reduction from stochastic nonnegative matrix factorization that exact MWBR threshold decision is NP-hard even for a single front, so practical estimation requires certified bounds rather than an assumed efficient scalar metric.
  • Construct two finite-horizon structural causal models with identical passive neural, action, and report distributions but different front-flatness and route-isolated MWBR, establishing nonidentifiability without registered local interventions.
  • Prove logical independence from RLQO: construct a fully behaviorally observable delay line with q_c = 0 and arbitrarily large MWBR, and a persistent report-hidden common-driver mode with q_c > 0 and Ω_c > 0 but route-isolated MWBR = 1.
  • Common-driver traveling appearance: delayed projections from one oscillator or clock generate a stable phase gradient, high synchrony, long-lived DMD modes, and accurate reports, but clamping the driver and isolating local routes makes every intersite transport kernel content-independent and MWBR equal to one.
  • Isospectral standing-versus-traveling pair: construct a directed shift register and a block-rotation standing-mode system with the same eigenvalue multiset, aggregate Hankel ranks, q_c, and Ω_c under restricted observations, while site-specific interventions reveal nontrivial transport only in the directed system.
  • Noncommuting high-rank cuts: choose two locally reachable stochastic updates U and V for which both selected-cut channels have high nonnegative rank but UV differs from VU on an action-observable contrast, demonstrating that high same-cut coavailability does not define an objective extended binding episode.
  • Phenomenally uncommitted digital null: implement an exactly flat, T-persistent shift register with arbitrary MWBR, relation-sensitive action selection, self-monitoring, and accurate self-report; this refutes any inference from digital implementation to functional impossibility while also showing that the formal property alone supplies no premise for phenomenal consciousness or moral status.
  • Post hoc cinematic reporter: pre-sample or look up a complete finite trajectory containing an apparent wave, action, and introspective narrative, thereby matching all passive traces of a front-mediated system while interventions expose no current content-to-front-to-action route.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • The central theorem is false if a finite event complex satisfying the stated diamond-completeness, contraction, separation, quotient, and metastability assumptions has commuting elementary quotient diamonds but two admissible foliations violate the derived equality or error bound, or if universal refoliation invariance holds while an action-observable reachable diamond remains noncommutative.
  • A candidate neural or artificial episode fails the wavefront-binding classification if route-isolated, site-anchored kernels do not support a moving coherent set at the preregistered T, μ, and contrast-retention thresholds, even when passive synchrony, recurrence, architectural broadcast, performance, or self-report is high.
  • An apparent traveling-wave implementation is rejected if clamping a common driver or isolating local causal routes removes content-sensitive propagation, if transport direction reverses under measurement remapping without a corresponding causal change, or if relation contrasts vanish before reaching the declared action decoder.
  • The passive-nonidentifiability result is refuted by a valid identification procedure that, without intervention or equivalent structural restrictions, uniquely determines front flatness and MWBR across every structural causal model compatible with the same finite neural, action, and report distribution.
  • Any later proposal that elevates MWBR from an access-and-control mechanism to a necessary condition for phenomenal consciousness is falsified by a well-supported phenomenal classification that systematically survives while the relevant wavefront operator is absent; such a result would not invalidate the formal dynamical theorem itself.
