## Alternatives and Disconfirmation

- Reversible or adiabatic implementations may reduce local reset work by retaining correlated records or using side information; this challenges any universal per-operation energy claim but is accommodated only if the auxiliary information and free-energy resources are included in the boundary ledger.
- A system may consume large amounts of energy because it is inefficient, poorly cooled, redundantly encoded, or coupled to parasitic processes, so high power is not positive evidence of recurrent access or phenomenal consciousness.
- A feedforward controller may achieve accurate current-state control without a loop-return path, while dreams, passive experiences, or report-hidden conscious states might be phenomenal without action-directed recurrent access; the frontier therefore measures a restricted functional capacity and is neither necessary nor sufficient for all proposed forms of phenomenality.
- A highly predictable environment can make a stale cache appear accurate and energetically efficient, so observational performance must be contrasted with synchronized innovations that are conditionally unpredictable from the past.
- Biological embodiment, homeostasis, affect, or cellular organization might contribute to phenomenal consciousness in ways not captured by the frontier, but that possibility would be an additional bridge or substrate hypothesis rather than a consequence of energy accounting alone.
- The formal reset proposition is falsified by a physically and mathematically complete counterexample that satisfies route isolation, the K-way error requirement, finite degenerate memory, isothermal local reset, no correlated reset side information, and no work recovery, yet achieves W_reset/(k_B T) below the stated conditional Fano bound after all physical resources are counted.
- The resource-accounting claim is falsified if a finite-memory recurrent controller can indefinitely process interventionally unpredictable distinctions at the stipulated reliability and latency while incurring neither the bounded reset or free-energy cost nor accumulating, exporting, preserving, or externally sourcing corresponding information and nonequilibrium resources under a closed boundary audit.
- The claimed independence from existing descriptors is falsified if mediated-control rank, recurrence topology, and recovery-linked predictive invariants are proved to determine the same budgeted frontier for every admissible physical realization, or if the proposed frontier changes under a mere latent-coordinate relabeling after the Hamiltonian, noise, dissipation, and control metrics have been transformed consistently.
