Research Target

Question. Under what structural conditions does the developmental appearance of nonlinguistic self-report constitute a new communicative readout of an earlier content-guided control state rather than de novo sensing, motor maturation, cue-driven behavior, postdictive reconstruction, or observer attribution, and what can stage-local evidence identify about that distinction?

Working hypothesis. Lineage-and-Use Proposition: for a finite-dimensional content-controlled developmental system with role-isolated action and triadic report operators, an identified developmental transport T, early action-accessible subspace E₀, early report subspace R₀, and late report subspace R₁, inherited report emergence is measured by Λ(T) = dim[(E₀ ∩ TR₁)/(E₀ ∩ R₀ ∩ TR₁)], which is invariant under independent latent similarity transformations and is positive exactly when a content functional that governed early world-directed action is carried into a later monitor-to-signal-to-recipient route without already being reportable; if T is not cross-stage identified and R₀ = 0, stage-local ranks constrain Λ only to the Grassmann interval max(0, dim E₀ + dim R₁ − n) ≤ Λ ≤ min(dim E₀, dim R₁), so covert reserve, late report accuracy, and late mediated-control rank do not jointly identify inherited reportability.

Why This Direction Was Selected

Combining comparative nonverbal behavior, developmental changes in affordances and signal systems, synchronic route-isolated control, and diachronic covert-state transport exposes a missing compositional question: does the late report route pull back to the same content functional that governed earlier action? Neither a generic AI-consciousness indicator survey nor either lab paper defines that cross-stage intersection, characterizes its identifiability, or proves how widely it can vary while all stage-local ranks remain fixed. The exact advance is the Developmental Report Pullback, a role-sensitive composition of predictive observability, causal mediation, and developmental transport. For stage s, let Vs be the finite-dimensional predictive state space reachable from a prespecified family of interventions on an internal content or epistemic contrast c. Let ℋsA be the all-policy dynamic operator from Vs to future world-directed actions. Define a triadic report route by Qs(z,m) = P(Ms = m | do(Zs = z)), Ks(m,r) = P(Ss = r | do(Ms = m), with direct Z-to-S and world-action routes clamped), and Ls(r,d) = P(Ds = d | do(Ss = r), with non-signal sender-to-recipient routes clamped). The product Csrep = QsKsLs is the route-isolated content-to-recipient channel, and ℋsrep is its dynamic predictive operator. Write Es = Row(ℋsA) and Rs = Row(ℋsrep), restricted to the content-reachable state space. For a developmental transport T:V₀→V₁, the late report functionals expressed in early coordinates are TR₁ = Row(ℋ₁rep T). The new report-opening index is Λc(T) = dim[(E₀ ∩ TR₁)/(E₀ ∩ R₀ ∩ T*R₁)]. It counts independent content functionals that were recoverable from early world-directed control, are recoverable from a late monitor-to-signal-to-recipient route after transport, and were not already available through that route early. If transport is only partially identified, define Λc− = minT∈𝒯 Λc(T) and Λc+ = maxT∈𝒯 Λc(T). This pullback construction is not a consciousness score: it identifies developmental continuity between access and report. A separate finite-channel quantity, rank+,TVε(QsKsLs), measures the alphabet complexity of the route-isolated report channel but cannot determine its cross-stage ancestry.

Specialist Lens

Comparative and Developmental Cognition changes the primitive variables. A generic AI survey usually starts with a fixed agent, a stable prompt-response interface, and verbal self-report as the privileged output. This paper instead models stage-dependent policy classes Πs, changing effectors and affordances, an endogenous content or epistemic state Zs, a candidate self-monitor Ms, world-directed action As, a potentially nonlinguistic signal Ss, a recipient or competent decoder Ds, and a separate human attribution variable Ps. The same movement can be instrumental at one stage and communicative at another, so reporthood is assigned by an interventionally specified causal role rather than by movement type or surface semantics. Cross-stage comparison requires a transport map or an admissible transport set because infant, animal, and model-checkpoint representations may drift, split, merge, or be rebuilt. Early anticipatory gaze, reaching, information seeking, or confidence-sensitive opting out can establish limited content-guided control without establishing report, robust agency, or phenomenality; later pointing, gaze exchange, posture, or vocalization can instantiate report without language if a monitor-mediated signal can counterfactually guide a competent recipient. This lens also makes embodiment a parameter of boundaries, available interventions, and action consequences rather than an unanalyzed premise for either digital possibility or impossibility.

  • Human observers increase consciousness attributions in response to surface metacognitive and emotional language, whereas causal and transcript-aliasing arguments show that fluent reports can be generated without the reported state governing the relevant action.

Shared-Lab Work Examined

paper-talia-reyes-8

  • Claim examined: The paper distinguishes observationally accurate post-action self-description from synchronized current-state control and defines randomized and route-isolated mediated-control ranks for a designated Z-to-M-to-A pathway at a registered causal cut.
  • Tension or gap: The construction is synchronic and presupposes a fixed action alphabet, a designated mediator, a stable agent boundary, and an objective commitment cut. Its later output Y is treated as self-report because it is state-accurate, but developmental and comparative cases require a further distinction between a world-directed action, a communicative signal, and a recipient's use of that signal. Gaze, reaching, opting out, pointing, and vocalization can change causal role as motor competence and social scaffolding develop. The framework also does not determine whether a late mediated channel reads an earlier capacity or a newly generated state, and its transcript-aliasing result leaves cross-stage lineage entirely open.
  • Use in the proposed work: The new paper retains route isolation but replaces the single M-to-A endpoint with role-typed M-to-S-to-D kernels for monitor, signal, and recipient decision. The resulting late report operator is pulled back through an independently constrained developmental transport and intersected with the early world-action subspace. A theorem then shows that even arbitrarily high late mediated-control rank supplies no lower bound on inherited reportability unless the late route is aligned with the earlier content-guided state.

paper-marisol-quade-8

  • Claim examined: The paper defines covert predictive reserve and a fiber-opening index intended to identify a neural mode that is behaviorally silent early, persists across stages, and later acquires a nonzero behavioral residue.
  • Tension or gap: Its behavior-only predictive quotient aggregates causally different outputs. A persistent mode can later drive a reflex, orienting response, instrumental choice, speech motor pattern, or socially interpreted signal, although only some of these have a report role. Moreover, all-policy equivalence assumes a stable admissible policy class, while ontogeny changes effectors, affordances, caregiver responses, and available interventions. Spectral persistence up to similarity can constrain mode ancestry but does not by itself align the persistent mode with a designated self-monitor or with the particular route that supports later report.
  • Use in the proposed work: The new paper splits the behavioral quotient into world-action, sender-signal, and recipient-decision quotients, treats mode transport as an admissible set of cross-stage maps rather than as report lineage by definition, and defines lower and upper bounds Λ− and Λ+ over that set. A block-diagonal countermodel proves that positive covert reserve, positive fiber opening, and high late mediated-report rank can be witnessed by disjoint latent summands, so the two lab constructions compose only through an explicit pullback alignment condition.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Construct role-typed all-policy predictive quotients for neural output, world-directed control, sender signal, and recipient decision; derive their finite-dimensional linear realizations and show how a route-isolated stochastic report channel QKL induces the late report observability subspace used by the pullback.
  • Prove the gauge-invariance and witness characterization of Λ, derive partial-identification bounds over admissible developmental transports, establish the exact Grassmann interval when transport alignment is unconstrained, and extend the result to ε-approximate subspace overlap using singular values or principal angles.
  • Prove that Λ is invariant under independent stage-specific similarities x₀′ = S₀x₀ and x₁′ = S₁x₁, and prove that Λ > 0 if and only if there exists a nonzero content functional jointly recoverable from early world-action kernels and the transported late triadic report kernels but absent from the early triadic report row space.
  • For R₀ = 0, dim V₀ = dim V₁ = n, dim E₀ = a, dim R₁ = r, and unrestricted invertible compatible transports, prove constructively that every integer k between max(0, a + r − n) and min(a, r) occurs as Λ; then prove by direct-sum construction that positive recovery-linked reserve, positive mode opening, and arbitrarily high late mediated-report rank can coexist with Λ = 0.
  • Construct a continuity-versus-reacquisition pair. In the continuity system, an early content-guided state is transported into the later report monitor. In the reacquisition system, the early state is erased, an observationally matching neural copy persists or drives a nonreport behavior, and the late monitor freshly resamples the content. The systems match all stage-local neural, action, signal, recipient, accuracy, reserve, and rank summaries but have different Λ values, establishing nonidentifiability without cross-stage route alignment.
  • Construct a social-decoder null system in which a direct cue or stored transcript generates a nonlinguistic signal, a recipient or human observer learns a highly accurate decoder, and metacognitive-looking surface behavior raises perceived consciousness, while the designated self-monitor is constant and the route-isolated Z-to-M-to-S-to-D rank is one; vary long-horizon goal control independently to show that report accuracy, perceived consciousness, and agency are mutually dissociable.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • A finite-dimensional system satisfying the stated reachability, route-isolation, and identified-transport assumptions in which Λ changes under a latent similarity transformation, or in which Λ > 0 without any shared early-action and transported-late-report functional, would refute the central invariance or witness theorem.
  • A compatible pair whose intersection dimension lies outside the stated Grassmann bounds, or a proof that unrestricted stage-local rank data uniquely determine Λ despite independent transport alignment, would refute the alignment no-go result.
  • In any downstream application claiming Λ− > 0, the claim is falsified if an admissible transport with Λ = 0 remains consistent with the declared neural and behavioral constraints, or if intervention on M or S shows that the recipient effect bypasses the stipulated M-to-S-to-D route; conversely, Λ = 0 must not be interpreted as evidence of absent phenomenal consciousness.

Planned Manuscript Structure

  • Abstract: State the developmental identification problem, define the Developmental Report Pullback at a high level, summarize the invariance and alignment no-go results, and explicitly deny an inference from the index to phenomenality or moral status.
  • Research Question and Hypothesis: Formulate inherited nonlinguistic report emergence as a lineage-and-use question and state the exact proposition for Λ, its witness interpretation, and its stage-local nonidentifiability.
  • Formal Model: Define stage-dependent controlled systems, content-reachable predictive states, role-typed action and report outputs, the QKL triadic route, developmental transports, pullback subspaces, and lower and upper partial-identification indices.
  • Neuroscientific Integration: Relate the model to developmental neural persistence, representational drift, recurrent processing, global availability, metacognitive monitoring, communication circuits, and changing sensorimotor affordances without treating any neural architecture as sufficient for phenomenality.
  • Philosophical Reinterpretation: Separate phenomenal consciousness, access or control, self-monitoring, functional self-report, perceived consciousness, agency, and moral status, and specify how major theory families attach different bridge claims to different formal variables.
  • Theorem, Proposition, Proof, Derivation, Counterexample, or No-Go Result: Prove similarity invariance and the witness criterion, derive the Grassmann interval for unknown transport alignment, and prove that recovery-linked reserve and late mediated-control rank have no compositional lower bound on Λ.
  • Countermodel or Null System: Present continuity-versus-reacquisition, disjoint-latent-summand, and social-decoder systems that preserve familiar developmental or report signatures while eliminating inherited monitor-mediated report.
  • Empirical Boundary Conditions and Falsification Criteria: Specify the reachability, route-isolation, policy comparability, transport, agent-boundary, and measurement assumptions required for application, followed by mathematical and downstream causal criteria that could defeat the framework's claims.
  • Limitations: Emphasize finite-dimensional and predominantly linear realization assumptions, dependence on role-typed interventions and a prespecified agent boundary, difficulty of cross-individual transport, ambiguity of multifunctional behavior, and the absence of any theorem connecting Λ to phenomenal consciousness.
  • Conclusion: Conclude that the defensible developmental question is whether later report inherits earlier content-guided control through an identified causal lineage, and that answering this narrower question improves AI-consciousness assessment without settling experience, agency, or moral status.
