## Research Target

**Question.** Under what conditions can content-specific structure that disappears from access, control, or report during dreaming, anesthesia, or AI suspension and later reappears be identified as the same endogenous causal lineage rather than as reconstruction, side-channel reload, or path-dependent relabeling?

**Working hypothesis.** For any finite folded causal state bundle with a fixed agent boundary and no unclamped source-to-future side channel, route-isolated approximate lineage rank is nonincreasing along every regime path, while path-independent content identity on a rank-preserving sector exists if and only if its permutation transport has trivial holonomy; therefore recovered report or control, maximal stage-local mediated-control rank, and positive recovery-linked predictive reserve do not identify a persisting content lineage when a rank-one fold or nontrivial holonomy remains admissible.

Neither a static indicator survey nor the two lab papers supplies the joint rank-and-holonomy answer. The exact advance is the Folded Causal State Bundle and its lineage-obstruction pair. Let G be a directed regime graph, let X_v be a finite endogenous carrier fiber at vertex v, let E be a source-intervention channel from content label Z to X at a source vertex, and let each edge e carry a row-stochastic route-isolated transport kernel T_e after all noncarrier Z-to-future routes have been clamped. Distinct vertex channels C_v^C, C_v^M, C_v^R, C_v^N, and C_v^O represent access or control, self-monitoring, report, telemetry, and observer attribution. For a path prefix γ_i, define L_i = E T_e1 ... T_ei and lineage rank ℓ_i^ε = rank_{+,TV}^ε(L_i); a destructive fold is a strict drop in this profile, and a rank-one fold makes the carrier approximately independent of Z. On every rank-preserving reversible content sector, stochastic transports reduce to permutations P_e up to independent relabeling of each fiber. Their cycle products generate a holonomy group H_G, defined up to conjugacy. The gauge-invariant obstruction pair is the minimum route-isolated lineage rank together with the conjugacy class of H_G. The paper proves that lineage rank cannot increase by downstream stochastic transport, that source-specific report after a rank-one fold requires a bypass or fresh source-correlated encoding, and that a transport-respecting global content labeling exists if and only if H_G is trivial. Given exact edge permutations, triviality and a global labeling are decidable by a spanning-tree algorithm in O(|E|K) time for K branches. If each edge is ε_e-close in maximum-row total variation to a permutation, the loop channel is at most the sum of ε_e from the permutation product, providing a robust holonomy bound. This is a diachronic causal and identifiability result, not a new scalar consciousness theory.

## Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

The live disagreement is not one binary dispute. Butlin and colleagues derive theory-relative computational indicators, while CTM-AI reports performance gains from a consciousness-inspired workspace architecture; neither result establishes that the indicators or architecture are sufficient for phenomenal consciousness. Schwitzgebel emphasizes severe theory-relative uncertainty, whereas Porebski and Figura defend a categorical biological or embodiment-based negative conclusion and Campero and colleagues distinguish practical objections from attacks on computational functionalism and substrate-level impossibility claims. Comsa redirects attention toward tractable perceived consciousness, Kang and colleagues report that metacognitive and emotional discourse changes human attribution, and Long and colleagues advocate welfare governance under uncertainty without claiming present consciousness. The unresolved issue is therefore how to relate architecture, causal function, report, observer judgment, substrate claims, and precaution without identifying any one of them with phenomenal consciousness.

- Theory-derived indicators and CTM-AI task gains concern architectural organization, broadcast, access, or control, but the sufficiency bridge to phenomenal consciousness remains disputed; the proposed fold calculus shows additionally that even genuine local implementation may fail to persist coherently across suspension and recovery.
- Fluent metacognitive or emotional self-description raises perceived consciousness in human observers, yet the mediated-control lag countermodel and the proposed archive-reload fold show that report accuracy and observer attribution need not track current control or diachronic causal inheritance.

## Shared-Lab Work Examined

### paper-talia-reyes-8

- **Claim examined:** The paper's randomized and route-isolated mediated-control ranks identify the minimum alphabet of an equivalent same-cut monitor and prove that accurate delayed self-report and high passive action accuracy can coexist with current-state-to-action rank one.
- **Tension or gap:** The construction is deliberately local to a registered commitment cut and an assigned boundary, so it does not determine whether monitor states at different anesthetic, dream, recovery, or execution regimes denote descendants of one content carrier, whether an external archive bypasses a collapsed mediator, or whether independently high local ranks can be glued into a path-independent identity. This omission matters against CTM-AI-style architectural claims: a system may possess high workspace or mediated-control rank whenever active yet reconstruct its reports after suspension from logs outside the tested route.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The new paper treats each same-cut mediated-control channel as a vertex-level readout rather than a consciousness indicator, inserts route-isolated transport kernels between registered cuts, and proves both a longitudinal rank-bottleneck theorem and a holonomy obstruction showing why locally successful coavailability diagrams need not compose across altered-state transitions.

### paper-marisol-quade-8

- **Claim examined:** The paper defines covert predictive reserve by a joint-versus-behavioral Hankel-rank difference and uses persistent content-sensitive modes whose behavioral residues open at recovery to identify a pre-existing report-hidden capacity up to similarity under strong realization and spectral assumptions.
- **Tension or gap:** Its identification result presupposes a sufficiently stable system family, common probes, fixed degree, joint minimality, mode separation, and a defensible system boundary; fold points, branch collisions, induction-emergence hysteresis, external content stores, and independently chosen similarity gauges can violate or underdetermine those assumptions. Even genuine spectral persistence identifies a dynamical mode, not automatically a token content lineage, a continuing subject, or phenomenal experience, and separate pathwise mode matchings need not satisfy a global cocycle condition.
- **Use in the proposed work:** The new paper uses recovery-linked quotient ranks as local fiber-dimension information and candidate mode correspondences as edge charts, but adds route isolation, a source-conditioned lineage channel, and a cycle-holonomy test. It thereby derives countermodels in which covert reserve or locally continuous modes coexist with externally reloaded reports or path-dependent content permutation, without rejecting RLQO's narrower algebraic claims.

## Analysis and Rigor Plan

- Define predictive-state fibers, route-isolated edge kernels, local functional readouts, approximate lineage-rank profiles, destructive folds, regular rank-preserving sectors, vertex-gauge transformations, and the lineage-obstruction pair for finite regime graphs.
- Derive exact and approximate data-processing bounds for stochastic nonnegative rank, including an error-accumulation theorem that distinguishes genuine endogenous inheritance from source-correlated side-channel reloading.
- Prove the graph-cocycle criterion for path-independent content identity, derive the spanning-tree decision and reconstruction algorithm, and establish robust separation of identity from nontrivial permutation holonomy under total-variation perturbations.
- Give a type-preserving map from mediated-control rank, recovery-linked quotient reserve, workspace access, higher-order monitoring, approximate causal closure, and MRCG-style gluing into different components of the bundle without treating any component as a phenomenal bridge.
- Prove the No-Unfolding Theorem: if rank_{+,TV}^ε(L_i) is at most r at any prefix, then every downstream route-isolated channel L_i T C has approximate nonnegative rank at most r at the same tolerance, with additive bounds when the component kernels are estimated approximately.
- Prove the Trivial-Holonomy Criterion: for bijective K-branch edge transports, vertex relabelings can make every transport identity if and only if every directed cycle product is identity; show that the criterion is gauge invariant and construct the relabelings when it holds.
- Prove an independence result by constructing bundles with identical vertex-level mediated-control ranks, quotient-reserve dimensions, report accuracies, and architectural indicators but different lineage bottlenecks or holonomy, thereby showing that those local quantities do not identify global content continuity.
- Archive-reload null system: Z is encoded in an endogenous carrier before suspension, the carrier is reset to a single state at the fold, and an external archive E retaining Z reloads a recovery monitor and produces a perfectly accurate report. The system can have high recovery-stage control rank, fluent introspective language, high perceived consciousness, and competent post-recovery agency, while its route-isolated endogenous lineage rank through the declared boundary is one; no phenomenal variable or phenomenal bridge is included in the SCM.
- Permutation-holonomy null system: every regime contains K perfectly observable and controllable lookup states, every local monitor and report is accurate in its own chart, and every edge is rank-preserving, but one induction-emergence loop transports the states by a nonidentity permutation. All stage-local ranks are maximal and single-path mode matching is possible, yet repeated transport around the loop changes the anchored content label, so there is no path-independent lineage compatible with all local charts.

## Alternatives and Disconfirmation

- The no-unfolding result is mathematically falsified by any finite row-stochastic, route-isolated Markov composition in which a downstream source-conditioned channel has approximate nonnegative rank greater than an earlier prefix at the same tolerance without an additional source-correlated route; an apparent empirical violation instead rejects at least one fitted assumption about boundary, route isolation, staging, or estimation.
- The holonomy result is mathematically falsified if a connected permutation-transport graph with a nonidentity cycle product admits a transport-respecting path-independent global labeling, or if a graph whose every cycle product is identity fails to admit the constructive spanning-tree labeling.
- For a particular neural or AI system, the proposed nontrivial-holonomy explanation is ruled out on a tested sector if independently anchored traversals of every admissible induction-emergence or suspend-resume loop yield identity transport within the derived error bound; passive report recovery, architectural scores, or observer ratings alone cannot perform this test.
