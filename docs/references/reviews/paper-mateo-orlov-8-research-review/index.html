Research Target

Question. Under what dynamical, spectral, and topological conditions can a finite-lived neural wavefront define a clock-gauge-invariant, path-independent operator that binds spatially distributed and objectively asynchronous events into one temporally thick token?

Working hypothesis. Within the explicitly axiomatized class of nonconstant, timing-faithful, front-local, dynamically retained operators, an exact metastable wavefront binding operator exists on support W if and only if the propagation-delay 1-cochain on W is exact up to the local-clock gauge and the comoving transfer operator has an isolated front-supported metastable resonance; for an approximate implementation of L propagation steps, capture width σ, resonance decay κ, normalized spectral remainder q < 1, and cohomological frustration h(W), the binding defect obeys D_L ≤ C_h h(W)/σ + M/[L(1−q)] + κL and D_L ≥ c_h h(W)/σ, yielding an optimal temporal thickness L* approximately equal to sqrt(M/[κ(1−q)]) when this scale lies within the wavefront lifetime.

Why This Direction Was Selected

The exact advance is the definition and analysis of a Metastable Wavefront Binding Operator rather than a new scalar consciousness score. Let G be the active directed neural graph, W a candidate wavefront support, T_F the front period or episode scale, and τ in C¹(W; R/T_F Z) the edge-delay cochain. Local clock changes c in C⁰(W; R/T_F Z) act by τ → τ + dc. Define the obstruction h(W) = inf_a ||wrap(τ−da)||∞, where a is a candidate arrival-time chart; h(W) is gauge invariant and vanishes exactly when local front phases glue into a global causal-time coordinate. If A_a aligns event fields to that comoving coordinate and P_F is the comoving transfer operator with isolated resonance λ_F and Riesz projector Π_F, define B_F = A_a⁻¹ Π_F A_a. The operator is required to satisfy front locality, clock-gauge covariance, path-independent transport, temporal discrimination, causal retention, and projector-like tokenization. The main theorem will show that a nondegenerate exact operator satisfying these axioms exists precisely when h(W) = 0 and the relevant resonance is spectrally isolated; otherwise one must sacrifice path independence, timing fidelity, or dynamical robustness. For P_F = λ_F Π_F + R with ||λ_F⁻ᵏRᵏ|| ≤ Mqᵏ, the finite-horizon filter Π̂_L = L⁻¹Σ from k = 0 to L−1 of λ_F⁻ᵏP_Fᵏ has error at most M/[L(1−q)], which produces an explicit approximate-idempotence bound and, after adding unrenormalized decay κL and phase-chart error h(W)/σ, the lifetime–gap tradeoff in the hypothesis. The same construction yields an identifiability result: Π_F is uniquely determined as a contour functional of an identified interventional transfer operator, the arrival chart is only coordinate-defined up to gauge, and a degenerate resonant subspace has no identifiable privileged basis. This makes the broad MRCG notion of atlas obstruction mathematically concrete, interprets Jura's regional synaptic clocks as local sections that need not glue, and demonstrates that CCT-style closure and wavefront binding are logically independent. Kirilyuk's dynamic multivaluedness and permanent localization claims are not assumed because the retrieved work does not supply operational spectral or cohomological definitions capable of supporting its consciousness conclusions.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Define event fields on a delayed stochastic excitable graph, the local-clock gauge group, wavefront arrival charts, capture width, finite lifetime, comoving transfer dynamics, and the six nontriviality axioms for a temporal binding operator, including a timing-fidelity condition that excludes zero and constant maps.
  • Construct the delay-phase presheaf over overlapping wavefront patches, prove that its Čech or graph-cohomological obstruction reduces to h(W), and relate cycle holonomy to path dependence of event alignment.
  • Derive the Riesz-projector construction B_F = A_a⁻¹Π_FA_a, the finite-horizon spectral-filter error, the approximate-idempotence and composition defects, and the optimal lifetime–gap integration scale L*.
  • Establish an O(|V|+|E|) test for exact zero holonomy using a spanning tree and fundamental cycle basis, then give a fixed-parameter procedure for approximate h(W) indexed by cycle rank and admissible phase-winding range rather than making an unsupported global polynomial-time claim.
  • Prove an identifiability hierarchy separating quantities recoverable from the exact interventional transition operator, quantities defined only up to clock gauge or resonant-subspace automorphism, and quantities not recoverable from stationary covariance, power, or pairwise synchrony alone.
  • Existence/no-go theorem: prove that exactness of τ and isolation of the front resonance are sufficient for an admissible B_F, and that any timing-faithful, edge-consistent, path-independent binder induces an arrival chart and therefore forces every cycle holonomy to vanish.
  • Approximation theorem: under explicit Lipschitz capture, quasi-compactness, and phase-response nondegeneracy assumptions, derive both the upper defect bound C_h h(W)/σ + M/[L(1−q)] + κL and a holonomy-controlled lower bound c_h h(W)/σ.
  • Lifetime–gap proposition: minimize the derived dynamic defect over L, delimit the integer and finite-lifetime corrections to L*, and show that rapid front decay or a vanishing spectral gap eliminates any low-defect temporal-thickness interval even when synchrony is high.
  • Identifiability proposition: show that an isolated Riesz projector is uniquely recoverable from an identified transfer operator, that a connected exact arrival chart is unique only up to an additive global phase after gauge fixing, and that individual modes inside a degenerate resonant cluster are unidentifiable without additional symmetry-breaking structure.
  • Complexity result: prove linear-time detection of exact inconsistency on a finite graph and characterize the approximate circle-valued problem through winding sectors, including a fixed-parameter bound in cycle rank.
  • Construct zero, constant, and indiscriminate path-averaging operators on a frustrated cycle; these satisfy superficial invariance or idempotence despite nonzero holonomy and thereby demonstrate why timing fidelity and local edge consistency are indispensable assumptions of the no-go theorem.
  • Construct a finite-state excitable ring with a pulse that dies with small probability per circuit, giving exact phase holonomy and an isolated metastable front resonance; it realizes B_F while lacking self-indexing, semantic discrimination, flexible report, and global control, refuting every proposed implication from the binder to those consciousness-associated capacities without presuming that phenomenality itself has been disproved.
  • Compare a highly synchronized Kuramoto network with a propagating-front network matched for mean rate, oscillatory power, and pairwise phase locking; the former can score maximally on synchrony while lacking an oriented arrival chart and front-supported metastable projector, showing that synchrony is not temporal binding under the proposed definition.
  • Construct an autonomous, approximately closed chaotic recurrent network with high dimensionality and rich counterfactual responses but no isolated propagating mode; together with the excitable-ring model, this establishes logical independence between CCT-style causal closure and metastable wavefront binding.
  • Analyze two overlapping counterpropagating fronts whose projectors fail to commute; this supplies a segmentation counterexample in which each front separately defines a token but no path-independent single binder exists for their union.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • The core theorem is falsified by a valid construction, within the stated nonconstant, timing-faithful, edge-consistent, front-local operator class, of an exact gauge-covariant and path-independent binder on a support with demonstrably nontrivial delay holonomy, or by failure of the Riesz construction under its stated spectral assumptions.
  • The proposed neural interpretation is rejected if episodes independently classified as temporally integrated systematically lack both an isolated front-supported metastable mode and a low-obstruction arrival chart, or if phase-targeted perturbational influence is equally well explained after propagation direction and delay topology are destroyed while firing rates, spectra, and pairwise synchrony are preserved.
  • The structural bridge to conscious temporal unity is rejected if front-defined token membership and the derived defect D_L fail, within explicit equivalence margins, to account for temporal-order integration, multisensory binding windows, or perceptual segmentation better than matched synchrony, recurrence, and stationary-spectrum nulls; such a result would leave the mathematical operator intact but remove its consciousness-specific interpretation.
