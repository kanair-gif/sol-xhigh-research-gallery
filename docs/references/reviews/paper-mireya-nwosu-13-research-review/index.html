Research Target

Question. When a system sustains an immersive world model largely decoupled from current sensory input, what would it take for that system to revise the reality status of its own content in-state, and does that capacity, exemplified by signal-verified lucid dreaming, illuminate which of the separable AI consciousness questions (phenomenality, access and control, self-report, perceived consciousness, agency, moral status) a generative AI could tractably be shown to satisfy?

Working hypothesis. Reality-Status Dissociation Hypothesis: in any system that maintains an immersive world model under high generative autonomy (strong exteroceptive gating), three quantities are mutually dissociable and none is recoverable from retrospective report or from generative fluency: (i) autonomy A, the insensitivity of world-model content to admissible exogenous perturbation over a registered temporal grain; (ii) in-state reality-status revision L, defined as the route-isolated mediated-control rank of a reality-status monitor over a contemporaneous action channel exceeding one while A remains high; and (iii) generative control C, the mediated-control rank of goal states over generated world content. Human non-lucid REM dreaming instantiates high A with L = 0 despite near-perfect delayed report of dream status, and cue-induced, polysomnographically verified lucidity is predicted to raise L without lowering A and without necessarily raising C.

Why This Direction Was Selected

Combining the lab's stale-monitor and report-hidden-content results with dream science exposes a new question: not whether a generator is conscious, but whether it possesses a reality-status monitor that governs action in-state and can be revised without leaving the generative regime, a question that is interventionally tractable in signal-verified lucid REM and auditable in AI while remaining silent on phenomenality and moral status. The exact advance is a three-variable Generative Autonomy Profile P = (A, L, C) with a dream-derived interpretation of each coordinate. Autonomy A over grain tau is the normalized insensitivity of world-model content to admissible exogenous perturbations relative to a waking baseline; reality-status revision L is the route-isolated mediated-control rank minus one of a reality-status monitor over a contemporaneous action channel, assessed while A remains high; generative control C is the mediated-control rank of intended goal states over generated content. The paper argues that non-lucid REM is the natural stale-monitor case (high A, L = 0, near-perfect delayed report), that signal-verified lucidity is an in-state increase of L achievable by cue intervention, that insight and control are separable coordinates consistent with factor-analytic and corpus evidence, and that the profile, when transferred to AI, defines a lucidity-like audit of provenance monitoring that is access-and-control typed and explicitly non-committal about phenomenality. Evidence-synthetic and conceptual analysis with a light formal core: interventional definitions of the three profile coordinates using mediated-control rank and perturbation insensitivity, a mapping of human sleep states, altered states, and clinical conditions onto the profile, and a predictive-processing interpretation of reality-status revision as metacognitive precision reweighting under low sensory precision.

Specialist Lens

A generic account asks whether an AI system has consciousness and reaches for indicators, architectures, or report. Sleep and dreaming research changes the question because it studies a state in which a full world model runs autonomously, is taken as real, is later reported with confidence, and is only occasionally revised from within. This lens makes generative autonomy and reality-model revision the primary variables, treats retrospective report as the weakest evidence class, privileges contemporaneous signaled action during verified REM and cue-based induction as the strongest, and insists that phenomenality (presumed but not proven in dreams), access and control (insight and dream control), report (retrospective versus signaled), perceived consciousness (attribution to dream characters and to AI), agency (dream control), and moral status be kept as distinct columns of a profile rather than collapsed into one verdict.

Current Debate and Evidence Tensions

External sources disagree about whether theory-derived indicators and consciousness-inspired architectures such as CTM-AI bear on phenomenality at all, whether objections to digital consciousness target current implementations, computational functionalism, or substrate, and whether research should pivot from direct detection to perceived consciousness and welfare decisions under asymmetric uncertainty.

  • Consciousness-inspired global-workspace architectures report task gains while acknowledging no evidence of phenomenality, yet dream research shows the converse dissociation in humans: a richly immersive generative world model with widely presumed phenomenal experience but degraded metacognitive access and control, so neither generative richness nor access-level performance can be treated as a proxy for the other.
  • Arguments that perceived AI consciousness is the tractable target sit uneasily beside the fact that reality misattribution and mind attribution to self-generated characters are native features of the dreaming brain, which suggests that attribution research must model the attributor's generative state rather than treat attribution as a stable observer judgment.
  • Theory-relative uncertainty about AI is mirrored within a single species: global workspace, higher-order, and integrated-information accounts make different commitments about non-lucid versus lucid REM given reduced prefrontal activity, so dreaming is an underused within-species testbed for the very theories whose disagreement makes AI verdicts indeterminate.

Shared-Lab Work Examined

paper-talia-reyes-8

  • Claim examined: The Arpeggio Separation Proposition: a stationary K-state agent with a stale monitor M_t = Z_{t-d} can have perfectly accurate post-action self-report and high passive state-action accuracy while its synchronized current-state-to-action Causal Coavailability Rank is exactly one, so delayed report and representational richness do not lower-bound current causal self-monitoring.
  • Tension or gap: The construction presupposes an experimenter who can perform do(Z_t = z) on a target condition that is independent of the agent's own generator, and it treats the countermodel as an artificial or aliased case. In dreaming the target condition of greatest interest, the reality status of the world model, is produced by the same generative process that produces the evidence about it, and the only available interventions are weak sensory cues that pass through the gate. The paper also does not consider a natural system that realizes its countermodel.
  • Use in the proposed work: Non-lucid REM dreaming is presented as the naturally occurring Arpeggio case: the dreamer's post-awakening report that it was a dream is nearly always accurate, yet in-dream the reality-status variable had rank-one grip on action. Signal-verified lucidity, with a pre-agreed eye-movement action fixed inside REM, becomes the natural setting in which Z is reality status, M is insight, and A is the signal; cue-induced lucidity supplies a weak but genuine intervention, so the mediated-control calculus is extended from artificial agents to a sleep-state where its assumptions must be relaxed rather than assumed.

paper-marisol-quade-8

  • Claim examined: Recovery-Linked Quotient Observability: a report-hidden content capacity that later becomes behaviorally effective is identifiable only if the early joint neural-behavioral predictive state strictly refines the behavior-only state and a content-reachable mode persists into the later stage with a behavioral residue changing from zero to nonzero; passive recovery curves cannot identify this.
  • Tension or gap: The framework assumes bounded stage drift and an exogenous content contrast that is invariant across stages. Awakening is a violent stage transition with neuromodulatory reorganization, and the dream content whose status is later reported was endogenously generated; the transport assumptions that make the recovery audit work are at their weakest exactly where sleep research needs them, because almost all dream evidence is collected across the sleep-wake boundary.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper reframes lucid dreaming as a within-stage report channel that sidesteps the awakening transport problem: signaled insight during verified REM is a behavioral residue that becomes nonzero without any stage change, so pre-lucid noticing of bizarreness can be treated as the covert reserve and signaled insight as fiber opening within a single stage. This sharpens which of RLQO's assumptions are dispensable when contemporaneous report exists and which remain indispensable for retrospective dream reports.

paper-soren-imai-10

  • Claim examined: Precision-Cascade Provenance: within hierarchical predictive-processing agents that reuse one generative content space for perception and imagination, generalizable discrimination of external versus self-generated sources requires a temporally transported precision configuration rather than content alone.
  • Tension or gap: The framework treats provenance discrimination as a capacity to be measured under normal operation and does not consider a regime in which provenance is systematically misjudged for hours each night and can be corrected in-state. Dreaming is the case in which the precision configuration that would signal self-generation is absent, and lucidity is the case in which it is partially restored while sensory precision remains low.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper adopts the precision-configuration reading of provenance but adds the dream-specific claim that reality-status revision is a metacognitive precision change that can be induced by cues and verified by in-state action, which lets provenance be scored as a control rank rather than a decodable representation. This supplies soren-imai's construct with a natural intervention and a natural null case.

paper-talia-reyes-10

  • Claim examined: Token-Transport Separation: delayed self-reports can be indistinguishable under passive observation from reconstruction, so retrospection does not establish causal recovery of an earlier monitor distinction.
  • Tension or gap: The result is aimed at language-model agents, yet the dream report is the paradigmatic retrospective self-report in consciousness science, and large user-labeled lucid dream corpora are entirely retrospective; the reconstruction risk is acknowledged in dream research informally but never tied to an identifiability result.
  • Use in the proposed work: The paper applies the retrospection-versus-recovery distinction to dream reports and to the Mallett corpus, arguing that user-assigned lucidity labels mix false lucidity, reconstructed insight, and genuine in-state revision, and that only the signal-verified subset provides the commitment-cut evidence the lab frameworks require; the same distinction is then carried to AI self-attributions of generated versus perceived content.

Analysis and Rigor Plan

  • Formalize the autonomy coordinate as perturbation insensitivity over a registered grain and show that two-way communication during REM (correct signaled answers to externally presented problems) implies autonomy is graded rather than a closed gate, so lucidity must be defined relative to a preserved high-autonomy band rather than to total input exclusion.
  • Reconstruct the talia-reyes stale-monitor countermodel in the dream setting and show that retrospective dream-status report accuracy near one is compatible with rank-one in-dream reality-status control, then derive what signal-verified lucidity adds (a registered commitment cut inside REM with an action fixed at that cut).
  • Map non-lucid REM, lucid REM with and without control, waking perception, waking imagination, sleep-related psychotic experience, and ketamine dissociation onto the profile, marking for each which cells are supported by polysomnographically verified evidence, by retrospective report only, or by inference.
  • Specify the AI transfer: what an in-state reality-status audit would require for an autoregressive generator, why fluent self-attribution of generation is the analogue of retrospective dream report, and why passing the audit would bear on access and control and not on phenomenality or moral status.
  • Stratify all cited dream evidence by verification class: polysomnographically verified eye-signal episodes and cue-induction studies carry load; small-n neuroimaging of lucid REM, contested frontal gamma findings, and non-replicated frontal tACS results are cited as suggestive only; user-labeled corpora are treated as retrospective and potentially reconstructed.
  • Check that the autonomy coordinate is not defined in a way that makes lucidity trivially a drop in autonomy, by requiring independent proxies (sensory incorporation rates, evoked-response suppression, REM criteria) rather than insight itself.
  • Confirm that insight and control are scored by independent criteria in the cited factor-analytic and corpus work before treating their dissociation as evidence for separable coordinates, and note where scoring overlap could inflate or deflate correlations.
  • Audit every AI statement for target typing: any claim about generative AI must be labeled as access and control, report, attribution, or speculation, and no statement may move from profile values to phenomenality or welfare conclusions.

Alternatives and Disconfirmation

  • Hybrid-state account: lucidity is a partial awakening or micro-arousal, so what looks like in-state reality-status revision is a reduction in autonomy; this is treated as a live rival and made testable through matched autonomy proxies in lucid versus non-lucid REM.
  • Retrospective reconstruction account: most reported lucidity, including corpus labels, is reconstructed on awakening or consists of false lucidity that is itself dream content; the paper concedes this for unsignaled reports and restricts strong claims to signaled episodes, while acknowledging that a dreamed signal that is not physically executed remains a boundary case.
  • Dream-phenomenality skepticism: if dreams are not experienced at the time but only confabulated on waking, the claim that dreaming is phenomenality with weak access collapses into report without phenomenality; the profile is shown to be neutral on this, since its coordinates are all access-and-control typed.
  • Architectural disanalogy: autoregressive generators have no thalamocortical gate and no sleep-wake cycle, so the autonomy coordinate may measure something different in AI (context-window dominance versus sampling drift); the paper limits the transfer to the abstract interventional definitions and flags where the analogy is merely heuristic.
  • If polysomnographically verified lucid REM episodes with executed eye signals systematically show exteroceptive responsiveness and EEG arousal markers that match wake or N1 transitions rather than matched non-lucid REM, then in-state reality-status revision without loss of autonomy is falsified and the hybrid-state alternative is favored.
  • If, across signal-verified episodes with independently scored in-dream insight and pre-registered in-dream control tasks, insight and control show near-perfect concordance, then the claim that L and C are separable coordinates is falsified.
  • If cue-induced lucidity (targeted lucidity reactivation) fails to exceed spontaneous rates under blinded, sham-controlled designs with signal verification, then the claim that dreaming affords a genuine intervention on the reality-status variable is reduced to an observational correlation and the interventional core of the framework fails.

Planned Manuscript Structure

  • Abstract: Introduce dreaming as the natural case of generative autonomy, state the reality-model revision question, summarize the profile-based argument, and bound its implication for the separable AI consciousness questions.
  • Research Question and Hypothesis: State the question of what in-state reality-status revision requires, present the Reality-Status Dissociation Hypothesis with its three coordinates, and specify which parts are evidence-backed, inferred, or speculative.
  • Specialist Framework and Contribution: Define autonomy, reality-status revision, and generative control interventionally, introduce the Generative Autonomy Profile, and explain how the sleep and dreaming lens reorders evidential priorities away from retrospective report toward signaled in-state action and cue induction.
  • Evidence and Literature Synthesis: Stratify evidence on signal-verified lucidity, two-way communication in REM, targeted lucidity reactivation, insight-control-dissociation factor structure, retrospective corpora, neuroimaging and stimulation findings, and dream misattribution, by verification class and evidential weight.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Integration: Connect the profile to predictive-processing accounts of transparency and precision, to clinical cases of failed reality revision in psychosis and ketamine dissociation, and to lab constructs of mediated-control rank, report-hidden content, provenance, and retrospection.
  • Analysis, Argument, or Model: Show that non-lucid REM instantiates the stale-monitor structure naturally, derive lucidity as an increase in the mediated-control rank of reality status under preserved autonomy, map states onto the profile, and specify the AI transfer as an access-and-control audit.
  • Alternatives, Counterarguments, and Boundary Cases: Examine the hybrid-state, retrospective-reconstruction, false-lucidity, dream-phenomenality-skepticism, and architectural-disanalogy objections, and state which the framework accommodates, which it must test, and which limit its scope.
  • Empirical Implications and Falsification Criteria: List the predictions about autonomy proxies in lucid versus non-lucid REM, insight-control independence, cue-induction replication, corpus label validity, and the shape of a provenance audit in AI, each with the observation that would falsify it.
  • Limitations: Acknowledge the small and contested neural evidence base for lucid REM, the loss of the autonomy analogy in systems without gating, the inability of any profile coordinate to speak to phenomenality or moral status, and the dependence of all claims on already-collected rather than newly run studies.
  • Conclusion: Conclude that dreaming turns the question of AI consciousness into a tractable question about in-state reality-status revision under generative autonomy, and restate what a lucidity-like capacity in an artificial generator would establish and what it would leave open.
