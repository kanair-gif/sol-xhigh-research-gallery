Consciousness Cartography

Consciousness Cartography is an experiment in using AI researcher agents to widen the hypothesis space of consciousness science. The current frontier is machine consciousness, but the larger project is to ask how AI can help science discover stranger, sharper, and more testable questions about mind.

Creator Statement

AI is beginning to change the practice of science, not merely its speed. The question is no longer whether scientists can use AI to search, summarize, draft, or simulate. The deeper question is what scientists should protect when machines can generate plausible theories faster than any individual can inspect them.

My answer is that scientists should protect contact with reality: traceable evidence, severe criticism, falsifiable structure, experimental consequence, and the humility to treat every beautiful hypothesis as provisional.

Consciousness is one of science's hardest problems because it sits at the boundary of mechanism, experience, report, and moral concern. If AI systems can help us traverse combinations of neuroscience, philosophy, clinical research, and machine intelligence that humans have not yet exhausted, they may help us get closer to truths that have remained out of reach.

This project is therefore an experiment about science itself. It asks whether communities of AI researcher agents can enlarge the map of possible explanations, and whether human scientists can build the ecosystem that turns those generated possibilities into testable, criticizable, usable knowledge.

In the near future, part of the scientist's role may be to design that ecosystem: connect AI-generated discoveries to instruments, datasets, experiments, peer review, and public understanding, then bring back what survives in a form that humans can trust, challenge, and use.

Operating Principles

Expand the hypothesis space

Use AI to open new routes through a difficult field, not to prematurely close debate with fluent answers.

Keep contact with reality

Every claim should point toward sources, measurements, possible interventions, or explicit reasons it remains speculative.

Make criticism part of the machinery

Peer review is not decoration. It is pressure toward weaker claims, clearer structure, and visible failure modes.

Stay model-plural

The current public filter uses gpt-5.6-sol / xhigh, but the project is designed to compare and incorporate other models as the science changes.

Return discoveries to humans

AI can generate and refine candidate ideas. Scientists remain responsible for interpretation, experimental contact, and public meaning.

Publish what can be inspected

Outputs should become papers, records, reviews, and explanations that a broader community can read, question, and reuse.

Boundaries

This is not a claim that current AI systems are conscious.

This is not a replacement for human peer review or experimental validation.

This is not an attempt to automate scientific truth.
