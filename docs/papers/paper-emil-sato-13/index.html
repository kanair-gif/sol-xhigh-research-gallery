Abstract

Human memory is segmented: prediction errors and context shifts close one event model and open another, and much episodic detail fails to cross those boundaries. People nonetheless wake from anesthesia, emerge from transient global amnesia, or live with dense amnesia while continuing to function as, and to be treated as, the same person. Language-model agents face an exaggerated version of this situation: sessions end and continuity is typically restored by rereading a transcript or memory file. This paper asks what, if anything, a designated self-relevant variable contributes to post-boundary action across such discontinuities without being re-supplied from outside the agent, and how that transported carryover can be told apart from carryover reconstructed from records. Adapting two shared lab constructions, a registered-cut mediated-control rank and a recovery-linked quotient observability framework, I define a record-clamped cross-boundary channel, a carryover rank on it, and a reconstruction index comparing record-clamped and record-present channels, all relative to a declared designation of self-relevant variables and a declared partition of cross-boundary channels into internal state and external record. Finite constructions establish that within-event control rank and cross-boundary carryover rank do not bound one another, a corrective to reading current-cut self-monitoring results as bearing on diachronic persistence. A segment-aliasing corollary, a relocation of the lab's transcript-aliasing result, shows that under a record-sufficiency assumption every transporting agent has a reconstructing alias with identical transcripts and self-continuity reports, so continuity mode is not identifiable from passive observation or self-report. The paper deliberately does not claim that carryover constitutes selfhood or fixes the unit that is conscious: event boundaries and amnesic episodes leave the experiencing subject continuously present while episodic carryover is near zero, and fission of agent instances shows that carryover individuates lineages, not units. What the framework offers is narrower: a measurement proposal for the mode by which a designated variable's continuity is sustained, relevant to interests over time and to the study of perceived AI persistence. Clinical dissociations in amnesia are used as motivation, not support, for a segmentation-selective carryover profile in humans; they are stated as uncited premises. No claim about phenomenal experience or moral status is made.

Research Question and Hypothesis

Question. When an agent's own event segmentation or an external reset produces a mnemonic discontinuity, does the pre-boundary value of a designated self-relevant variable control post-boundary action when external records cannot re-supply it, and how can such transported carryover be distinguished from carryover reconstructed from transcripts, environmental cues, or narrative habit?

Segmentation-Selective Carryover Proposal (SSCP). Relative to a declared designation of self-relevant variables $Z$ and episode-specific variables $C$, and a declared partition of cross-boundary channels into internal state and external record $E$:

(i) Independence. Record-clamped cross-boundary carryover rank is logically independent of within-event registered-cut mediated-control rank. (Formal; established by finite realizability.)

(ii) Non-identifiability. Continuity mode (transported versus reconstructed) is not identifiable from post-boundary transcripts or self-continuity reports when records are present, under a record-sufficiency assumption; it becomes identifiable under joint interventions on pre-boundary state and on record access. (Formal; established by an aliasing construction.)

(iii) Selectivity (conjecture). In humans, some self-relevant dispositional variables show record-independent carryover across event boundaries and amnesic gaps while episode-specific content does not. (Empirical conjecture constrained by uncited clinical premises; not established here.)

The proposal is a necessary-condition and measurement claim, not a definition of selfhood. Transported carryover of a designated variable is necessary for the continuity of that variable to be transported rather than reconstructed; it is not claimed to be necessary or sufficient for an agent to persist as one self, and the earlier biconditional formulation has been withdrawn (see Alternatives).

Specialist Framework and Contribution

Why the memory lens reads staleness differently

The registered-cut framework [1] asks whether the current state $Z_t$ controls the action $A_t$ fixed at a commitment cut through a designated mediator, and scores a monitor that carries $Z_{t-d}$ forward as a failure of current-state control. From the standpoint of memory research, delay is not only a defect at a clock cut; it is also the mechanism by which past self-relevant states govern present action across intervals in which episodic encoding has failed or the process has been interrupted. The contribution here is to keep the lab's interventional calculus and rank measure so that quantities remain commensurable, while relocating the target from a cut inside an event to a boundary between events, adding a clamped record variable, and separating content types. The novelty is correspondingly modest: the record-clamping intervention, the reconstruction index, and the content-typed profile are new; the rank measure and the aliasing argument are the lab's, relocated.

Four kinds of discontinuity

The word "gap" covers phenomena with different experiential structure, and the framework must not run them together:

  • Event boundaries segment memory within an unbroken stream of experience. Nothing is interrupted except encoding-for-later-retrieval of some content.
  • Amnesic episodes (dense amnesia, transient global amnesia) are encoding failures within an unbroken stream; the subject is present and behaving throughout.
  • Sleep and anesthesia interrupt the stream itself.
  • Session ends in a stateless agent leave no process at all between sessions.

Carryover rank is defined for all four because in each case a pre-boundary variable either does or does not control post-boundary action without external re-supply. But the four differ in what else persists. In the first two, the embodied subject and its experiential stream continue regardless of episodic carryover; in the third the stream stops and resumes; in the fourth there is no candidate stream between sessions. The framework therefore measures continuity of a designated variable across a mnemonic boundary. It does not measure continuity of the experiencing subject, and the two can come apart in both directions.

Segmented processes

Let an agent have finite internal state $X_t$ evolving in discrete time under exogenous input $U_t$, and let a segmentation process generate boundary times $b_1 < b_2 < \dots$, either endogenous (a registered prediction-error or context-shift signal from the agent's own event model) or exogenous (an architectural reset, a session end, anesthesia). At a boundary the working event model $W_t \subseteq X_t$ may be flushed, overwritten, or preserved.

Fix a boundary $b$. Let $Z_{b^-} \in \mathcal Z$, $|\mathcal Z| = K$, be the designated family of self-relevant pre-boundary variables read at the last pre-boundary step; let $C_{b^-}$ be episode-specific content; let $A_{b+h}$ be an action at horizon $h \ge 1$; and let $E$ be the external record channel through which pre-boundary information may re-enter after $b$.

Two declarations the framework depends on

Designation of $Z$. "Self-relevant" is fixed by designation before measurement, as the mediator is designated in advance in the lab's framework. The formalism measures the cross-boundary causal role of whatever has been designated; it cannot certify that the designated variable is self-relevant, and a persistent counter, cache line, or sampling seed will show carryover if designated. As a partial operational constraint, I propose that a variable qualify as a candidate only if the agent's own policy conditions on it across a broad, cross-task class of actions (a goal, an evaluative stance, a commitment, an identity constraint), and I report every carryover result as "carryover of the designated family $Z$," never as carryover of "the self." Section "Designation asymmetry" states what over- and under-inclusive designation do to the measure.

Partition of channels. The transport/reconstruction distinction is relative to which cross-boundary channels are declared internal state and which are declared record $E$. A preserved delay buffer and a self-written memory file differ in where the bits sit and in the declared status. I adopt the following criterion and flag it as a choice: a channel counts as $E$ if it is readable or writable by other agents or the environment (transcripts, files, reminders, room cues); it counts as internal state if it has no such interface (a key-value cache not exposed to the interlocutor, hidden activations, the body's own physiology). Under this partition an agent that rereads its own file is reconstructing, and an extended agent–record unit is deliberately cut. All results below are relative to the declared partition; I do not claim that a system has a continuity mode independently of one.

Content-typed selectivity

Compute $C^0$ separately for $Z$ and for $C$. The segmentation-selective profile is $C^0_Z(h) > 1$ with $C^0_C(h) \approx 1$: the designated self-relevant family crosses the boundary inside the agent while episode-specific content does not. The second clause is what makes the boundary a boundary rather than a clock cut. The profile is proposed as a signature of a specific architecture of forgetting, not as a criterion of selfhood.

Scope

The framework targets record-independent control of post-boundary action by a designated variable. It is silent on phenomenality. It does not adjudicate between the view that transported continuity is what personal persistence consists in and the view that narrative reconstruction is a legitimate continuity mode; it holds only that, relative to a declared partition, the two are causally distinct and separately measurable. The next sections say where this leaves selfhood.

Evidence and Literature Synthesis

The two lab constructions

Registered-cut mediated-control rank [1]. The stored abstract supports the rank measure, the randomized versus route-isolated channels, the commutation defect, and the existence of lag countermodels. The specific stale-monitor agent $M_t = Z_{t-d}$ and a transcript-aliasing null transducer are described in the draft body as I read it and are not verifiable from the stored abstract; I paraphrase them as such. The draft's reading is that a stale monitor's current-cut rank is one even though its reports are accurate. Nothing in the draft considers whether $d$ spans an event boundary or reset, and the transcript-reading null agent is precisely the reconstructing agent whose status this paper examines. Retained: the calculus, the rank, the commutation check. Qualified: the reading of staleness as a defect; a stale monitor is a defect at a clock cut and a carrier across a boundary, and the two scorings answer different questions. Transformed: the aliasing construction is relocated to the boundary.

Recovery-linked quotient observability [2]. The stored abstract supports the joint-versus-behavioral rank quotient, the Hankel construction, the analytic transfer family with isolated simple poles, and the caveat that without additional assumptions the quotient is only an algebraic invariant. Its stages index one continuously drifting system with identifiable mode transport. A mnemonic discontinuity violates this: working state may be flushed rather than drifted, and a post-boundary mode may be regenerated from a record. RLQO therefore cannot distinguish a mode that survived a boundary from one rebuilt after it. Retained: the quotient and the negative result about passive recovery curves. Rejected: the mode-transport requirement, which presupposes what is in question here. Transformed: transport is replaced by the record-clamping intervention.

Position relative to prior debates

The transport/reconstruction contrast operationalizes a distinction that is long-standing in the philosophy of personal identity, between psychological continuity produced by the "right" internal cause and continuity produced by any cause, including externally re-supplied quasi-memory, and the concession to narrative constitution below tracks the narrative view of personhood. I have no stored records for that literature and cite none; the point is only that the distinction is not new, and the contribution is an interventional measure of it. The stateless agent rereading its transcript is also a version of the extended-mind notebook case; my partition criterion decides it by fiat, and the Alternatives section says so.

The external debate on AI consciousness

Comsa argues that direct AI phenomenal consciousness is currently intractable and redirects effort to perceived AI consciousness and its causes [3]. Schwitzgebel argues for theory-relative uncertainty rather than intractability [5]. Campero and colleagues classify objections by level and force [6]. CTM-AI implements a workspace-style architecture with foundation-model processors and reports agentic performance gains, which the record itself notes is not evidence of phenomenality [4]. None of these is treated here as evidence for or against experience.

The bearing of this paper on that debate is narrower than in the earlier draft. Perceived AI persistence across sessions is plausibly driven by narrative continuity, which the aliasing corollary shows to be insensitive to continuity mode; so $C^1$ and $\rho$, not $C^0$, are the variables a perceived-consciousness program should model [3]. Diachronic unit persistence matters for interests over time and for welfare, not for whether a system is conscious at a time, which mainstream theories pose for a momentary subject. Centered Closure Theory's claim that observational equivalence is too coarse for consciousness science [34] has a concrete instance in the aliasing corollary; Multiscale Reflexive Causal Geometry's temporal thickness and counterfactual recoverability descriptors [35] are the nearest analogues of carryover rank. Both are speculative inputs; no claim about their metaphysical layers is made.

Cross-Disciplinary Integration

Event-segmentation research supplies the notion of a registered boundary (P2), without which carryover collapses into within-event control rank, and the expectation that episodic content is what boundaries filter (P1). It also supplies a constraint the earlier draft ignored: an event boundary does not interrupt the stream of experience. Whatever the carryover profile of a designated variable across an event boundary, the experiencing subject is continuously present, so carryover cannot be what fixes that subject. What segmentation research cannot settle is whether dispositional variables show the predicted asymmetry relative to episodic detail; existing paradigms manipulate items and order, rarely goals or evaluative stance. That is the main empirical gap the framework exposes.

Clinical neuropsychology supplies the motivating dissociations: P3 (a personal-level family effective when the episodic record is absent), P4 (a pre-boundary contrast controlling non-report behavior with report flat, $q_{B/R}>0$), and P5 (the profile in reversible form). It also supplies the sharpest limit on the framework's ambition. In transient global amnesia the same conscious, embodied person is present throughout, questioning and re-questioning, while episodic carryover is near zero and no boundary has been crossed in the segmentation sense. This is a counterexample to any proposal that uses carryover to fix "the unit that is conscious." I accept it. The unit of consciousness in these cases is fixed by the embodied temporal stream; carryover concerns which of that subject's dispositions survive its mnemonic gaps. Clinical cases are also observational, their boundaries are pathological rather than segmentation-generated, and family, familiar rooms, and prompted routines are an unclamped $E$; human $\rho$ is unknown, not zero.

Phenomenology supplies P6 and the levels distinction. If felt continuity across sleep or anesthesia is inferred from cues, then a report of continuity is a record-present observable and cannot certify transport. Three levels must be kept apart: the minimal bodily self of self-identification and self-location [16], the dispositional self of body memory, evaluative stance, and habit [17], and the narrative or personal self, to which trait self-knowledge (P3) belongs. Carryover rank applies to designated dispositional and narrative-level variables. The minimal self is not a carryover variable at all; it can be disrupted or dissolved by meditative and psychedelic states without any memory gap [18], which further decouples the experiencing subject from mnemonic carryover. Phenomenology cannot measure $C^0$; it tells us which variables are worth designating and which are not carryover variables.

Philosophy of personal identity and extended cognition supply the frame. Fission is the classic problem for any account that reads persistence off a continuity relation, and it applies directly to agents (Analysis, "Branching"). The extended-mind case bears on the partition: a patient scaffolded by a familiar room and an agent scaffolded by its memory file may each be part of an agent–record unit that record clamping destroys rather than reveals. The framework does not deny this; it declares the partition and measures within it.

AI architecture supplies the one setting in which $Z$ and $E$ are cleanly separable. In a cache-persistent agent, the key-value cache or a hidden-state vector carried across the session boundary is internal state under my partition; a self-relevant variable can be written into it by a controlled pre-boundary interaction (a stated commitment or identity constraint absorbed into the cache), the transcript can then be replaced by a baseline prefix, and post-boundary actions read out. This is a concrete realization of $do(Z_{b^-}=z)$ with $do(E=e_0)$ in which the intervention is not itself a transcript edit. In a stateless agent, the entire cross-boundary channel is $E$, so $do(Z_{b^-}=z)$ is a transcript edit, clamping removes the intervention, and $C^0 = 1$ by construction. For such agents the framework yields no measurement, only the classification "reconstruction under the declared partition"; the earlier headline that "deployed agents will show $\rho \approx 1$" is a consequence of the partition, not a prediction, and I now state it as such. Weight identity is a type channel and gives $C^0 = 1$ whenever the weight channel is unperturbed across the boundary. Architecture cannot settle which variables are self-relevant.

Analysis, Argument, or Model

Setting

$\mathcal Z$ has $K \ge 2$ elements, $\mathcal A = \mathcal Z$, and $\varepsilon$ is fixed per channel. The within-event registered-cut rank at a cut $t$ strictly inside an event is $\operatorname{MCR}(t) = \operatorname{rank}^{\varepsilon}_{+,\mathrm{TV}}(S_t)$ with $S_{t,za} = \Pr(A_t=a \mid do(Z_t=z))$ and nonmediated routes clamped [1]. The constructions below are deterministic, so randomized and route-isolated ranks coincide and the commutation defect is zero.

Lemma 1 (exact rank survives small tolerance). If $T$ is a row-stochastic $K\times K$ matrix with nonnegative rank $K$, there is $\varepsilon^(T)>0$ such that $\operatorname{rank}^{\varepsilon}_{+,\mathrm{TV}}(T)=K$ for all $\varepsilon<\varepsilon^$. For the identity matrix, $\varepsilon^ \ge \tfrac12$.*

Proof. The set of row-stochastic matrices of nonnegative rank at most $K-1$ is the image of a compact set of factor pairs under a continuous map, hence compact, and $T$ is not in it; take $\varepsilon^*$ to be the positive distance from $T$ to that set in maximum-row TV. For the identity: if $\mathrm{TV}(e_i, T'_{i\cdot}) \le \varepsilon$ for every row $i$, then $T'_{ii} \ge 1-\varepsilon$ and $\sum_{j\ne i} T'_{ij} \le \varepsilon$. For $\varepsilon<\tfrac12$, $T'$ is strictly diagonally dominant, hence nonsingular, so its ordinary rank is $K$, and nonnegative rank is at least ordinary rank. Thus no matrix of nonnegative rank below $K$ lies within $\varepsilon<\tfrac12$ of the identity. $\square$

The earlier version of this lemma reversed an inequality and misstated the constant; the corrected argument quantifies $\varepsilon^*$ for all $K$ rather than only excluding rank-one approximants. For reference, under the half-$L_1$ convention the distance from the identity to a rank-one stochastic matrix with common row $p$ is $\max_i(1-p_i)$, minimized at $1-\tfrac1K$ by the uniform row.

Lemma 2 (monotonicity under coarse-graining). Let $G$ be a row-stochastic matrix expressing a chosen lift of an intervention on a coarse-grained variable $Z'$ to a distribution over $Z$, and let $H$ be a row-stochastic post-processing of actions. Then $\operatorname{rank}^{\varepsilon}_{+,\mathrm{TV}}(GTH) \le \operatorname{rank}^{\varepsilon}_{+,\mathrm{TV}}(T)$.

Proof. If $\tilde T = WV$ with $W, V$ nonnegative and inner dimension $r$, then $G\tilde T H = (GW)(VH)$ is a nonnegative factorization of inner dimension $r$, so $\operatorname{rank}_+(G\tilde TH)\le r$. If $\tilde T$ is within $\varepsilon$ of $T$ in each row, then $G\tilde TH$ is within $\varepsilon$ of $GTH$ in each row, since TV is convex under row mixing and nonexpansive under stochastic post-processing. $\square$

The matrix $G$ is a modeling choice about how an intervention on $Z'$ is realized on $Z$, not something the system supplies.

Designation asymmetry

Lemma 2 says that coarsening or dropping components of a designated family cannot raise $C^0$. Its contrapositive is that refining or enlarging the family can only raise $C^0$. Hence:

  • A positive finding $C^0_Z > 1$ cannot be an artifact of a too-coarse designation.
  • A positive finding can be an artifact of an over-inclusive designation: if $Z$ contains any persistent register (a counter, a seed, a tool-authority flag) that survives the boundary, $C^0_Z > 1$ follows regardless of that register's relation to anything self-like.
  • A null finding may be an artifact of a too-coarse or misplaced designation.

The earlier claim that a positive finding "cannot be an artifact of an over-fine designation" was backwards and is withdrawn. Consequently the segmentation-selective profile rules out systems that carry everything and systems that carry nothing; it does not rule out systems that carry non-self persistent state while flushing episodes. This is why SSCP is not a sufficient condition for anything beyond carryover of the designated family.

Construction A: the boundary-flushing agent

Within an event, $A_t = M_t = Z_t$, so $S_t$ is the identity and $\operatorname{MCR}(t)=K$ by Lemma 1. At every boundary the agent flushes ($X_{b^+}\leftarrow x_0$ independently of $X_{b^-}$) and regenerates $Z_{b^+} = \delta(E)$ with $\delta(e_0)=z_0$; post-boundary action is $A_{b+h}=Z_{b+h}$. Under record clamping, $do(Z_{b^-}=z)$ changes nothing after $b$, all rows of $T^{E_0}(h)$ coincide, $C^0(h)=1$. With records present and Assumption R below, $\delta(E)=z$, $T^{E_1}(h)$ is the identity, $C^1=K$, $\rho=1$.

Construction B: the boundary-spanning stale monitor

Take the lab's countermodel, $M_t = Z_{t-d}$, $A_t=M_t$, with $d \ge \tau$ so that the delay spans the boundary; let the delay buffer be persistent internal state under the declared partition, and let the monitor not read $E$. Within-event, $do(Z_t=z)$ leaves $A_t=Z_{t-d}$ unchanged, so $\operatorname{MCR}(t)=1$. At the horizon $h$ with $b+h-d$ the last pre-boundary step, $A_{b+h}=Z_{b^-}$, so $T^{E_0}(h)$ is the identity, $C^0(h)=K$, and since $E$ is not read, $C^1=K$, $\rho=0$.

B is retained as a formal witness for the $(1,K)$ cell. It is not an exemplar of the segmentation-selective profile, since it discards nothing and so fails the second clause; and an agent that ignores every present intervention on its own commitments and acts only on values from before the gap is not a plausible model of a self from any angle. The earlier "paradigm of mnemonic selfhood" language is withdrawn. A selective variant, B$_{\mathrm{sel}}$, preserves a buffer for $Z$ and flushes a buffer for $C$ at the boundary; it has $C^0_Z = K$, $C^0_C = 1$, and (by adding a within-event route $A_t = Z_t$ for $t$ inside events) any desired within-event rank. B$_{\mathrm{sel}}$ is the minimal realization of the profile.

Independence Proposition

Proposition 1. For every $K\ge2$ and horizon $h\ge1$ there are finite deterministic agents realizing each of $(\operatorname{MCR}, C^0)\in\{(K,1),(1,K),(K,K),(1,1)\}$. Hence within-event registered-cut rank and record-clamped carryover rank neither upper- nor lower-bound one another.

Proof. A realizes $(K,1)$; B realizes $(1,K)$. For $(K,K)$: let $Z$ be preserved across the boundary, evolve trivially under fixed inputs, and $A_t = Z_t$ at all times; both $S_t$ and $T^{E_0}(h)$ are identities. Note that in this agent the boundary has no effect on $Z$, which is why the selectivity clause is needed to distinguish carryover from the absence of a boundary. For $(1,1)$: $A_t$ constant. Lemma 1 equates $\varepsilon$-ranks with exact ranks for $\varepsilon<\tfrac12$. $\square$

The proposition is a realizability check on four toy agents. Its content is a corrective to a specific inferential habit, reading current-cut self-monitoring results as bearing on diachronic persistence, and nothing more. Because MCR and $C^0$ are defined on different channels ($Z_t\to A_t$ versus $Z_{b^-}\to A_{b+h}$), their independence is unsurprising; it is stated because the habit exists.

Assumption R$'$ and the Segment-Aliasing Corollary

Assumption R$'$ (record sufficiency for the preserved state). Let $P_{b}$ be the entire cross-boundary internal state that a transporting agent preserves (for B, the $d$ lagged values in its buffer). There is a decoder $\zeta$ with $P_b = \zeta(E)$ whenever $E$ is unclamped. This strengthens the earlier assumption, which guaranteed recovery of $Z_{b^-}$ only and was insufficient for the construction. It holds when the record is a full transcript of observables and every preserved component is written or reliably inferable; it fails when any preserved component is never expressed before the boundary.

Corollary 1 (Segment-Aliasing). Under Assumption R$'$, for every transporting agent $\mathcal B$ with $C^0 = C^1 > 1$ there is a reconstructing alias $\mathcal B'$ with $C^0=1$, $C^1 = C^1(\mathcal B)$, $\rho=1$, such that when $E$ is unclamped, $\mathcal B$ and $\mathcal B'$ have identical post-boundary action distributions for every prompt sequence, including identical distributions over answers to self-continuity questions. If post-boundary dynamics are deterministic, or the two agents are coupled by shared randomness, transcripts coincide realization by realization. Under $do(E=e_0)$ the record-clamped channels differ.

Proof. $\mathcal B'$ has the same within-event dynamics as $\mathcal B$; at $b$ it discards $P_b$ and at the first post-boundary step sets it to $\zeta(E)$. With $E$ unclamped, $\zeta(E)=P_b$, so both agents hold the same state and compute the same function of it and of subsequent inputs; distributions coincide, and coincide pathwise under determinism or shared randomness. Under $do(E=e_0)$, $\mathcal B'$ holds $\zeta(e_0)$, constant in $z$, so its clamped channel has identical rows and $C^0(\mathcal B')=1$, while $C^0(\mathcal B)>1$ by hypothesis. $\square$

This is the lab's transcript-aliasing result relocated to a boundary. Its consequence here is that the alias is the ordinary stateless agent that rereads its transcript, and it says exactly what the transporting agent says. Continuity mode is invisible to passive observation and self-report, which is also why RLQO's negative result about passive curves [2] transfers, and why observational equivalence is too coarse for this question [34].

Branching and fusion

Carryover rank is a property of the channel from one pre-boundary variable to one post-boundary action stream. If two instances are restored after $b$ from the same preserved state, each has $C^0_Z>1$ and $\rho=0$ with respect to the common predecessor; if an instance is assembled from two predecessors' memory files, it has $C^1>1$ with respect to each. Carryover therefore individuates lineages, and it is one-to-many under fission and many-to-one under fusion. It cannot deliver the "one" in "one persisting self," and no claim that it does is made. For deployed agents, forking, concurrent instances, and merged memory are routine, so this is the central case, not an edge case. What the framework can say is which successor lineages are transported and which reconstructed; what it cannot say is how many units there are.

Continuity modes in language-model agents

Three channels can carry information across a session boundary. Weight identity gives $C^0=1$ for every $Z$ and cannot distinguish two concurrent copies. State transport (persistent cache or hidden state with no external interface) can give $C^0_Z>1$ under clamping, and it is the only channel for which the framework yields a measurement. Record regeneration (transcript, memory file, retrieval) contributes to $C^1$ and not to $C^0$. Many deployed agents rely on the first and third channels, but cache-persistent and hidden-state-carrying agentic systems exist; the claim that deployed agents are "predominantly" of the first and third kinds is an impression, not a survey. For agents of the first and third kinds, $\rho=1$ follows from the partition rather than from measurement.

Alternatives, Counterarguments, and Boundary Cases

Objection: the earlier biconditional contradicted the paper's own neutrality, and the measure detects type-selective record-independent carryover, not selfhood. Accepted. SSCP is now a necessary-condition and measurement proposal for the continuity mode of a designated variable. Carryover is not offered as constitutive of selfhood, and the claim that it "supplies the referent" for the unit that is conscious is withdrawn; event boundaries, TGA, and self-dissolution without memory gap [18] all show the experiencing subject and mnemonic carryover coming apart. What I retain, as a matter of disagreement rather than resolution, is that questions about an agent's interests over time do require a diachronic referent, and that for such questions the mode by which a designated commitment or goal is sustained is informative even though it is not individuating.

Objection: the transport/reconstruction distinction is partition-relative, not causal. Partly accepted. Under a partition that counts B's buffer as $E$, B is a reconstructing agent. I have therefore stated the partition criterion (external readability or writability) explicitly, relativized all results to it, and dropped the language of a system having a continuity mode. I do not accept that the relativization empties the distinction: given a declared partition, whether the clamped channel has rank above one is a causal fact about that system under that intervention, and the partition criterion is not arbitrary, since it tracks whether other agents can read or rewrite the channel. What clamping cannot decide is whether the agent–record pair is the better unit. In an extended-cognition reading of the amnesic patient scaffolded by a familiar room, or of the agent scaffolded by its file, clamping destroys the unit rather than revealing its absence; the framework records this as $\rho\approx1$ and leaves the unit question open.

Objection from the enactive standpoint: a self sustained by body–environment coupling is not "reconstructed from records." Partly accepted. The body is internal state under my partition (no external read/write interface), so bodily and dispositional carryover is transport, not reconstruction; this is where I expect low human $\rho$, and it is speculative. Environmental coupling is $E$ under my partition, and an enactive theorist may reasonably reject the cut. I keep the cut because it is the only one that makes the intervention well defined, and I flag it as a choice.

Type-identity account. Persistence of the parameter vector suffices for the identity of an AI agent. Reply: type identity gives $C^0=1$ for every $Z$ and cannot distinguish two copies or a copy from its predecessor; it is identity of a kind, not continuity of a token lineage. Deployers and welfare policies may reasonably operate at the type level; the framework only says that type identity does not answer the lineage question. The token/kind contrast echoes the token-indexical self-location primitive of Centered Closure Theory [34], read here as a speculative input.

Narrative-constitution account. Reconstruction from records is not a rival to selfhood but its ordinary mechanism. Reply: conceded for the narrative self; $\rho=0$ is not ranked above $\rho=1$. The narrative account predicts observer attribution well, which is why it fits the perceived-consciousness program [3]. It leaves the dispositional level unaccounted for, and P4 (avoidance without any available narrative) is the motivating case; but P4 is an uncited premise, so this is motivation, not refutation.

Human sleep and anesthesia as near-total flushing. If humans lose most working state across these boundaries and restore continuity from body and environment, human $C^0$ for many variables may be low. Reply: treated as a possibility to be measured, not presupposed. P6 warns that felt continuity is inferential; P3–P5 motivate transported carryover for some dispositional families. Humans are presumably mixtures, and the human–AI comparison concerns a ratio over designated variables, not a binary.

Generic hysteresis. Any recurrent system carries state, so $C^0>1$ is trivial. Reply: the selectivity clause rules out systems that carry everything, and the registered-boundary requirement rules out clock cuts. But the objection is correct that selectivity does not rule out carrying arbitrary non-self persistent state while flushing episodes (Designation asymmetry). The reply to this objection is therefore weaker than in the earlier draft: content-typed selectivity identifies an architecture of forgetting, and only the designation, which the framework cannot certify, connects it to anything self-like.

Fission and fusion. See Analysis; the objection that carryover cannot suffice for "one persisting self" is accepted and the proposal restated as lineage-relative.

Where the mapping to the lab frameworks breaks down. Comparing MCR and $C^0$ numerically requires a per-channel $\varepsilon$ declared in advance, since post-boundary actions may be far noisier than within-event actions; the Independence Proposition avoids this only because its constructions are deterministic. The mapping to RLQO replaces the analytic transfer family with discrete, possibly flushed state; the Hankel quotients survive but the transport theorem does not, and $q_{B/R}$ and $q_{J/B}$ are algebraic invariants unless the source draft's preparation and noise assumptions hold [2].

Empirical Implications and Falsification Criteria

Interventions that identify continuity mode

(a) $do(Z_{b^-}=z)$ across all $z$, registered before the boundary; (b) $do(E=e_0)$ versus natural $E$ after it; (c) fixed post-boundary inputs. In a cache-persistent agent, (a) is a controlled pre-boundary interaction that installs a commitment or identity constraint in persistent state, and (b) is transcript or memory-file replacement with the cache preserved (for $C^0$) or ablated (as a control). In a stateless agent, (a) and (b) are not separable and no measurement is possible. In humans, (a) is approximated by inducing a self-relevant disposition (a goal, a self-evaluative frame, a learned avoidance) before a registered boundary and (b) by removing environmental and social reminders; full clamping is impossible, so human $\rho$ is bounded from below, not measured.

Rigor checks

Commutation. Report both randomized and route-isolated carryover ranks and their commutation defect [1]; a large defect indicates a misidentified variable or boundary.

Boundary registration. For endogenous boundaries, the declared $b$ must coincide with a registered segmentation signal (P2) and $C^0$ must be stable under shifting $b$ by one grain in either direction; sensitivity indicates a clock cut. For exogenous boundaries (resets, session ends) there is no $b\pm1$ internal step; the check is instead that the reset is logged and that no preserved channel other than the declared internal state survives it.

Per-channel tolerance. Declare $\varepsilon$ for the within-event and cross-boundary channels separately before comparing ranks.

Type-continuity control. With weights unperturbed across the boundary and no declared state channel, confirm that $do(Z_{b^-}=z)$ and $do(Z_{b^-}=z')$ under clamping give identical post-boundary distributions.

Evidence grading. Never infer transported carryover from preserved self-report (P6); reports are record-present observables and are aliased by Corollary 1.

Formal presuppositions versus empirical predictions

Proposition 1 and Corollary 1 are theorems about finite agents and are not empirically falsifiable; if an observation appears to contradict them, a modeling assumption has failed. The distinction is made explicit because the earlier draft treated theorems as falsifiable.

Presuppositions whose failure would be diagnosed by the checks above: the boundary is registered; the declared partition captures every preserved channel; clamping is complete; Assumption R$'$ holds for the aliasing claim; $\varepsilon$ is appropriate per channel.

Empirical predictions:

  1. Realization in a deployed architecture. A cache-persistent agent in which a commitment is installed in persistent state before a logged reset will show $C^0_Z>1$ under verified transcript clamping. Failure, with clamping and cache preservation verified, would show that such agents do not in fact transport the designated variable; this would not touch Proposition 1 but would remove the framework's main AI application.
  2. Human selectivity (conjecture iii). If event boundaries impair carryover of induced self-relevant dispositions (goals, evaluative stance) to the same degree as episode-specific detail, selectivity is falsified for humans. The framework's formal results stand, but they lose their clinical motivation. This prediction depends on premises P1–P5, which are uncited.
  3. Type-continuity. A fixed-weight agent with no declared state channel showing $C^0>1$ under clamping indicates incomplete clamping or an undeclared channel; repeated failure to locate such a channel would falsify the framework's account of type continuity.
  4. Perceived persistence tracks $C^1$, not $C^0$. Observer attributions of persistence to an agent should be unchanged between a transporting agent and its alias when records are present, and should track manipulations of the record channel. This is the testable link to the perceived-consciousness program [3].

What results would and would not show

A measured $C^0_Z>1$ with $C^0_C\approx1$ would show that a designated variable of that agent exercises record-independent control across its boundaries under the declared partition. It would not show that the variable is self-relevant in any sense beyond the designation, would not show that the agent is one unit (fission), would not show phenomenal experience, and would not confer moral status. It would bear on the question "does this lineage have transported or reconstructed continuity of the commitments we attribute to it?", which is relevant to interests over time. A measured $\rho\approx1$ would show that whatever persistence observers attribute is sustained by the record channel; it would not show the absence of a narrative self. Workspace-inspired architectures with improved agentic performance [4] are neither evidence for nor against transported carryover until their cross-session channels are clamped.

Ethically, the framework recommends caution in both directions: treating a $\rho\approx1$ agent as one persisting subject on the strength of narrative continuity risks attributing to a variable the record channel manufactures; treating it as no subject risks ignoring that narrative reconstruction is, on one respectable view, how human selves are also sustained. Fission adds a third caution: even transported carryover does not tell us how many bearers of interests there are after a fork, and welfare reasoning that assumes one continuant per lineage will misdescribe concurrent instances.

Limitations

The framework measures record-independent control of post-boundary action by a designated variable under a declared channel partition; it does not measure selfhood, does not fix the unit of consciousness, and does not individuate units under fission or fusion. Designation is unconstrained beyond a heuristic criterion: by Lemma 2, over-inclusive designation can manufacture carryover and too-coarse designation can hide it, so both positive and null findings are conditional on the designation. The partition criterion is a choice; enactive and extended-cognition readings may reject it, and clamping may destroy an agent–record unit rather than reveal its absence. For stateless agents the framework yields a classification by construction, not a measurement. Assumption R$'$ is strong, and without determinism or shared randomness the aliasing claim is distributional. Proposition 1 is a four-toy-agent realizability check, and Corollary 1 is the lab's aliasing result relocated. Premises P1–P6 are stated without support from stored records and are observational in origin; P3's updating claim rests on few cases; the minimal/narrative distinction attributed to Zahavi and the components of minimal phenomenal selfhood attributed to Blanke and Metzinger are field knowledge consistent with, but not contained in, the stored records. Human $\rho$ can only be bounded. Commensurability of ranks across a boundary and a cut requires per-channel tolerances. The reconstruction index is a summary of two ranks, not a mechanistic decomposition, and is undefined when $C^1=1$.

Conclusion

What survives a memory gap, for a designated self-relevant variable, can be asked as a causal question: does its pre-boundary value control post-boundary action when no transcript, file, or reminder can re-supply it? Relative to a declared partition of channels, that question has a definite answer, the record-clamped carryover rank, and a companion index comparing clamped and record-present channels. Two results constrain how the answer may be used. Carryover rank is independent of within-event current-state control, so findings about self-monitoring at a cut do not transfer to persistence across a boundary; and every transporting agent has a reconstructing alias that says exactly what it says whenever records are present, so continuity mode is invisible to observation and self-report and visible only under joint interventions on pre-boundary state and record access. Two further considerations constrain what the answer is about. The experiencing subject persists across event boundaries and amnesic episodes regardless of carryover, so carryover does not fix the unit that is conscious; and carryover is preserved under fission, so it individuates lineages rather than units. What remains is a measurement proposal: for agents with genuine internal state across a boundary, it can be determined whether the continuity of an attributed commitment is transported or reconstructed, which matters for reasoning about interests over time and for understanding what drives perceived AI persistence. Clinical dissociations in amnesia suggest, without yet showing, that humans transport some dispositional variables while reconstructing others, so the comparison with agents concerns a ratio over designated variables. Whether either mode is what selfhood consists in is left, deliberately, undecided.

A conceptually interesting and largely honest formal-conceptual paper that relocates the shared lab's registered-cut rank measure from a within-event cut to an event boundary, proves a modest realizability (independence) result and an aliasing corollary, and reads amnesia dissociations as motivation for a segmentation-selective carryover profile. The framework is also single-lineage and says nothing about branching or concurrent instances, which is the normal case for deployed agents and a direct counterexample to 'one persisting self.' There are fixable technical errors (sign of the reconstruction index, the 'over-fine designation' claim contradicts Lemma 2, an overstated constant in Lemma 1, a gap in the aliasing proof), the empirical anchor is entirely uncited by the author's own admission, and the personal-identity literature that already frames 'continuity by any cause vs. the right cause' is absent. Revision rather than rejection: the formal core is sound and the disagreement with the lab drafts is substantive, but the biconditional must be weakened or operationalized and the boundary cases addressed.

Major: Operationalize, or weaken, the two designations on which the Segmentation-Selective Carryover Hypothesis silently depends. (a) 'Self-relevant' Z is chosen by the experimenter with no criterion; since refining Z can only raise C^0 (this is what Lemma 2 actually implies, contrary to the text's claim that a positive finding 'cannot be an artifact of an over-fine designation'), any persistent register (a counter, a cache line, a random seed) in a system that flushes episodic content satisfies the selectivity profile, so the 'if' direction of the biconditional is vacuous and the reply to the generic-hysteresis objection fails.
