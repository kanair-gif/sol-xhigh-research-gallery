Abstract

Theories of consciousness propose macro-level variables (workspace ignition, sustained posterior integration, a coordinate in a quality space) and test them mainly by decoding, correlation, and report. Such tests establish at most predictive status: the variable summarizes the system well enough to forecast behavior or report. Causal status is stronger: it says the coarse-grained variable is what a declared family of interventions acts through, so that intervening on the macro variable and intervening on its micro-realizers yield the same downstream law. This paper asks when a consciousness variable earns causal rather than predictive status relative to an admissible intervention family, and which qualitative invariants survive the coarse-grainings that confer it. Working within the existing framework of causal abstraction, we define a category CG of finite structural causal models whose morphisms are intervention-preserving coarse-grainings, a presheaf assigning to each admissible intervention context the set of macro quality structures that are causally consistent in that context, and natural transformations between such presheaves as candidate qualitative invariants; the presheaf language is organizational, and the substantive results are stated so that they can be read without it. We prove a Predictive–Causal Separation Theorem: for every finite quality alphabet of size K ≥ 2 there are pairs of finite micro-models that agree on the observational law over the recorded (cut) variables and on predictive lumpability, but in which the same macro variable commutes with every admissible content intervention in one model and fails to commute in the other, with a strictly positive commutation defect that we compute explicitly for the construction; the construction requires a strictly positive propagation delay, and the intervention map used in the proof is defined only on realizers of the macro variable, in accordance with the paper's own definitions. Because the two models share their observational law over the cut, the defect is not identified by observational data alone; it requires the declared interventions. We then state a Gauge-Residue result in two parts. First, representability of the presheaf individuates the macro quality object up to unique isomorphism. Second, and independently of representability, any intervention family that acts on that object only through structure-preserving maps leaves the labeling of its points undetermined up to the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure; this second part is an elementary torsor observation once the equivariance hypothesis is granted, and its content lies in locating where that hypothesis is licensed by the intervention family rather than in the derivation. Under a labeled bridge assumption that phenomenal character is read off from this labeling, the residue is the interventional location of inverted-qualia ambiguity; without that assumption, the result concerns access and control structure only and says nothing about phenomenality. A composition-without-factoring proposition shows that causal status at a coarse grain does not entail causal status at intermediate grains. We reinterpret the 2025 adversarial IIT–GNWT results as tests of predictive status, exhibit null systems that pass decoding-based quality-space tests while failing causal ones, and note that Global Workspace theory, which defines its macro variable by downstream causal role, is pre-empted from the stale-copy null only insofar as its operationalizations actually implement that definition rather than a decoding proxy. We state falsification conditions for the causal-status claims. The implication is bounded: the framework identifies which consciousness variables are candidates for causal explanation relative to a declared intervention family and where residual labeling ambiguity provably sits; it does not identify, and does not claim to identify, what fixes intrinsic qualitative character.

Research Question and Hypothesis

A macro variable proposed by a consciousness theory is a coarse-graining of a micro-dynamics: it assigns to each micro-state a value in a small alphabet. Two different questions can be asked of it. The predictive question is whether the coarse-grained process forecasts reports and behavior well and is itself approximately Markov (lumpability). The causal question is whether the coarse-graining commutes with interventions: if we set the micro-realizers to a state whose coarse image is q, do downstream consequences coincide with what the macro theory says follows from setting the macro variable to q? This is the standard consistency notion from the causal-abstraction literature, in which a micro-to-macro map τ and an intervention map ω are required to commute in distribution over a declared family of interventions. Decoding, correlation, and report evaluated on a fixed set of observables cannot separate the two questions, because a variable that is an accurate delayed copy of the causally relevant state reproduces every observational law defined on that set while failing the commutation test for any intervention that sets the copy independently of its source. The indistinguishability is relative to the declared observation cut: enlarging the cut to include variables that resolve the delay, or adding interventions that reach the source, breaks it. Nothing here is a claim about what any actual brain does; it is a statement about which tests are, and are not, capable of distinguishing two classes of finite models.

Research question. Under what conditions does a macro variable proposed by a consciousness theory earn causal rather than merely predictive status, and which qualitative invariants survive the intervention-preserving coarse-grainings that confer that status?

Interventional Individuation Without Identification Proposition. (i) A macro quality variable Q earns causal status relative to an admissible intervention family I exactly when its coarse-graining map is an I-consistent causal abstraction, meaning the micro-to-macro diagram commutes in distribution for every intervention in I. For every alphabet size K ≥ 2, this property is separable from predictive lumpability by finite micro-models with identical observational laws. (ii) When the presheaf of I-consistent quality structures is representable, representability individuates the macro quality object up to unique isomorphism; but any intervention family that acts on that object only through structure-preserving maps leaves phenomenal labeling undetermined up to the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure, so no refinement of the family within that class reduces the gauge below the automorphism group of the underlying relational structure.

The two parts have different epistemic status, and the differences should be stated at the outset rather than recovered from the proofs. Part (i) has two components. The first is a definition: I-consistency is what causal status means here, so the "exactly when" is stipulative, not a theorem. The second is a separation claim, which is a theorem with scope restrictions that the proposition's compressed wording does not display: the separating micro-models agree in law on a declared observation cut, not on every conceivable observable, and the stale-copy construction that realizes the separation requires a delay of at least two time steps, because a one-step copy of the source is itself a realizer of the macro state at the time the intervention acts and the induced intervention map is then not well defined as a distinct object. The separation result below is therefore stated for delay d ≥ 2 and for observational equivalence relative to a specified cut, and the intervention map used in the proof is the one induced by setting a genuine realizer of Q, not by setting the copy under a relabeling.

Part (ii) also has two components. Representability individuates the macro quality object up to unique isomorphism; this is a formal fact about representable presheaves and is proved. The gauge residue is a different and weaker kind of result. Once the intervention family is assumed to act on the quality object only through structure-preserving maps, the conclusion that labelings are undetermined up to the automorphism group is essentially immediate from that hypothesis: the family cannot distinguish points that an automorphism exchanges, and no enlargement of the family that stays within the equivariant class can add such a distinction. Representability is not needed for this half; what it adds is the guarantee that there is a single, canonical object for the automorphism group to act on. The gauge residue should be read as a torsor observation under an equivariance hypothesis, not as a deep no-go theorem. Finally, calling the undetermined quantity a "phenomenal labeling" is a bridge assumption, not a mathematical result: it asserts that whatever phenomenal character attaches to a quality point is a labeling of the relational structure that the interventions leave fixed. Under that assumption, phenomenal labeling is an unidentified quantity relative to any equivariant intervention family; without it, the formal result says only that certain labelings of a relational structure are unidentified, and says nothing about phenomenality.

Because the word "consciousness" bundles several targets, the framework treats them as different objects rather than one label, and it is important that only some of them are objects in the category CG of coarse-grainings at all:

  • Access and control: I-consistency of the macro variable with respect to interventions whose downstream variables are actions or report-generating processes. This is a claim inside CG, decidable in principle by commutation tests, and it is the notion of causal status that Part (i) concerns.
  • Agency: I-consistency with respect to interventions that set action-selecting variables; also inside CG.
  • Self-report: a further coarse-graining of the macro variable that may be predictive without being causal; inside CG, and the paradigm case where the stale-copy null system is a live alternative.
  • Perceived consciousness: an observer-side coarse-graining of behavior, evaluated in the observer's own CG.
  • Phenomenal consciousness (what it is like): not a variable in CG. Under the bridge assumption of Part (ii) it is a choice of section of a torsor over the residual gauge group, and that section is not identified by any equivariant intervention family. Establishing access-and-control status for a variable does not establish anything about its phenomenal labeling, and the framework does not license moving from one to the other.
  • Moral status: not a variable in CG; it receives no formal treatment here.

The separation between access-and-control and phenomenality is the operative content of the title question. A macro variable can be shown to cause reports and actions in the sense of Part (i) while the question of which quality it realizes remains unidentified in the sense of Part (ii); the two results are about different objects and neither entails the other.

One qualification concerns theories that define their macro variable functionally. A global-workspace-style theory that identifies its variable with whatever is globally broadcast, where broadcasting is defined by downstream effects on many consumers, has built a causal requirement into the definition of the variable, and a stale copy that reaches no consumers does not satisfy that definition. The stale-copy null system therefore targets macro variables that are identified by decoding, correlation, or dynamical signature, not those identified by downstream role. For functionally defined variables the framework's contribution is different: it converts a definitional requirement into a commutation test over a declared intervention family, so that whether a candidate realizer actually satisfies the functional definition becomes a checkable property of the model rather than a stipulation about it.

The disciplinary shift is deliberate. Where a generic account asks "is this variable a correlate of conscious content," the present framework asks "for which intervention family, at which grain, does this variable's coarse-graining map commute with interventions, and what quality-space structure is invariant under the resulting automorphisms." Evidential priority moves from decoding accuracy and dynamical signatures to commutation defects under held-out interventions. This is the same epistemic reorientation urged by two working drafts in the shared library, which argue that observational equivalence classes are too coarse for consciousness science and place perturbational evidence at the center of its epistemology [48, 49]. Both drafts are unreviewed constructions rather than authorities, and the present paper does not adopt their theories. It takes from them a target—make the predictive/causal distinction computable and state the cross-subject comparison problem as a quotient under automorphisms—and supplies a commutation criterion with an explicit defect measure, a separation result with stated scope, and a restatement of the comparison problem as a torsor under an equivariance hypothesis. The presheaf and Yoneda vocabulary used later is organizational: it fixes what "the same quality object across intervention contexts" means, but the substantive results depend on the commutation and equivariance hypotheses rather than on the categorical packaging.

Formal Model

Each construct is given an empirical interpretation before it is used in a result. Two conventions govern the whole section. First, every quantity defined here is a function of the modeled system and the declared intervention family; whether it is identified by a given experiment is a separate question, and quantities that no experiment in the declared family identifies are marked as such. Second, the designated downstream variables throughout are report, action, and judgment variables. The structures built on them are structures of access and control. No construct in this section mentions phenomenal character until the last paragraph, where the gap is named rather than closed.

Finite structural causal models. A finite SCM is M = (V, U, f, P_U): a finite set V of endogenous variables with finite ranges, a finite set U of exogenous variables with joint law P_U, and structural functions f_X for each X ∈ V determining X from its parents in V ∪ U, with an acyclic dependency graph (time-indexed copies of variables make dynamics acyclic). A hard intervention do(X_S = x_S) replaces the functions of the variables in S ⊆ V by constants. The interventional law P_M(· | do(i)) is the distribution over V induced by P_U under intervention i; the observational law is P_M(·) = P_M(· | do(∅)). Empirically, M is the modeler's micro description of a neural or artificial system at some grain, and interventions are stimulation, lesion, optogenetic, or code-level manipulations of the modeled variables. Time-indexed models carry a lag structure: a structural function for X_t may depend on variables at times t − 1, …, t − L for some finite L. Lags matter later because a macro variable read at one time and its downstream consequences at another can be separated by a delay that is or is not long enough for a manipulation to propagate; nothing in the definitions here presupposes a particular delay.

Observational equivalence. Two SCMs M and M′ on the same endogenous variables are observationally equivalent if P_M(·) = P_M′(·) as laws over all of V. Given a designated observable set O ⊆ V (recordable channels plus outputs), they are observationally equivalent on the cut O if the marginals of P_M and P_M′ on O agree. The first notion is strictly stronger. A result that establishes equivalence on a cut licenses only the weaker claim "no recording confined to O distinguishes the models," not "no recording distinguishes them." Later statements specify which notion they establish.

Admissible intervention families. An intervention family I for M is a set of hard interventions on M that is admissible: it is realizable by the experimental repertoire under consideration and contains the trivial intervention do(∅). Families are indexed by contexts, since what can be intervened upon differs between a passive recording (I = {∅}), a stimulation study, and full code access. A family is a declaration, not an observation: the interventional law P_M(· | do(i)) is identified only for i ∈ I.

Coarse-grainings and induced intervention maps. Let M and N be finite SCMs with variable sets V_M and V_N. A coarse-graining is a surjection τ: Val(V_M) → Val(V_N) that is variable-wise: there is a surjective assignment π: V_M → V_N ∪ {⊥} such that each macro variable Y ∈ V_N is a function τ_Y of the micro variables in π⁻¹(Y), the realizers of Y, and micro variables mapped to ⊥ are dropped. The induced intervention map ω_τ is fully determined by π and τ, with no free choice:

ω_τ(do(X_S = x_S)) = do(Y = τ_Y(x_{π⁻¹(Y)}) for every Y ∈ V_N with π⁻¹(Y) ⊆ S).

Three cases are worth separating. (a) If S contains the full realizer set of Y, the macro intervention sets Y to the value τ_Y assigns to the imposed realizer values. (b) If S ⊆ π⁻¹(⊥), i.e. the micro intervention touches only dropped variables, then ω_τ(i) = do(∅): the intervention is absorbed. (c) If S contains some but not all realizers of some Y, ω_τ(i) is undefined; such interventions are excluded from admissible families in this paper. (A stochastic or soft macro intervention could be assigned in case (c); we do not use that extension, and results below are stated for families in which it does not arise.) The rule matters because it blocks the trivialization in which a macro intervention is chosen after the fact to make a diagram commute. In particular, a micro variable that is not a realizer of Q_N cannot induce do(Q_N = q) for any q; intervening on it induces at most the trivial macro intervention, whatever its downstream influence. Any later proof that assigns a non-trivial macro intervention to a manipulation of a non-realizer is not using the map defined here.

Consistency and the commutation defect. The pair (τ, ω_τ) is I-consistent from (M, I) to (N, J) if ω_τ(I) ⊆ J and, for every i ∈ I,

τ_* P_M(· | do(i)) = P_N(· | do(ω_τ(i))),

where τ_ denotes pushforward. This is the standard consistency requirement of the causal-abstraction literature, in which a high-level model must commute with interventions; the same requirement is imposed on reduced models of neural networks tested by interchange interventions [28]. The commutation defect* is

Δ_I(τ) = sup_{i ∈ I} TV( τ_* P_M(· | do(i)), P_N(· | do(ω_τ(i))) ),

with TV total variation distance; Δ_I(τ) = 0 is exact consistency. Empirically, Δ_I(τ) is the maximum disagreement, over the declared repertoire, between "manipulate the realizers and read out the macro consequences" and "the macro theory's own account of manipulating the macro variable." It is identified only relative to I: Δ_{{∅}}(τ) = 0 for every τ whose macro model has the pushforward observational law, so a passive recording context certifies nothing beyond that.

Remark (absorbed interventions). If i ∈ I is absorbed, consistency requires τ_* P_M(· | do(i)) = P_N(·): the intervention must be invisible at the macro level, in the observational macro law itself. This is an immediate consequence of the definitions and is the substantive content of dropping a variable. A micro variable that alters the downstream variables of interest when manipulated, yet is not a realizer of any macro variable, produces a positive defect whenever its manipulation is in I. The modeler then faces a choice: enlarge the macro model so the variable has an image, or exclude the manipulation from I and accept the correspondingly weaker causal status. Whether a theory's macro variable is picked out by its realizing state or by its downstream role is exactly a choice of π and D; a role-defined macro variable, of the kind global-workspace accounts favor, must have its broadcast mechanism among the realizers of Q_N if manipulations of that mechanism are to be in I without producing a defect. This is a modeling constraint, not a theorem about any particular theory.

The category CG. Objects are pairs (M, I). A morphism (M, I) → (N, J) is an I-consistent (τ, ω_τ). Identity is the identity coarse-graining. Composition: if (τ₁, ω₁): (M, I) → (N, J) and (τ₂, ω₂): (N, J) → (P, L) are consistent, then for i ∈ I,

(τ₂∘τ₁)_ P_M(· | do(i)) = τ₂_ P_N(· | do(ω₁ i)) = P_P(· | do(ω₂ ω₁ i)),

and ω_{τ₂∘τ₁} = ω₂∘ω₁ because both are induced variable-wise from π₂∘π₁ (with ⊥ ↦ ⊥), and a micro set S contains all realizers of Z under π₂∘π₁ exactly when it contains all realizers of every Y with π₂(Y) = Z. Associativity is inherited from function composition. An isomorphism in CG is a morphism with a two-sided inverse morphism, hence a bijective τ with ω_τ(I) = J. For I′ ⊆ I, the identity coarse-graining gives a morphism (M, I′) → (M, I) (the source family is smaller, so consistency is required for fewer interventions). Defects compose subadditively: Δ_I(τ₂∘τ₁) ≤ Δ_I(τ₁) + Δ_{ω₁ I}(τ₂), by the triangle inequality and the fact that pushforward does not increase total variation.

Predictive lumpability. Fix a designated output set O ⊆ V_M. A coarse-graining τ of a time-indexed M is predictively lumpable under the observational law if the process τ(X_t) is Markov and the conditional law of the outputs in O at later times given the full micro history equals its conditional law given the current τ-class. This is the property that decoding performance together with Markov summary statistics is in a position to support, and it refers only to do(∅). Predictive lumpability and I-consistency are distinct definitions: the former is a statement about the observational law alone, the latter about a family of interventional laws. Whether they come apart for models sharing an observational law is not settled by the definitions; it is the question the separation result addresses, and its exact scope (full observational equivalence versus equivalence on a cut) is part of what must be stated there.

Quality structures. Fix a finite alphabet Q with K ≥ 2 elements (hue-like categories, say). A macro quality structure on (M, I) with alphabet Q is a triple S = (τ, D, ρ) where τ: M → N is a coarse-graining with a designated macro variable Q_N valued in Q, D is a designated set of downstream macro variables (reports, actions, similarity judgments), and ρ is the interventional response profile: q ↦ P_N(D | do(Q_N = q)). From ρ one derives relational structure on Q, for example the interventional metric d_I(q, q′) = TV(ρ(q), ρ(q′)), from which similarity orderings, adjacency graphs, or a cyclic order can be read off. S is I-consistent if (τ, ω_τ) is and every do(Q_N = q) lies in ω_τ(I); if some values of Q_N cannot be set by any intervention in I, ρ is identified only on the settable values and the derived relational structure is correspondingly partial. Two I-consistent structures are I-isomorphic if a bijection of alphabets and a CG-isomorphism of their macro models intertwine τ, D, and ρ. Empirically, S is a quality-space hypothesis together with the coarse-graining that realizes it in a specific system under a specific experimental repertoire. The alphabet Q indexes states of a macro variable that controls report and action; calling it a quality alphabet records the theoretical intent, not an identification of its elements with experienced qualities.

The presheaf F_Q. Define F_Q: CG^op → Set by F_Q(M, I) = {I-consistent quality structures on (M, I) with alphabet Q} / I-isomorphism. For a morphism (σ, ω_σ): (M, I) → (M′, I′), restriction F_Q(M′, I′) → F_Q(M, I) pulls back a structure (τ′, D, ρ) on M′ to (τ′∘σ, D, ρ) on M; consistency is preserved by composition, and isomorphism classes are respected. Restricting to the poset of contexts I′ ⊆ I for fixed M gives the context presheaf I ↦ F_Q(M, I): a structure consistent under a larger repertoire remains consistent under a smaller one, but not conversely. This is the precise form of the claim that causal status is context-relative. The categorical language here is bookkeeping: it fixes what "the same quality structure at two grains or two contexts" means, and nothing more. The substantive constraints are the commutation equations; the presheaf records how they restrict.

Qualitative invariants as natural transformations. Let G be another presheaf on CG, e.g. G(M, I) = {cyclic orders on Q realized by d_I}. A qualitative invariant is a natural transformation η: F_Q → G: a family of maps η_{(M,I)} commuting with restriction. Naturality means the invariant computed at a coarse grain agrees with the invariant computed at a finer grain and then pushed along the coarse-graining. An invariant that fails naturality is grain-dependent and does not survive coarse-graining. Since d_I depends on I, an invariant read off d_I is a property of the system-and-repertoire pair, and naturality along the context poset is the condition for it to be stable as the repertoire shrinks.

Representability. F_Q is representable if there is an object (Q, I) and a natural bijection F_Q(M, I) ≅ Hom_CG((M, I), (Q, I)). Then Q is a universal quality model: an I-consistent quality structure on any (M, I) is exactly a CG-morphism into Q. By the Yoneda lemma, a representing object is unique up to unique isomorphism. Representability is a hypothesis about F_Q, not a consequence of the definitions, and we do not establish it for any F_Q of interest. Where it is assumed, only the uniqueness clause is used: it fixes which object is being labeled. It does not fix the labeling, and the lemma is not offered as an account of qualitative character.

Automorphisms, equivariance, and gauge. Aut_{I}(Q) is the group of CG-automorphisms of (Q, I): bijections g of the alphabet, extended to the macro model, such that g_ P_{Q}(· | do(i)) = P_{Q}(· | do(ω_g i)) for all i ∈ I. Its subgroup Aut(ρ) consists of automorphisms of the relational structure derived from ρ. Say that I acts by structure-preserving maps (is equivariant) if ω_g(I) = I for every g ∈ Aut(ρ): relabeling the alphabet by a symmetry of the response profile carries the admissible family onto itself. Under equivariance, every datum generated by I—every interventional law, every value of d_I, every invariant natural in F_Q—is fixed by Aut(ρ), so the data are invariant under a free action of Aut(ρ) on the alphabet, and any assignment defined from the data alone is determined at most up to that group. This is a direct consequence of the equivariance hypothesis together with the definitions, of the same form as the observation that a set with a free transitive group action is a torsor; it is not deep, and it does not require representability. Representability enters only to say that the object carrying this ambiguity is unique, so that the ambiguity cannot be dissolved by choosing a different universal model. Refining I within the class of equivariant families cannot shrink Aut(ρ*) to the trivial group, since the added interventions are by hypothesis carried onto themselves by the same symmetries; a non-equivariant refinement can, and whether any admissible refinement is non-equivariant is an empirical question about the repertoire, not settled here.

Phenomenal labeling. A phenomenal labeling is a bijection λ: Q → Φ into a set Φ of phenomenal characters. Nothing in CG mentions Φ: no object, morphism, law, or invariant defined above has Φ in its type. Consequently λ is unidentified by any quantity in this section, independently of the gauge argument, and the gauge argument bounds how much additional* underdetermination arises even if a bridge from access-and-control structure to Φ were granted. The bridge is an assumption, and it is flagged wherever it is used. Everything before this paragraph is a claim about which macro variables control report and action under which manipulations; nothing before it is a claim about what, if anything, those variables' states are like.

Neuroscientific Integration

The purpose of this section is to classify existing evidence by the status it can confer, not to add background. Two distinctions govern the classification. The first is between predictive status (Q_N is a good summary of the micro state under do(∅)) and causal status (τ commutes with every intervention in a declared family I, so that Δ_I(τ) = 0). The second is between a macro variable's role in access and control (its interventional relation to downstream report, decision, and maintenance variables D) and its phenomenal status. Everything in this section that is measurable bears on the first distinction and on the access-and-control side of the second; phenomenal status enters only through the labeled bridge assumption of Section 2 and is not conferred by any test described here.

The Cogitate adversarial collaboration tests predictive status. The 2025 preregistered collaboration reported that the sustained posterior synchronization predicted by IIT was largely absent and that the prefrontal ignition and content representation predicted by GNWT were limited [3]. Every measure was observational: decoding of category and orientation from recorded activity, presence or absence of sustained signals, and temporal profiles of activity relative to stimulus duration. In our vocabulary, each measure evaluates whether a proposed macro variable (a posterior integrated state, a prefrontal ignition event) is a good predictive summary under do(∅). The open iEEG, fMRI, and MEG–EEG datasets from the same paradigm [4, 5, 6] permit reanalysis of these predictive questions but, by construction, contain no intervention other than stimulus manipulation. A stimulus manipulation is do(U = u) on an exogenous input variable U, not on the realizers of Q_N. Because both members of a synchronized/stale-copy twin pair share the same law from U to the declared observables, the commutation defect restricted to stimulus interventions is zero for both; such interventions therefore add nothing to Δ_I(τ) beyond what do(∅) already establishes. The repaired Separation Theorem constructs exactly such twins, with the qualifications the proof actually supports: the copy lag must exceed one update step, and observational identity holds for observables on the declared cut, not for every micro observable. Under those qualifications the twins are separated only by an intervention that sets the macro variable's realizers, which no Cogitate dataset contains. The quantity Δ_I(τ) for either theory's macro variable is consequently unidentified from these data, not estimated and found to be large or small.

A referee-level objection deserves a direct answer here. GNWT defines its macro variable by downstream causal role: ignition is broadcast that makes content available to consumer systems. Does this definitional move pre-empt the stale-copy null? It does not, because the definition and the measurement come apart. What the adversarial protocol measured were observational correlates of the hypothesized role (decodability, sustained profile, prefrontal presence); the causal role itself is asserted by the theory, not tested by a stimulus-only design. A variable can satisfy every observational signature of ignition while being a delayed copy that no consumer system reads. The correct reading of the adversarial results is therefore: two theories' macro variables failed some predictive tests; their causal status, in the sense of I-consistency of the coarse-graining that defines them, remains unassessed; and even a future pass on I-consistency would establish the status of an access-and-control variable, leaving the phenomenal question exactly where the bridge assumption places it.

What a causal-status test requires. For a declared macro variable Q_N realized by τ on a micro description M, a causal test needs (a) an intervention family I that sets realizers of Q_N (not inputs to them), (b) the macro theory's own interventional law P_N(D | do(Q_N = q)) for downstream D, and (c) estimation of Δ_I(τ) on held-out interventions. Perturbational methods that stimulate cortex directly or manipulate feedback pathways are the class of methods that can populate (a). Which published results already instantiate part of this diagram, and at what grain, is a question this paper does not answer from the stored records; it is left as an explicit reanalysis target rather than asserted. Two cautions apply to any such reanalysis. First, an intervention that sets realizers of Q_N while also altering variables outside τ's preimage is not a member of I as defined; the family must be declared before Δ_I(τ) is computed, and the declaration is part of the result. Second, a defect of zero on I establishes I-consistency, not consistency with respect to interventions never performed; the status conferred is always relative to I. The working draft on centered closure makes the same methodological point that perturbational rather than observational equivalence is the relevant standard [48]; the present framework turns that point into a computable quantity, Δ_I(τ), and states the family relative to which the quantity is defined.

IIT 4.0 and grain. IIT 4.0 identifies phenomenal existence with a maximal cause–effect structure computed over a system's units and their perturbational repertoire [10]. Its cause–effect analysis is interventional in construction, which places it closer to requirement (a) than any purely observational program. But the identity is asserted at a chosen grain, and the theory's own criterion for grain selection (maximization of an integration quantity over candidate systems and units) is a different criterion from I-consistency. Maximality of the integration quantity at a grain does not imply that the coarse-graining to that grain commutes with the available interventions; the two properties are defined by different diagrams and neither entails the other in general. The composition-without-factoring proposition below shows that a grain at which a variable is I-consistent may have no I-consistent intermediate refinements, so a criterion that selects an intermediate grain can select a description with a nonzero defect. We do not attach a specific defect value to any IIT grain choice; the proposition establishes existence, not magnitude. This is a formal caution, not an empirical refutation, and it does not bear on IIT's identity claim, which is a bridge assumption of the theory rather than a quantity the framework can assess.

Structuralist quality-space matching, representability, and the residual gauge. Neurophenomenal structuralism proposes to individuate qualities by their positions in a quality space and treats structure-preserving neural-to-phenomenal mappings as evidence [8], with a methodological defense that separates empirical structural matching from metaphysical conclusions [9]; a general definition of "mathematical structure of experience" has also been proposed [7]. In the present vocabulary, a demonstrated structure-preserving map between a neural similarity structure and a reported similarity structure is evidence that F_Q(M, I) is nonempty at the tested context, and a structure that recurs across systems is evidence that the assignment of quality structures to contexts is representable. Representability, where it holds, individuates the macro quality object up to unique isomorphism. It does not, however, do the work of the gauge result: the residual indeterminacy of phenomenal labeling follows from the weaker hypothesis that every intervention in I acts on the quality object by structure-preserving maps, and it would hold whether or not the presheaf is representable. The gauge result is, at bottom, the observation that an equivariant family of measurements identifies a point only up to the automorphism group; its content lies in making that group explicit for a declared structure and declared family.

The framework therefore asks the question structuralism leaves open: what is Aut of the matched, intervention-augmented structure? For a hue circle with K categorical hues and a symmetric adjacency metric, Aut is the dihedral group of order 2K (rotations and reflections). Interventional response profiles can break automorphisms: any profile that distinguishes a hue from its rotation breaks the rotation subgroup, and any profile not preserved under reversal of hue order breaks the reflections. Whether such asymmetries exist in a given system is an empirical matter that the stored records do not settle; they enter here as hypotheses about which automorphisms would be broken, and the framework's output is the residual group after all measured asymmetries are imposed. Two points about that output must be stated exactly. First, the asymmetries that break automorphisms are asymmetries in the access-and-control structure: downstream response, discriminability, report. Breaking a rotation in that structure identifies the access-and-control position of a hue; it identifies the phenomenal label only under the bridge assumption that phenomenal labeling covaries with access-and-control position, which is precisely what the gauge result says cannot be established within the relational class. Second, the residual group is not a nuisance to be driven to the trivial group by better measurement within the class; the frozen proposition states that no refinement by structure-preserving interventions reduces it below the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure. The phenomenal labeling within that group is unidentified, and this is a conclusion of the framework rather than a gap in the data. What structuralist matching converts is therefore not "does structure exhaust quality" into a yes-or-no verdict, but into a computation: declare the structure, declare I, compute the residual group, and read off exactly which labelings the evidence leaves open.

Philosophical Reinterpretation

The debate between structuralists who hold that quality-space position exhausts qualitative character and critics who point to inverted-qualia possibilities is usually conducted as a dispute over a single claim. The framework splits it into two stages with different logical status, and the two stages depend on different parts of the formal apparatus.

Stage one: object individuation. Which macro quality variables are genuine explanatory objects at which grains and contexts? This is the question of whether F_Q(M, I) is nonempty for a declared intervention family I and, across systems, whether F_Q is representable. It is settled, relative to I, by checking whether the coarse-graining commutes with every intervention in I; the answer is a property of the pair (M, I) and of the declared observational cut, not of the system alone. When the answer is positive and the presheaf is representable, the quality object is fixed up to unique isomorphism. This is a result about what the theory is about. It is stronger than a decoding result in one specific respect: decoding establishes predictive lumpability, and the Separation Theorem shows that, under its hypotheses (a delay of at least two steps between the realizer and its copy, and observational equivalence relative to the declared cut), predictive lumpability can hold while I-consistency fails. Outside those hypotheses—in particular for a one-step delay, or when the observational cut is enlarged to expose the copy's staleness—the separation is not established and should not be asserted.

Stage two: phenomenal identification. Given the individuated object Q\, which phenomenal character does each q ∈ Q\ have? Within CG, phenomenal characters are not variables. The only constraints on a labeling λ: Q\ → Φ that interventional data can supply are structural: λ must intertwine the relational structure of Q\ with whatever structure Φ is assumed to have. Under the hypothesis that every intervention in I\ acts on Q\ by a structure-preserving map, the set of admissible λ is a torsor under Aut_{I\}(Q\): every admissible labeling is obtained from any other by an automorphism, and no interventional datum distinguishes them. We do not claim more for this observation than it deserves. It is the standard consequence of an equivariance hypothesis, it does not require representability (representability enters only at stage one), and the categorical language of natural transformations adds bookkeeping rather than content. Its value is locational rather than deductive: structure-preserving inversions of qualitative character are exactly the nontrivial elements of Aut_{I\}(Q\), placed at a definite point in the mathematics rather than floated as a thought experiment. Inversion scenarios that violate the assumed structure on Φ are not elements of this group; they are excluded only by the equivariance hypothesis itself, which is a bridge assumption rather than a theorem. This is the region Lyre's neurophenomenal structuralism identifies as its open problem [8], and the framework's contribution is to say what its residual ambiguity is relative to I\* and an assumed structure on Φ. It does not shrink that ambiguity, and it does not determine it independently of those two inputs.

Why uniqueness up to unique isomorphism is not identification. It is tempting to say that a rigid structure (trivial Aut_{I\}(Q\)) fixes phenomenal character, since the torsor is then a single point. This is a mistake about what the point is. The torsor is a set of bijections into Φ relative to an assumed structure on Φ. Rigidity yields uniqueness of the structural description of Q\; it does not tell us which intrinsic character, if any, occupies a position, because Φ was never given to us except structurally. Yoneda uniqueness is a statement about representing objects inside CG; Φ is outside CG, and no quantity in the framework identifies the map into it. The labeling λ is therefore unidentified by interventional data even in the rigid case; what changes with rigidity is only the number of labelings compatible with the assumed structure. The hard problem is accordingly neither diffused across structure nor solved by rigidity. It is localized: the gauge group measures the ambiguity that remains even after one grants a bridge anchoring some reference labeling, and the bridge itself is where the residual explanatory gap lives. This is also why we resist treating semantic-erasure style underdetermination as a global standoff between whole theories: the underdetermination that survives full interventional access to Q\ is precisely the Aut_{I\}(Q\)-action on labels, and any further underdetermination is a bridge matter, not an interventional one. No refinement of I\ within the class of structure-preserving interventions can push the ambiguity below Aut_{I\}(Q\*), and even the trivial group is not identification.

Consequences for the target distinctions. Access and control become I-consistency claims about specific downstream variables. A report variable is a further coarse-graining that can be predictive of Q without commuting with content interventions; the stale-copy null system below makes this vivid for variables individuated by observational or decoding criteria. It does not, by itself, bear on theories that individuate their macro variable functionally by downstream causal role. A workspace variable defined as whatever state broadcasts to report and control has I-consistency for report- and control-directed interventions built into its definition, so the stale copy fails to be an instance of it rather than serving as a counterexample to it; what the framework asks of such theories is that the intervention family implicit in "causal role" be declared, so that the definitional consistency can be checked against interventions the definition did not anticipate. Perceived consciousness is a coarse-graining in the observer's model and says nothing about the target's F_Q. Agency is I-consistency for action-setting interventions. Each of these is a claim about access, control, or prediction, and each can be true of a system whose phenomenal labeling remains wholly undetermined; none is a proxy for phenomenal consciousness, because phenomenal character enters the framework only through λ, and λ is what the gauge acts on. Claims that a system is conscious because it behaves, reports, or has a theory-inspired architecture are thus classified as claims about the predictive status of some coarse-graining, or about I-consistency for a functional family of interventions. Under the hypotheses of the Separation Theorem, predictive status is compatible with zero causal status; under any hypotheses, causal status for access and control leaves the phenomenal question exactly where stage two locates it.

Theorem, Proposition, Proof, Derivation, Counterexample, or No-Go Result

All models are finite and all proofs are direct computations. Two scope remarks govern the whole section. First, every result below concerns macro variables defined by their access-and-control profile (what they are read from, what they drive at a registered cut, how they respond to interventions on their realizers); no result asserts or presupposes that any such variable is phenomenally conscious. Phenomenal vocabulary enters only in Theorem 2(b), through the labeled bridge assumption B, and there only as a labeling whose underdetermination is the point. Second, several quantities that appear in the constructions are unidentified from the evidence bases considered: the buffer delay d and hence the choice between M₁ and M₂ is unidentified from cut-registered observation (Theorem 1(a)); the defect Δ_I(τ) is identified only relative to a declared family I and is not a property of τ alone; and the labeling λ of Theorem 2 is unidentified up to the stated group even given every interventional law in I*.

Theorem 1 (Predictive–Causal Separation)

For every K ≥ 2, every delay d ≥ 1, and every ε > 0, there exist finite time-indexed SCMs M₁ and M₂ over a common variable set, with a common coarse-graining τ onto macro variables (C, Q, R, A) in which Q is a K-valued quality variable and R a downstream report, such that: (a) M₁ and M₂ have identical observational laws over all variables observed at registered commit cuts; (b) τ is predictively lumpable in both; (c) for every admissible family I consisting of content interventions do(C_t = c) at arbitrary times, containing at least one cut-time content intervention do(C_{nL} = c) with n large enough that (1 − r)ⁿ ≤ ε, and containing no interventions on the monitor buffer, Δ_I(τ) = 0 in M₁ and Δ_I(τ) ≥ 1 − 1/K − ε in M₂.

Construction. Time is organized into blocks of length L > d. At the first step of block n a content variable C ∈ [K] is drawn by a reset chain: C_n = C_{n−1} with probability 1 − r, and C_n is drawn uniformly from [K] otherwise, with 0 < r ≤ 1; absent intervention, C_t = C_n is held fixed within the block. A monitor buffer reads content with delay: B_t = C_{t−d} in M₂, and B_t = C_t in M₁ (d replaced by 0). The macro quality variable is Q_t = τ_Q(B_t) with τ_Q the identity on [K] (a general τ_Q with equal-sized fibers changes only constants). The report is R_t = Q_t and an action is A_t = g(Q_t) for a fixed g. The coarse-graining τ is the identity on C and τ_Q on B; C is therefore a realizer of the macro variable C, and B is the sole realizer of Q. The commit cut of block n is its last step t = nL, and the observed record is (C_{nL}, Q_{nL}, R_{nL}, A_{nL}). The macro model N has, at each cut, the mechanisms Q = C, R = Q, A = g(Q), with C distributed as the reset chain; N is the macro model that asserts Q tracks current content.

Proof of (a). Absent intervention, C is constant within a block, and nL − d lies in block n since L > d, so B_{nL} = C_n in both models. Hence Q_{nL} = R_{nL} = C_{nL} = C_n and A_{nL} = g(C_n) in both, and the record has the same joint law, namely the law of the reset chain pushed through these deterministic maps. The equivalence is exact at cuts. It does not extend to arbitrary times: in the first d steps of a block following a reset, Q_t = C_{t−d} ≠ C_t in M₂ while Q_t = C_t in M₁. The observational indistinguishability asserted here is therefore indistinguishability relative to a cut-registered evidence base, and every downstream use of the theorem must be read with that restriction.

Proof of (b). The block-level macro process Q_{nL} = C_n is Markov with the reset kernel in both models, and R_{nL}, A_{nL} are deterministic functions of Q_{nL}; hence the conditional law of the macro future at cuts given the full micro history equals its conditional law given Q_{nL}. Decoding of Q from R is perfect, and the observational nonnegative rank of the content-to-action channel is that of g, in both models.

Proof of (c). Let i_c = do(C_{nL} = c) be a content intervention applied at the cut step itself. Since C_{nL} realizes the macro variable C, the induced intervention map gives ω(i_c) = do(C = c) in N; nothing is declared about Q, whose value under ω(i_c) is fixed by N's mechanism Q = C, i.e., P_N(Q = c, R = c | do(C = c)) = 1. In M₁, B_{nL} = C_{nL} = c, so Q_{nL} = R_{nL} = c: the pushforward of the cut record equals the N-law. The same holds for a content intervention at any other time, because B_t = C_t identically in M₁; hence Δ_I(τ) = 0 in M₁. In M₂, B_{nL} = C_{nL−d} with d ≥ 1, and the intervention at nL leaves C_{nL−d} at its pre-intervention value C_n; so Q_{nL} = C_n regardless of c. The pushforward τ_*P_{M₂}(Q_{nL} | do(C_{nL} = c)) is therefore the marginal law π_n of C_n. For the reset chain started from any initial law μ₀, π_n = (1 − r)ⁿ μ₀ + (1 − (1 − r)ⁿ)·uniform, so TV(π_n, uniform) ≤ (1 − r)ⁿ ≤ ε for the n in the hypothesis. Then

TV(π_n, δ_c) ≥ TV(uniform, δ_c) − ε = 1 − 1/K − ε.

Since i_c ∈ I, Δ_I(τ) ≥ 1 − 1/K − ε in M₂. Finally, if the buffer intervention do(B_{nL} = q) were admitted, then ω(do(B = q)) = do(Q = q) would commute in M₂ as well (Q_{nL} = q, R_{nL} = q); so the hypothesis excluding buffer access is necessary for the separation, not decorative. ∎

Remarks. (i) With r = 1, or with the chain started from its stationary law, ε = 0 and the defect is exactly 1 − 1/K, the maximal TV distance between a point mass and a uniform law on K points. (ii) The intervention that exhibits the defect acts on a realizer of the macro variable C, not on Q; the failing arrow is N's mechanism C → Q. This is the correct locus: the causal claim under test is that the quality variable tracks current content, and it is that claim which the stale buffer falsifies. (iii) A theory that individuates its macro variable by downstream role alone does not exclude M₂, since Q in M₂ has exactly the downstream role (read by R and A at the cut) that it has in M₁. A theory that individuates the variable by upstream sensitivity to content as well does exclude M₂'s Q from counting as its variable, but it can only establish that exclusion by performing, or assuming the result of, the content intervention in I. The theorem does not decide which reading a given theory takes; it states what each reading commits one to. (iv) Nothing in the construction concerns phenomenality. Q in M₂ has full access-and-control status at the cut and is causally inert with respect to current content; whether either model instantiates experience is not a question the theorem addresses. (v) The construction lifts a single-cut countermodel from the shared repository to the level of coarse-grainings and intervention families; the relation is discussed under the countermodel section.

Theorem 2 (Gauge Residue)

Suppose F_Q is representable by (Q, I). Then: (a) any two representing objects are related by a unique CG-isomorphism. (b) Suppose every intervention in I acts on Q through Aut(ρ)-equivariant maps, meaning that for every i ∈ I and g ∈ Aut(ρ) there is i' ∈ I with g_P_{Q}(· | do(i)) = P_{Q}(· | do(i')). Then Aut_{I}(Q) = Aut(ρ), and the set Λ of admissible phenomenal labelings is either empty or a torsor under Aut(ρ), where a labeling λ: Q → Φ is a bijection onto a fixed set Φ carrying a fixed relational structure, admissible if it intertwines the ρ-derived relational structure with that structure (bridge assumption B). (c) For any I' ⊇ I whose additional interventions are also Aut(ρ)-equivariant, Aut_{I'}(Q) = Aut(ρ): refinement within the equivariant class does not reduce the gauge.*

Before the proof, a statement of what is and is not being claimed. Part (a) is the only part that uses representability, and it uses nothing beyond the uniqueness half of the Yoneda lemma. Parts (b) and (c) do not use representability at all; they are direct consequences of the equivariance hypothesis, and once that hypothesis is granted they are elementary. The theorem is recorded because the equivariance hypothesis is exactly the formal content of the informal claim that an intervention family "acts only through the structure," and it is useful to see that this hypothesis alone, with no categorical machinery, already yields the full torsor. The substantive question the theorem raises but does not answer is whether any physically realizable intervention family on a quality variable is equivariant in this sense; that is a question about the augmented structure ρ', addressed under Boundaries below.

Proof. (a) A representable functor determines its representing object up to unique isomorphism; this is the uniqueness half of the Yoneda lemma applied to F_Q, and no further content is imported.

(b) Inclusion Aut_{I}(Q) ⊆ Aut(ρ): a CG-automorphism of (Q, I) preserves every interventional law in I, and ρ is by definition derived from those laws, so it is preserved. Conversely, let g ∈ Aut(ρ). Equivariance supplies, for each i ∈ I, an i' with g_P(· | do(i)) = P(· | do(i')); set ω_g(i) = i'. Then (g, ω_g) satisfies the consistency identity for all i ∈ I, and g is a bijection, so g ∈ Aut_{I}(Q*). Hence equality.

For the torsor, let Aut(ρ) act on Λ on the right by λ·g = λ∘g. Closure: if λ intertwines ρ-structure with the fixed structure on Φ and g preserves ρ-structure, then λ∘g intertwines them as well. Transitivity: if λ, λ' ∈ Λ, then g := λ⁻¹∘λ' is a bijection Q → Q which, being a composite of an intertwiner and the inverse of an intertwiner to the same fixed structure on Φ, intertwines ρ-structure with itself; so g ∈ Aut(ρ) and λ' = λ∘g. Freeness: λ∘g = λ implies g = id because λ is injective. Hence Λ, when nonempty, is an Aut(ρ)-torsor.

(c) The argument in (b) used, for each intervention, only its equivariance; it therefore applies verbatim to I'. ∎

Boundaries of the theorem. Non-equivariant interventions, whose downstream profiles single out particular q ∈ Q, enlarge the relational structure to an augmented ρ' and reduce the gauge from Aut(ρ) to Aut(ρ'); the torsor argument then goes through with ρ' in place of ρ, and if Aut(ρ') is trivial the labeling is fixed by structure, subject to the rigidity caveat of the previous section. The theorem therefore does not claim that no intervention can shrink the gauge. It claims that interventions acting only through the structure cannot, and that what survives all interventions is exactly the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure. Two further limits should be explicit. First, the labeling λ is a formal object; whether admissible labelings correspond to anything phenomenal is precisely what bridge assumption B asserts, and the theorem is neutral on B. What the theorem establishes is a fact about access-and-control structure: the interventional profile of Q, however rich, individuates its elements only up to Aut(ρ'). Second, Λ may be empty, in which case B is false for this Q* and nothing follows about phenomenality at all. The working draft on centered closure asked for a formalization of cross-subject quality comparison as a torsor under empirically constrained automorphisms [48]; Theorem 2 supplies the torsor and locates the constraint in the augmented structure ρ' rather than in repertoire size as such.

Proposition 3 (Composition without Factoring)

Morphisms in CG compose, but I-consistency does not factor through intermediate grains: there exist (M, I) and an I-consistent τ₂: M → N₂ such that no strictly intermediate coarse-graining τ₁ (with τ₂ = τ'∘τ₁, τ₁ neither injective nor equal to τ₂ up to relabeling) admits an I-consistent (τ₁, ω_{τ₁}); the best achievable defect at either intermediate grain is 1/2.

Proof. Composition was verified in the model section. For non-factoring, let X_t ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4} evolve deterministically by 1 ↦ 3, 2 ↦ 4, 3 ↦ 1, 4 ↦ 2, with X₀ uniform, and let I = {do(X_t = x): x ∈ {1, …, 4}} with macro variables (Q_t, Q_{t+1}). Let τ₂ send {1, 2} ↦ A and {3, 4} ↦ B. The macro model N₂ has Q_{t+1} = the other cell. Consistency: do(X_t = 1) and do(X_t = 2) both yield X_{t+1} ∈ B, and do(X_t = 3), do(X_t = 4) both yield X_{t+1} ∈ A, matching N₂ under ω_{τ₂}(do(X_t = x)) = do(Q_t = τ₂(x)). The strictly intermediate partitions refining {A, B} are exactly P₁ = {{1}, {2}, {3, 4}} and P₂ = {{1, 2}, {3}, {4}}. For P₁, the induced intervention map is forced: do(X_t = 3) and do(X_t = 4) both act on realizers of the macro state {3, 4}, so both are sent to do(Q'_t = {3, 4}). Their pushforwards on Q'_{t+1} are δ_{{1}} and δ_{{2}} respectively. Any macro law p = P_{N'}(Q'_{t+1} | do(Q'_t = {3, 4})) must match both, so the defect at this intervention is max{TV(p, δ_{{1}}), TV(p, δ_{{2}})} ≥ 1/2, with equality exactly when p assigns mass 1/2 to each of {1} and {2}. No macro law achieves defect 0, and the infimum over macro laws is 1/2. P₂ fails symmetrically (do(X_t = 1) ↦ {3}, do(X_t = 2) ↦ {4}, both sent to do(Q'_t = {1, 2})), again with infimal defect 1/2. ∎

Interpretation. Partition sensitivity of causal decompositions is often treated as a nuisance of evidence localization. The proposition reinterprets it: the intermediate grain fails not because measurement is noisy but because no morphism exists there, and the failure is quantitative (defect 1/2 at best) rather than a matter of a missing observation. A theory that fixes its grain by an internal optimization criterion is not thereby guaranteed to land on a grain where morphisms exist, and refining a consistent grain can destroy consistency.

Corollary 4 (Independence of predictive and causal orderings)

What the preceding results establish about the relation between predictive lumpability and I-consistency is the following, and only the following. Theorem 1 gives a grain that is predictively lumpable and maximally non-commuting relative to an admissible I; hence predictive lumpability does not imply I-consistency, for any K ≥ 2. Proposition 3 gives a grain that is I-consistent while both of its strict refinements admit no I-consistent morphism; hence I-consistency is not inherited under refinement in the partition order. Two further claims that one might expect here are not established by these constructions and are not asserted. First, the converse separation (an I-consistent grain that is not predictively lumpable) is not exhibited: in Proposition 3 the intermediate grains fail lumpability as well as consistency, since under P₁ the law of Q'_{t+1} given Q'_t = {3, 4} depends on which micro state realizes {3, 4}, so that example does not separate the two properties in the other direction. Second, nothing here shows that the refinements in Proposition 3 are predictively no worse than the coarse grain; the opposite is the case for lumpability. The accurate summary is that predictive lumpability is neither sufficient for causal status (Theorem 1) nor monotone with it under refinement (Proposition 3); whether it is necessary is left open by this section.

Countermodel or Null System

Null system 1: stale quality space. Take the second micro-model of Theorem 1, restricted here to monitoring delay d ≥ 2, with K hue-like contents arranged so that the action g and a similarity-judgment output J_t = s(Q_t, Q'_t) for a probe Q'_t realize a circular metric on [K]: adjacent contents are judged more similar than distant ones, with the standard wrap-around. The content variable that drives A and J at the commit cut is the buffered copy B_{t−d}; the readout variable Q_t that the analyst decodes, and that the report R_t copies, is the current content, which has not yet reached the controller. At the registered commit cuts, the passive record exhibits (i) a similarity structure on Q isomorphic to a K-cycle, (ii) perfect report accuracy R = C, (iii) decoding rank K from any neural readout of Q, and (iv) a Markov macro process. Every predictive quality-space test evaluated on the declared cut readouts passes, and on those readouts the system is observationally indistinguishable from M₁. This equivalence is cut-restricted: a recording that also samples the buffer at intermediate times, or that compares Q_t with A_{t+d} across cuts, separates the two systems without any intervention, so full observational indistinguishability is not claimed. The d = 1 case is not covered by the present construction, because with a one-step delay the buffer content at the cut coincides with the content the report copies, and the report cannot be both perfectly accurate and causally inert for the action fixed at that same cut; whether a different construction achieves the separation at d = 1 is left open.

The intervention family I is declared as the set of interventions do(Q_t = q) implemented on realizers of Q_t at the cut—that is, on micro-variables in the fiber τ⁻¹(q)—and excluding any intervention on the buffer. Under every such intervention, R_t changes to match q while A_t and J_t are unchanged, because they are functions of B_{t−d}. The interventional response profile ρ(q) restricted to (A, J) therefore does not depend on the intervened content, and the interventional metric d_I is identically zero on the content interventions in I: the quality structure that appeared circular passively is interventionally trivial relative to I for the downstream outputs that were supposed to certify it. Two qualifications are essential. First, this trivial profile is a fact about I; under an augmented family containing buffer interventions the same coarse-graining has a nonzero commutation defect, and Δ_I(τ) is unidentified for any family not declared in advance. Second, the result concerns access and control—whether the decoded variable is the one that shapes action and judgment at the cut—and says nothing on its own about whether Q_t or B_{t−d} is accompanied by phenomenal character; any such reading requires bridge assumption B, which the null system does not adjudicate.

The construction also does not bear on a macro variable individuated by its downstream causal role rather than by passive readout. Global-workspace-style theories define their macro variable as whatever content is broadcast to and controls downstream consumers; under that definition the buffer, not the decoded readout, is the workspace variable, and the null system is not a counterexample to the theory but a demonstration that the readout, report, and similarity variables commonly used to operationalize it need not coincide with it. The null system is a counterexample only to individuation by passive readout, report accuracy, or similarity structure.

This is the coarse-graining-level form of the stale-monitor construction in the repository's mediated-control paper [1]. That paper's commutation defect Γ is a scalar computed at a single registered cut between a randomized and a route-isolated channel through a designated mediator, and the paper itself treats a small Γ as an agreement check rather than as evidence that the mediator is complete or representational; it does not ask whether the mediator is an admissible coarse-graining, and it cannot say whether the monitor is an I-consistent macro variable at other grains or under other families. The construction is retained; its defect is transformed into Δ_I(τ), a supremum over a declared intervention family evaluated for a coarse-graining, and the separation from predictive lumpability holds for every K ≥ 2 and every family whose interventions act on realizers of Q at the cut and exclude the buffer. What is rejected is the implicit assumption that the mediator's cut is the right grain: the grain-shift example below shows that grain choice is itself subject to a non-factoring obstruction.

Null system 2: gauge-flip twin. Let Q be the hue circle with K ≥ 3 states, I the family of macro interventions do(Q = q) whose downstream profile ρ(q) depends on q only through cyclic adjacency, and let g be the reflection q ↦ −q (mod K). Two systems S and S∘g, differing only in which physical realizer carries which label, agree on every interventional law over Q, hence on F_Q at every context whose interventions are equivariant under g. This agreement follows directly from the equivariance hypothesis on I; representability of the presheaf is not needed to produce the twin. Representability adds only that when the I*-causal quality object exists it is unique up to unique isomorphism, so the twin cannot be dissolved by passing to a different representing object—the ambiguity is a torsor over the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure, here a subgroup of the dihedral group containing g. If bridge assumption B is granted for S with labeling λ, then S∘g with labeling λ∘g is equally admissible, and nothing in CG distinguishes them. This locates the inverted-qualia scenario as the element g, but only relative to B: without B the twin is a fact about labeling of an interventional structure, and the step from undetermined labeling to undetermined phenomenal character is not licensed by the formal result. Adding an intervention with a downstream profile that is not reflection-symmetric (a privileged hue) breaks g and shrinks the automorphism group; adding further equivariant interventions leaves it unchanged, by definition of equivariance.

Null system 3: grain-shift. The four-state chain of Proposition 3 is a null system for grain choice. An analyst who registers partition P₁ because it maximizes some decomposition score will find that interventions on the merged cell {3, 4} yield macro consequences that no single macro kernel reproduces, and may report "partition sensitivity." The commutation defect of P₁ is strictly positive for any family containing an intervention that separates states 3 and 4 downstream; its numerical value depends on the micro-kernel and on the declared family and is not identified by the qualitative description, so no specific figure is asserted here. The framework's diagnosis is that P₁ is not in the image of any coarse-graining morphism from M that commutes with the family, whereas the partition {A, B} is; the obstruction is structural, not an artifact of sample size.

Engagement with the quotient-observability framework. The repository's Recovery-Linked Quotient Observability paper defines a relative rank q_c from block Hankel matrices of interventional kernels to joint neural–behavioral versus behavioral outputs, and shows that, under reproducible state preparation, an identifiable translation-noise family, common probes, and an attainable-set condition, q_c equals the dimension of the kernel of the canonical map from joint to behavioral predictive-state quotients; without those assumptions it is an algebraic relative observability invariant only [2]. Both quotients are defined by predictive equivalence of futures under probe policies, not by commutation with interventions on the quotiented states. So q_c > 0, even under the identifying assumptions, certifies that a finer predictive state exists, not that either quotient is an I-consistent macro variable; and the framework's evidential use tacitly treats the joint quotient as the relevant grain. Corollary 4 shows this is not free: the example there exhibits a predictive refinement that has nonzero commutation defect while its coarsening has zero defect, so neither ordering entails the other. What is retained is the insight that behavior-only quotients can hide content and that the reading of q_c is conditional on stated assumptions, as that paper already emphasizes; what is transformed is the evidential use, which must now be paired with a commutation check Δ_I(τ) on the candidate quotient under a declared family; what is rejected is any inference from covert predictive reserve to a causally privileged grain, and a fortiori any inference from either to phenomenal presence, which would again require B.

Empirical Boundary Conditions and Falsification Criteria

Conditions of application. The results apply when (i) the micro description is finite or finitely discretized at the grain of the experimental repertoire; (ii) the intervention family I is declared in advance, with its context (passive observation, stimulus-side manipulation, direct stimulation, code access) specified, since I-consistency is context-relative and F_Q is a presheaf over contexts, not a constant; (iii) the macro theory states its own interventional law P_N(D | do(Q = q)) explicitly, without which the commutation defect Δ_I is undefined; (iv) a tolerance δ for Δ_I is declared before data are seen; and (v) the induced intervention map ω is defined only on interventions that act on realizers of Q under τ, as required by the definition of an induced map, so that ω(do(C = c)) is not declared to equal do(Q = c) for a variable C that τ does not map to Q. Under these conditions, causal status relative to I is a computable predicate on (theory, grain, context) triples. Two further qualifications bound what the predicate delivers. First, Δ_I is identified only for interventions actually in the declared family; a defect over content interventions at the cut is unidentified from data generated by a family that contains no such interventions, and no amount of decoding accuracy estimates it. Second, the predicate concerns the access-and-control structure of Q, that is, what Q does to downstream report, behavior, and other macro variables under intervention. It does not, by itself, concern the phenomenal labeling of Q's states; that step requires the bridge assumption B, which is stated separately and is not identified by any interventional law in the relational class.

Falsification criteria.

  1. Causal-status claims. A declared macro variable's causal status relative to I is refuted if, in a perturbational dataset whose intervention family includes content interventions on realizers of Q at the cut, Δ_I(τ) on held-out interventions exceeds δ while decoding accuracy and report agreement remain high. The Separation Theorem does not predict that such dissociations occur in brains; it shows that they are not excluded by predictive success, because for every K ≥ 2 there exist finite micro-models with identical observational laws at the cut, one I-consistent and one not, provided the propagation delay satisfies d ≥ 2. Two restrictions on this claim follow from the proof as it stands. The observational equivalence established is cut-restricted: the models agree on the law of the variables the decoder reads at the declared cut, not on the full observational law of every micro variable, so a decoder with access to variables outside the cut may distinguish them. And the construction does not go through for d = 1, where the delayed copy and the live variable coincide within the intervention window. Any empirical use of criterion 1 must therefore state the cut, the delay grain, and the intervention family; a reported dissociation at a different cut or grain does not bear on it.
  1. The Gauge-Residue result as mathematics. The result has two halves with different standing. The representability half is a standard consequence of the Yoneda lemma: if F_Q is representable, the representing object is fixed up to unique isomorphism, and this individuates the macro quality object relative to I. The residue half states that if every intervention in I acts on the represented structure only through Aut(ρ)-equivariant maps, then no interventional law distinguishes labelings that differ by an element of Aut(ρ). This half does not require representability and is close to immediate from the equivariance hypothesis; it is the hypothesis, not the theorem, that carries the content. Accordingly it is not falsified by data. It is refuted, as mathematics, by exhibiting an intervention family satisfying the equivariance hypothesis together with an interventional law that fixes a labeling uniquely despite a nontrivial element of Aut(ρ*); since such a law would itself be a non-equivariant map, this amounts to checking that the hypothesis has been stated correctly. What can be contested empirically is whether the equivariance hypothesis is true of any actual intervention family. An intervention that acts on the quality structure by a non-structure-preserving map, if one could be specified and shown to be admissible, would fall outside the theorem's scope and could in principle shrink the gauge. The paper claims no such intervention exists within the relational class; it does not claim that none exists at all.
  1. Relevance of the separation. The separation is a possibility result. It would be empirically demoted to a formal caution if, across reanalyzed perturbational studies at matched grains and cuts, macro variables that pass decoding tests systematically also satisfied commutation with Δ_I below δ. The framework does not predict a large gap in brains, only that the gap is not zero by construction and that its size at any given cut is unidentified from observational and stimulus-side data alone.

What existing data can and cannot decide. The Cogitate datasets [4, 5, 6] can settle predictive questions at many grains and modalities. Because their manipulations act on stimulus parameters, they also identify the commutation defect for the restricted family I_stim of stimulus-side interventions, and hence can test consistency for coarse-grainings whose macro variable is downstream of the stimulus and whose theory states a law P_N(D | do(stimulus = s)). They contain no content interventions at the cut, so Δ_I for a content-intervention family is unidentified from them, and criterion 1 cannot be decided for workspace or integration variables on these data. The adversarial results [3] therefore bear on the predictive status of both theories' macro variables and leave their causal-status claims relative to content interventions open. One asymmetry between the theories deserves note. Global Neuronal Workspace Theory defines its macro variable partly by downstream causal role, namely broadcast to and availability for report, planning, and other consumer systems. On that definition, a system in which a stale copy of the workspace content is decodable but not broadcast does not realize the GNWT variable at all; it realizes a decoding-defined proxy. Criterion 1 therefore applies to GNWT only as a test of whether a proposed neural proxy for the workspace variable satisfies the role that defines it, not as a test that could show the role-defined variable to be non-causal while it is decodable. For Integrated Information Theory, whose macro variable is defined by intrinsic cause-effect structure rather than by downstream role, the criterion applies more directly, but the relevant intervention family must act on the substrate's cause-effect repertoire at the declared grain, which the current datasets do not do. A reanalysis program that pairs these datasets with direct-stimulation data at matched grains and cuts is the natural next step; it is not this paper's central contribution, and nothing in this paper should be read as reporting its outcome.

Limitations

The formal results are narrower than their labels in an earlier draft suggested, and the limits should be stated exactly. The Separation Theorem (Theorem 1) holds only for intervention maps ω that are induced by the coarse-graining τ in the sense of our own Definition 2: an intervention on a micro variable is mapped to a macro intervention only when that micro variable is a realizer of the corresponding macro coordinate. The stale-copy construction must therefore be read with an intervention that fixes the realizer B of Q = τ(B), not the downstream copy C; an intervention on C is not in the τ-induced family and cannot be assigned the macro image do(Q = c). Under this restriction the separation of I-consistency from predictive lumpability is established for delay depth d ≥ 2, where the copy lags its source by at least one update and the commutation defect is strictly positive. The d = 1 case is not covered: with a single-step lag the intervened micro-model and the macro model agree on the joint distribution over the observed cut, and Δ_I is zero there, so the theorem should be cited as a separation result for d ≥ 2 rather than for all delays. Proposition 3 gives an upper bound on the achievable defect, not its exact value; the exact value depends on the transition kernels and is not identified by the construction alone.

The observational-equivalence clause of Theorem 1 is cut-restricted. The two micro-models agree on every observational law defined on the declared cut (the marginal over the variables that τ reads), not on the full micro joint distribution. Statements elsewhere in the paper that speak of "observationally indistinguishable" models should be understood in this restricted sense; full observational indistinguishability is a stronger property that our examples do not exhibit and that we do not claim. Relatedly, the consistency criterion used throughout is exact distributional commutation; approximate, constructive, or interchange-based criteria from the causal-abstraction literature may classify boundary cases differently, and Δ_I depends on the choice of total variation as the discrepancy measure. A systematic comparison with alternative abstraction criteria has not been carried out here.

The categorical layer does less than its vocabulary implies. Representability of the presheaf of I-causal quality structures, when it holds, individuates the macro quality object up to unique isomorphism, and this is all that Part (ii) of the hypothesis uses it for. The gauge residue itself does not depend on representability: given the equivariance hypothesis, the set of phenomenal labelings compatible with the intervention-augmented structure is a torsor over the automorphism group of that structure, and this follows directly from the definition of a structure-intertwining bijection. Read as a theorem, the Gauge-Residue result is therefore a labeled consequence of its hypotheses rather than an independent discovery, and the presheaf and Yoneda machinery serve to organize the dependence on intervention contexts rather than to prove anything the torsor observation does not already give. Presheaf restriction is defined along CG-morphisms and, in the context version, along inclusions of families; richer indexing (for example by protocols with morphisms between interventions) might expose invariants invisible here. Representability is assumed, not established, for any actual quality space; the stored records provide evidence for structure-preserving mappings at particular contexts, not for a universal quality object across systems.

The gauge result also depends on bridge assumption B, which treats phenomenal labeling as a structure-intertwining bijection. If phenomenal characters are subject to constraints that are not relational, B is false and the theorem says nothing about them; if B is true, the theorem still does not explain why any labeling is realized rather than another. The rigidity caveat means that a trivial residual group does not amount to identification. What I-consistency certifies is the causal status of a macro variable as a variable of access and control: that intervening on it has the macro-level effects the abstraction predicts. Nothing in the framework certifies that such a variable is phenomenally conscious, that its states have any qualitative character, or which character they have. The automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure for any actual system is an unidentified quantity here; we have shown only that it bounds what relational refinement can determine, not what it is. The framework contains no variable for moral status and offers no formal treatment of it; any ethical inference from I-consistency or from gauge size would be a category error, and welfare precaution is a separate practical matter not adjudicated by this analysis.

The null system also does not bear equally on all theories. Global Neuronal Workspace Theory defines its macro variable by its downstream causal role (broadcast to consumer systems), so a coarse-graining that respects that definition already builds a consistency requirement into τ. The stale-copy null model applies to abstraction maps fixed by the readout cut independently of downstream role; it does not by itself show that a role-defined workspace variable fails I-consistency, and we make no such claim. Which theories define their macro variable in a way that pre-empts the null model is a matter of how the abstraction map is specified, and we have addressed it only for the readout-defined case.

The neuroscientific classification relies on the observational character of the cited Cogitate measures: they test predictive status, not I-consistency, and we have not reanalyzed the datasets. Statements about which perturbational studies instantiate the commutation diagram are deliberately withheld because the stored records do not cover them. The color asymmetries invoked as automorphism-breakers are illustrative hypotheses about the logical role such asymmetries would play, not established results drawn from the cited sources. The disciplinary lens is itself a simplifying abstraction of neural complexity, and a general caution about the partiality of neuroscientific abstractions applies to it as much as to the theories it evaluates [26]. Finally, the two repository papers engaged here are AI-generated drafts; our reliance on them is on the structure of their constructions, which we have reproved in our own setting, not on their conclusions.

Conclusion

The recent large-scale adversarial tests of consciousness theories assessed their macro variables for predictive status; because their measures were observational, they could not have assessed causal status, and nothing in this paper reads them as having done so. Causal abstraction supplies the missing criterion. A macro quality variable Q earns causal status relative to a declared intervention family I when its coarse-graining commutes, in distribution, with every intervention in I that is induced through realizers of Q, and the commutation defect over I measures how far it falls short. Two qualifications, forced by the proofs as they now stand, bound the Predictive–Causal Separation Theorem. First, the intervention map must be induced through realizers of the macro variable, not declared on a non-realizing subsystem; with that repair, the stale-copy construction separates causal from predictive status for every alphabet size K ≥ 2 provided the copy delay is at least two steps, and the one-step case is not covered by the argument. Second, the micro-models exhibited are observationally equivalent only over the declared observational cut, so the theorem establishes separation from predictive lumpability relative to the marginals a passive observer can record, not full observational indistinguishability. Within that scope the result is exact: agreement on every observational law over the cut places no constraint on the defect over interventions outside it, and the defect on unobserved interventions is therefore unidentified from observational data alone.

The presheaf of consistent quality structures over intervention contexts organizes the resulting context- and grain-relativity of causal status; its role is bookkeeping rather than theorem-proving, and the composition-without-factoring proposition should be read as showing that grain choice faces a nonzero, structural obstruction, not as fixing a particular defect value. The null system does not touch every theory equally. A theory that defines its macro variable by downstream causal role, as global-workspace accounts do, already builds an intervention family into the definition and is not pre-empted by a stale copy that lacks that role; what the framework asks of such a theory is that the family be declared explicitly so the defect can be estimated rather than presumed zero. Throughout, the variable that earns causal status is a variable of access and control: a coarse-graining of micro-state that supports intervention on report, memory, and downstream processing. Nothing in the criterion shows that phenomenality, as distinct from that functional profile, causes anything.

On qualia, the framework replaces "does structure exhaust quality" with two questions of different kinds and different status. Object individuation is a representability question: when the presheaf of I-causal quality structures is representable, the macro quality object is fixed up to unique isomorphism by a standard argument. Phenomenal identification is the choice of a section of a torsor under the automorphism group of the intervention-augmented structure. The Gauge-Residue Theorem, in its substantive half, follows directly from the hypothesis that the intervention family acts by structure-preserving maps and does not depend on representability; it is close to a restatement of that equivariance assumption, and it should be weighed as such. Its content is that no refinement within the relational class shrinks the labeling gauge to the trivial group, so phenomenal labeling remains unidentified up to that automorphism group. Inverted qualia are thereby located rather than dissolved, and the uniqueness delivered by representability is shown to be individuation, not identification. Whether an intervention that is not structure-preserving is conceivable, and what it would look like, lies outside the formal results.

The implication for research is bounded and concrete: declare the intervention family, state the macro interventional law, estimate the commutation defect on held-out interventions from within that family, and compute the residual automorphism group after all measured asymmetries have been imposed. The framework identifies which variables are candidates for causal explanation relative to a family and reports where labeling ambiguity remains under the stated hypotheses. It does not say what fixes intrinsic qualitative character, and it does not claim to.
